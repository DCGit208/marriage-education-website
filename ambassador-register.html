Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I start earning before completing the Foundation School?

Yes! You can begin advocacy work (Stack 1: Access Deployment) immediately after registration. The Foundation School enhances your skills and unlocks advanced pathways, but you're not blocked from earning while you learn.

Q: What if I can't complete the 6-week school on time?

The curriculum is self-paced. Six weeks is recommended, but you can take longer if needed. The CRLA credential is awarded upon completion, not on a fixed timeline.

Q: Is the $247 Teleios Blueprint scholarship really included?

Yes, for the first 500 ambassadors. It's a launch incentive—full access to our flagship course at no additional cost. After the first 500, ambassadors will receive the Foundation School training separately.

Q: Do I need to become a counselor to be successful?

No. Many ambassadors build excellent careers through advocacy work, regional leadership, or corporate deployment without ever becoming counselors. The counseling track is one option, not the only option.

Q: How are training credits calculated and used?

Training credits are earned through sustained advocacy work (deployments, activations, service routing). They can be applied toward counseling certification tuition, advanced courses, or faculty mentorship programs—significantly reducing your out-of-pocket costs for professional development.

Q: Can organizations purchase bulk access for their members?

Absolutely. Churches, companies, schools, and NGOs can purchase access at 15,000 FCFA per member. Example: A church purchases access for 100 couples (200 people) = 3,000,000 FCFA → you earn 300,000 FCFA.

Q: What's the difference between this and "selling books" or affiliate marketing?

Three critical differences:

  1. Infrastructure: You're deploying access to a $247 scholarship program powered by a credentialed curriculum, not just selling a book.
  2. Credentialing: You earn a recognized credential (CRLA) and build toward professional certification.
  3. Career Pathway: This is a 5-year professional development arc from advocacy to counseling, not a side hustle.
