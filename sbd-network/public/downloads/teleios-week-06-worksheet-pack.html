1. Teleios Marriage Readiness Scan

Rate each statement based on consistent, observable behavior—not the answer you wish were true. Complete every item before interpreting the totals.

Overall score: 0 / 250 — Complete the scan to see your reflection range.

Use a score as a prompt for reflection, not as a pass/fail decision about marriage. Review low-scoring domains, discuss consequential decisions with trusted, qualified support, and consider the actual patterns and responsibilities in your life.
