Lesson Notes

Key Concepts

  • → Contract Mindset: "What do I get out of this?" Transactional, conditional, self-protecting.
  • → Covenant Mindset: "Who am I becoming?" Transformational, unconditional, self-giving.
  • → The Identity Shift: Moving from negotiation partner to covenant counterpart.
  • → Practical Application: Learning to ask "What's faithful?" instead of "What's fair?"

Reflection Questions

  1. When conflict arises, do you naturally think "What's fair?" or "What's faithful?"
  2. What would change in your marriage if you viewed differences as complementary design rather than incompatibility?
  3. How have you been operating from a contractual mindset without realizing it?
