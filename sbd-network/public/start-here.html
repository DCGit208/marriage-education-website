What is SBD?

SBD (Seals By Design) is a marriage education system that teaches couples how to build covenant marriages — relationships designed for permanence, intimacy, and purpose.

We don't offer "communication tips" or "date night ideas." We offer formation — a systematic education that transforms how you think about marriage, sex, identity, and spiritual intimacy.

Why Most Marriage Advice Fails

Most marriage advice focuses on symptoms, not systems. You're told to "communicate better" without understanding why communication breaks down. You're told to "prioritize intimacy" without understanding the design of sexual connection.

"The problem isn't that couples don't try. The problem is that they're working with incomplete information."

SBD gives you the blueprint. We teach you the underlying principles that make marriages thrive — principles rooted in theology, psychology, and the lived experience of covenant.

The Teleios Identity

The foundation of SBD is the Teleios Identity — a framework for understanding who you're designed to become in covenant marriage.

Most people enter marriage with a contractual mindset: "What do I get out of this?" The Teleios Identity flips that equation. It asks: "Who am I becoming?"
