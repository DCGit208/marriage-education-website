## Usage rules

1. Preserve the supplied artwork; do not recreate or replace it with generated substitutes.
2. Course cards use the approved artwork as the primary visual rather than generic gradient posters.
3. The Series A collection artwork is used for discovery/navigation and is not treated as a pricing source.
4. Product prices, duration, access, and checkout behavior continue to come from the canonical catalog.
5. For Anthropocentric Development, product copy follows the design-reference philosophy: capacities develop in reference to human design; it does not imply that a person becomes whole by development.
6. The Science of Self-Command course card uses the approved course/book artwork with the current catalog's **5 weeks / $199** metadata; the separate coaching creative is retained for later coaching-specific presentation.
