Part 1: True/False

10 questions | 2 points each | 20 points total

Question 1
Epistemology is the discipline of knowing—asking "How do I know what I know?"
✅ Correct Answer: True
Epistemology is the branch of philosophy studying knowledge, belief, justification, and truth. For teleios persons, it's the discipline of verifying claims before accepting them—distinguishing Scripture from culture, principle from preference.
Question 2
Axiological maturity means prioritizing personal happiness above all other values because authentic living requires self-fulfillment.
✅ Correct Answer: False
Axiology is ordering goods by TRUTH, not trend. Matthew 22:37-39 establishes hierarchy: God first, neighbor second, self rightly loved. Covenant over comfort. Faithfulness over feelings. Self as highest value is axiological immaturity.
Question 3
Ontology is the discipline of being—understanding reality as designed, not as desired.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Ontology studies the nature of being and existence. For covenant, this means recognizing marriage as ONTOLOGICAL REALITY (Genesis 1:27, Matthew 19:4-6)—divinely designed, not culturally constructed. One-flesh union is being-level, not optional.
Question 4
Etymology is the stewardship of language—honoring the weight and meaning of words, not redefining them for convenience.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Etymology studies word origins and meaning. Proverbs 18:21: "Death and life are in the power of the tongue." Teleios persons honor language precision—keeping promises, resisting redefinition. Words name realities; ambiguity breeds deception.
Question 5
Hebrews 5:14 teaches that maturity comes automatically with age, not through disciplined training of the senses.
✅ Correct Answer: False
Hebrews 5:14: "Strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age (teleios), even those who BY REASON OF USE have their senses exercised to discern." Maturity requires TRAINING—disciplined practice, not passive aging.
Question 6
Epistemological immaturity shows up when someone believes "I feel unloved, therefore I am unloved"—conflating perception with reality.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Epistemological immaturity = treating feelings as facts. The immature person says "I feel it, so it's true." The teleios person distinguishes impression from intent, tests perception against truth, and verifies before deciding.
Question 7
According to Scripture, values are subjective—"your truth is your truth; my truth is my truth."
✅ Correct Answer: False
John 17:17: "Thy word is truth." Truth is OBJECTIVE—grounded in Scripture, not sentiment. Culture says values are subjective; Scripture establishes fixed order. Teleios persons resist relativism and order goods by God's hierarchy.
Question 8
Marriage is an ontological reality (being-level union) according to Matthew 19:6: "What God hath joined together, let not man put asunder."
✅ Correct Answer: True
Matthew 19:4-6 establishes marriage as ONTOLOGICAL UNION—one-flesh reality that cannot be undone by paperwork or preference. Not contract (terminable), but covenant (permanent). Ephesians 5:31-32 calls it "great mystery."
Question 9
James 3:2 states that the teleios (perfect/mature) person is able to "bridle the whole body" through mastery of language.
✅ Correct Answer: True
James 3:2: "If any man offend not in word, the same is a perfect (teleios) man, and able also to bridle the whole body." Etymology mastery = covenant capacity. Words create or destroy. Precision honors truth.
Question 10
The Four Axiomatic Dimensions (Epistemology, Axiology, Ontology, Etymology) are abstract academic categories with no practical application to covenant relationships.
✅ Correct Answer: False
The Four Dimensions are INTEGRATED LENSES for covenant navigation. Not theory—PRACTICE. Teleios persons use all four to discern before deciding, weigh before choosing, understand before acting. Covenant necessity, not academic luxury.

Part 2: Multiple Choice

15 questions | 3 points each | 45 points total

Question 11
What does 2 Timothy 2:15 mean by "rightly dividing the word of truth"?
✅ Correct Answer: B
2 Timothy 2:15: "Study to shew thyself approved unto God...rightly dividing the word of truth." This is EPISTEMOLOGICAL COMPETENCE—trained perception to distinguish truth from error, Scripture from culture, principle from preference. Section 1.2 foundation.
Question 12
Proverbs 14:15 contrasts the "simple" who believe every word with the "prudent" who:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Proverbs 14:15: "The simple believeth every word: but the prudent man looketh well to his going." Prudent = epistemologically mature. Verifies before accepting. Tests claims against Scripture. Distinguishes source from noise. Section 1.2 principle.
Question 13
According to Philippians 1:9-10, love must "abound...in knowledge and in all judgment" so believers may:
✅ Correct Answer: A
Philippians 1:9-10: Love must be ordered by knowledge and judgment to "approve things that are excellent." This is AXIOLOGY—discerning what is good vs. better vs. best. Love alone is insufficient; love must be ORDERED by truth. Section 2.2 foundation.
Question 14
Luke 14:26 teaches that disciples must "hate" father, mother, wife, children—this means:
✅ Correct Answer: D
Luke 14:26 is not literal hatred—it's AXIOLOGICAL HIERARCHY. God must be valued above all human loves. When loves compete, truth determines order. Matthew 22:37-39 establishes: God first, neighbor second, self rightly loved. Section 2.2 framework.
Question 15
A spouse says "I don't feel in love anymore, so I shouldn't stay." This demonstrates:
✅ Correct Answer: B
"I don't feel in love" = AXIOLOGICAL IMMATURITY. Elevating feelings above faithfulness, comfort over covenant. Immature spouse prioritizes emotional state; teleios spouse values covenant over comfort. Happiness is not highest good—holiness is. Section 2.5 example.
Question 16
Genesis 1:27 establishes that being "male and female" is:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Genesis 1:27: "Male and female created he them." This is ONTOLOGICAL REALITY—divinely designed, rooted in being itself. Not cultural construct, not personal preference. Gender is design fact, not subjective choice. Section 3.2 foundation.
Question 17
Matthew 19:4-6 declares that in marriage "they twain shall be one flesh." This means:
✅ Correct Answer: D
Matthew 19:6: "They are no more twain, but one flesh." ONE-FLESH = ONTOLOGICAL UNION. Being-level fusion, not emotional connection. Permanent reality, not pleasant feeling. "What God hath joined, let not man put asunder." Section 3.2 framework.
Question 18
Ephesians 5:31-32 calls marriage a "great mystery" because it:
✅ Correct Answer: B
Ephesians 5:31-32: "This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church." Marriage is TYPOLOGICAL ONTOLOGY—mirrors gospel reality. To dishonor marriage is to distort Christ-Church union. Section 3.2 depth.
Question 19
John 1:1 states "In the beginning was the Word (Logos)." This establishes that:
✅ Correct Answer: A
John 1:1: "In the beginning was the Word (Logos), and the Word was with God, and the Word was God." Language is FOUNDATIONAL TO REALITY. Words name realities, not preferences. Etymology is sacred stewardship. Section 4.2 foundation.
Question 20
Proverbs 18:21 declares "Death and life are in the power of the tongue." This means:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Proverbs 18:21: Words CREATE or DESTROY. Language is not neutral—it builds or breaks, clarifies or confuses. Death and life dwell in the tongue. Etymology = stewardship of language with moral consequences. Section 4.2 principle.
Question 21
Matthew 5:37 commands "Let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay." This teaches:
✅ Correct Answer: D
Matthew 5:37: "Let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil." Teleios communication = PRECISION. Ambiguity is language of deception. Clear yes, clear no. Honor meaning. Section 4.2 standard.
Question 22
A spouse redefines "forever" mid-conflict, saying "When I said forever, I meant as long as I feel it." This demonstrates:
✅ Correct Answer: B
Redefining "forever" = ETYMOLOGICAL IMMATURITY. Shifting definitions to avoid commitment. Immature: "I didn't mean literally." Teleios: "I said forever—I meant it." Vows become suggestions when words lose weight. Section 4.5 example.
Question 23
The Four-Lens Decision-Making Framework applies all four dimensions before major decisions. The correct order is:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Four-Lens Audit sequence: (1) EPISTEMOLOGY—How do I know this is true? (2) AXIOLOGY—What am I valuing most? (3) ONTOLOGY—What is the reality? (4) ETYMOLOGY—What do my words mean? Section 5.3 framework.
Question 24
When culture says "Follow your heart" and Scripture says "The heart is deceitful" (Jeremiah 17:9), the teleios response is:
✅ Correct Answer: A
Error #1: "Thinking doesn't matter—feelings do." TELEIOS RESPONSE: Feelings are real but not always true. Test feelings against truth. Heart is deceitful (Jeremiah 17:9). Epistemology over emotion. Section 6.1 correction.
Question 25
The Covenant Readiness Diagnostic (Section 7) scores 40-60 as "strong axiomatical maturity." This requires mastery in:
✅ Correct Answer: D
Covenant Readiness requires INTEGRATION of all four: (1) Epistemology—verify claims, (2) Axiology—order goods by truth, (3) Ontology—honor marriage as designed, (4) Etymology—keep promises, honor words. 12 questions, 5-point scale. Section 7 diagnostic.
