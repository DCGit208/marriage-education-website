Position overview

The Ambassador is the foundational deployment tier inside the Marriage Foundation Access Network.

The role of the Ambassador is to:

  • deploy Marriage Foundation Access daily
  • acquire recurring subscribers
  • expand educational access
  • support subscriber retention
  • grow toward leadership progression

A Marriage Foundation Access deployment operator

Not: a passive promoter, a one-time salesperson, or a temporary affiliate.

Core mission

To deploy recurring educational access daily across society.

Deployment infrastructure: sbd-network.web.app

Subscribers gain access to Marriage Foundation School, Teleios Formation, Advocacy Formation, Relationship Literacy, Personal Development, Leadership Systems, Marriage Education Infrastructure, Community Access, and future certifications.

$2,500+ equivalent educational access value.

Monthly deployment economics

Geometric recurring economics

The Ambassador builds recurring subscriber retention economics — not one-time commissions or temporary income.

  • Month 2: 400 retained subscribers

  • Month 10: 2,000 retained subscribers

As deployment and retention continue: commissions compound, recurring income compounds, subscriber infrastructure compounds.
