Position overview

Primary team leadership tier in the Marriage Foundation Access Deployment Network.

A team deployment leader

Build and supervise Ambassador teams, scale recurring deployment, maintain consistency, support retention, duplicate systems.

Network structure

10 Ambassadors × 200 subscribers/month each = 2,000 team subscribers/month

Each Ambassador: 10 subscribers/day × 20 deployment days.

Commission

5% override on recurring retained team subscriptions

~$0.45 per retained subscriber/month → ~$45/day and ~$900/month at full 2,000 retained team model.

Responsibilities

  • Ambassador supervision & inactive replacement
  • Deployment operations & performance
  • Retention operations
  • Leadership development & duplication

Daily operations

Team monitoring — activity, deployment numbers, growth, consistency.

Leadership communication — support, resolve challenges, discipline.

Deployment expansion — community, church, campus growth.

Retention monitoring — renewals, engagement, churn reduction.

Promotion

Regional Director — build and sustain a fully operational deployment structure.

Dashboard path to RD also tracks Senior leaders recruited toward the network target.

Final mandate

Scale Marriage Foundation Access through disciplined team deployment, recurring retention, operational duplication, and continuous educational access expansion.
