5-Day Freedom Lab Curriculum

1

Why Porn "Works" — And Why Marriage Must Do It Better

Foundation: Understanding the Power Dynamic

Pornography doesn't succeed by accident. It takes nakedness, imagery, desire, and seduction seriously — while many marriages abandon these divine assets. This day confronts the uncomfortable truth: porn is powerful because it imitates what belongs to covenant.

"And they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not ashamed." — Genesis 2:25 (KJV)

Core Teaching:

  • What Porn Imitates (That Belongs to Marriage): Unashamed display, directed allure, practiced presentation (Attitude, Attire, Allure)
  • The Problem Isn't Porn—It's Marital Abdication: When couples don't practice nakedness, seduction, or sexual imagination within covenant, counterfeits fill the void
  • Axiom: "What is unpracticed in covenant will be perfected by the counterfeit" (Romans 12:21)
  • The SBD Solution: You don't defeat porn by shame—you defeat it by a marriage that is more naked, more devoted, more alluring, more erotic, and more faithful than any substitute

Day 1 Exercise: The Honesty Audit

Individual Reflection (30 minutes):

Answer privately in your journal:

  • On a scale of 1-10, how practiced is nakedness in my marriage?
  • On a scale of 1-10, how practiced is seduction in my marriage?
  • What has been outsourced to screens that should be stewarded in covenant?
  • What am I afraid to practice in my marriage that porn does without shame?

Couple Exercise (if applicable): Share ONE answer each. No judgment. Just listening.

2

Nakedness as Divine Asset — Not Private Shame

Covenant Stewardship: Reclaiming Genesis 2:25

Nakedness is not a side-effect, an accident, or an embarrassment. It is the divine asset of marriage, the habitat of sexual fidelity, and the liturgical environment of covenant desire.

"The wife hath not power of her own body, but the husband: and likewise also the husband hath not power of his own body, but the wife." — 1 Corinthians 7:3-4 (KJV)

Core Teaching:

  • Your Body is Covenant Property: Not private property. Nakedness is mutual stewardship between husband and wife
  • Why Porn Has Power: It takes nakedness seriously (puts it on display, maneuvers it for pleasure, aims it with intensity) while many marriages hide, neglect, or despise their own
  • The SBD Directive: If husband and wife do not know, practice, celebrate, or share nakedness with skill and devotion, porn will teach the culture that marriage is irrelevant
  • Nakedness ≠ Nudity: Nakedness is relational, covenantal, and devoted. Nudity is clinical. Learn the difference.

Day 2 Exercise: Nakedness Inventory

Individual Reflection (20 minutes):

  • When was the last time I presented my nakedness as a gift to my spouse?
  • What shame narratives do I carry about my body that prevent covenant nakedness?
  • What would it look like to steward nakedness with the same intentionality porn uses—but within covenant?

Couple Exercise: Schedule your first "Song Hour" (Songs of Solomon 1:2; 2:16; 7:10) — 30 minutes of unashamed presence, no agenda except attention and appreciation.

3

Pics & Pearls — Covenant Imagery vs. Counterfeit Imagery

Erotic Imagination as Fidelity

Pornography does not own erotic imagination. Marriage does. Every affair, every porn habit, every secret fantasy starts in imagination. So imagination must be governed, not ignored.

"How fair and how pleasant art thou, O love, for delights! This thy stature is like to a palm tree, and thy breasts to clusters of grapes." — Song of Solomon 7:6-7 (KJV)

Core Teaching:

  • Pics & Pearls Defined: Private images, private phrases, private memories, private rituals that are exclusive to the covenant
  • Pics: Visual catalog—that text you saved, that voice note, that picture, that outfit, that night you keep remembering
  • Pearls: Verbal catalog—erotic language, affirmations, phrases that awaken desire ("You drive me wild when you...")
  • Rules of Covenant Imagery: Never for public. Never for strangers. Never for "likes." Never for leverage. Monogamous imagination only.
  • Scripture as Template: Song of Solomon functions as the biblical model for erotic language—body descriptions, fragrances, breasts, thighs, taste, and presence

Day 3 Exercise: Build Your Pics & Pearls Catalog

Individual Task (30 minutes):

  • Create a private "Pics" folder on your phone—for covenant images only (with spouse's consent)
  • Write down 5 "Pearls"—phrases or memories that arouse desire for your spouse
  • Delete or archive ALL images/memories/fantasies that are outside the covenant

Couple Exercise: Exchange one "Pearl" with your spouse. Practice erotic language without shame.

4

Marriage = Sexuality = Sex — Living the Lifestyle

Sexual Fidelity as Daily Stewardship

Sex is the product. Sexuality is the lifestyle. The world focuses on "sex acts"—but marriage focuses on "sexual life." Sexuality is how you dress, how you smell, how you present nakedness, how you steward your body, how you keep allure alive, how you maintain seduction.

"Let the husband render unto the wife due benevolence: and likewise also the wife unto the husband." — 1 Corinthians 7:3 (KJV)

Core Teaching:

  • Marital Fidelity = Sexual Fidelity: Not generic loyalty. Not emotional wellness. Not "relationship softness." Full sexual covenant—nakedness, arousal, allure, imagination, desire, seduction.
  • The A³ Practice: Attitude, Attire, Allure rehearsed weekly. How you show up sexually is your fidelity in action.
  • Sexuality Components: How I dress • How I smell • How I present nakedness • How I steward my body • How I keep allure alive • How I maintain seduction • How I keep sexual imagination faithful • How I build erotic memory • How I cultivate desire
  • This IS Marital Fidelity: Fidelity isn't defined by "not cheating" or "not watching porn." Those are symptoms of a broken or starved covenant. Fidelity is complete sexuality—showing up fully in the sexual union.

Day 4 Exercise: A³ Night — Attitude, Attire, Allure

Both Partners (45 minutes preparation):

  • Attitude: Decide to seduce (not just "be available"). Intentionality matters.
  • Attire: Dress for your spouse's desire. What awakens them? Wear it.
  • Allure: Create an environment—scent, lighting, music, touch—that says "I want you."

Tonight: Practice A³. Make it a weekly habit.

5

Building a Marriage Porn Can't Compete With

Covenant Superiority — Making Counterfeits Irrelevant

The goal is not survival. The goal is covenant superiority—a marriage so sexually alive, so erotically devoted, so nakedly practiced, that porn has nothing to offer.

"Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good." — Romans 12:21 (KJV)

Core Teaching:

  • The Teleios Marriage: Abundant, intelligent, erotic, covenantal joy—marriages that children can safely imitate and nations can safely depend on
  • Making Counterfeits Irrelevant: You don't fight porn with filters and shame. You make porn irrelevant by out-practicing it in every dimension—nakedness, devotion, allure, memory, imagination, and fidelity
  • Ongoing Practices: Song Hour (weekly), Pics & Pearls journaling (ongoing), A³ Night (weekly), Tender pursuit (daily), Seduction as lifestyle (constant)
  • The SBD Promise: Where sexuality is stewarded, fidelity becomes joy. Where sexuality is abandoned, infidelity thrives—not because good became evil, but because the good was never practiced.

Building the System:

  • Weekly Check-In (15 minutes): Green/Yellow/Red list—what's working, what needs attention, what's off-limits
  • Monthly Inventory: Review Pics & Pearls catalog. Add new memories. Celebrate milestones.
  • Quarterly Deep Dive: Re-read Marriage=Porn & Beyond book. Revisit exercises. Recalibrate covenant practices.
  • Accountability (Optional): Join an SBD accountability group or find a trusted mentor couple for ongoing support

Day 5 Exercise: Your 90-Day Freedom Plan

Together (60 minutes):

  • Write out your weekly habits: Song Hour (day/time), A³ Night (day/time), Check-In (day/time)
  • Set up accountability: Who will you tell about this journey? (Pastor, mentor couple, trusted friend)
  • Schedule your first monthly inventory (30 days from today)
  • Commit to reading companion books: The Art of Sexuality, The Art of Nakedness, Muliebrity/Virility Baron
  • Pray together: Ask God to make your marriage a testimony of covenant superiority

Congratulations! You've completed the 5-Day Freedom Lab. Now live it.
