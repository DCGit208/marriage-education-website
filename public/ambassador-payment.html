How to get your MTN MoMo Virtual Card

Choose one of these options:

  1. USSD: Dial *126*43# on your MTN phone and follow the prompts to create your MoMo Virtual Card.
  2. App: Download the MTN MoMo app from the Google Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS), open it, and create your MoMo Virtual Card there.
  3. You will receive your 16-digit card number, expiry date, and CVV (by SMS or in the app).
  4. Enter those details in the card form above to pay with Stripe.

Ensure your MoMo account has enough balance. Standard virtual-card creation fees may apply. This is for the Card / Stripe path — not a MoMo transfer to our number.
