Regional Director

The Regional Director is responsible for translating the Marriage Education deployment architecture into an organized regional operating system: leadership, recruitment, standards, coordination and expansion.

What the role does

Regional leadership is an operating responsibility.

The RD layer exists above individual acquisition. Its work is to create the environment in which teams can recruit, train, deploy and retain consistently.

A

Architect

Define territory, leadership relationships, priorities and operating cadence.

B

Develop

Identify and develop Senior Ambassadors capable of managing Education Operator teams.

C

Govern

Maintain standards, visibility and accountability across the regional network.

Leadership chain

The RD does not replace the operator. The RD makes the operator network scalable.
