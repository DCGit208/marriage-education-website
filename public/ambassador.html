The operator's work

Deployment is the bridge between education and impact.

The Education Operator does not merely promote a website. The role is to move a defined educational resource into real human use, then support continued participation.

01

Introduce

Explain the purpose, structure and practical value of the Foundation education pathway.

02

Deploy

Guide qualified users into Foundation Access and help them begin their education journey.

03

Retain

Support continued use and develop the operational discipline required for advancement.

Current operating economics

The deployment model is measurable.

The existing SBD Network framework states a target of 10 new subscribers per deployment day across 20 deployment days per month, or 200 new subscribers per month per Ambassador. These are operating targets, not guaranteed outcomes.
