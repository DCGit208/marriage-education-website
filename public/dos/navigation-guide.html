Deployment Operations — Navigation Guide

Full sitemap for your role. Use Guide on the dashboard for the interactive tour.

Where to start training (Formation): Open Formation in the left sidebar to see progress and enroll, or click Foundation School in the sidebar footer to open the course viewer (lessons). Top bar School goes to the same place.

Sidebar footer (every role)

  • Foundation School — Course viewer: Teleios Blueprint, advocacy weeks, CRLA (course-viewer.html?courseId=foundation-school).
  • Resources — Ambassador tools, downloads, and reference materials.
  • Leaderboard — Network-wide rankings (also under Performance in the sidebar).
  • Marriage Education home — Public website homepage.

Top bar

  • Guide — Replay the interactive walkthrough (rank-specific).
  • Map — This page.
  • Copy main link — Primary referral URL.
  • Home / School / Leaderboard — Same as footer links.
  • Logout — Returns to your role login page (Ambassador, Senior, RD, or Executive).

Main sidebar sections (deployment ops)

  1. Overview — KPIs, monthly target, quick actions.
  2. Daily ops — Log daily outreach.
  3. Field deploy — Field scripts.
  4. Deployment links — Referral & product URLs, SBD panel.
  5. Subscribers & pipeline — Prospects and subscribers.
  6. Retention — At-risk subscribers.
  7. Commissions — Earnings and history.
  8. Social deploy — Social captions.
  9. Reports — Exports.
  10. Network — Your recruits.
  11. Formation — CRLA progress + link to start school.
  12. Performance — Contests, stacks, milestones.

Suggested daily flow

  1. Overview → Daily ops → Field deploy / Links.
  2. Check Subscribers, Retention, Commissions.
  3. Training (weekly): Formation or Foundation School.
  4. Leaders: Team command daily.
  5. RD+: Regional command.
  6. Executive: Global command.
