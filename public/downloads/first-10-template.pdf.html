FIRST 10 BENEFICIARIES TEMPLATE

A tracking worksheet to map, deploy, and follow up with your initial advocacy network

Instructions: Every advocate begins with 10 names. Think through the people already in your life — family, friends, church, work, community. Identify their psychological state (Protection / Relief / Hope), match a resource to their need, and write a clear next step. This list is your first deployment field.

Where to Find Your First 10

Personal Relationships

  • Family preparing for marriage
  • Friends with relationship challenges
  • Colleagues navigating marriage
  • Siblings or cousins

Community Connections

  • Church members
  • Neighbors
  • Coworkers
  • Social media followers

High-Impact Targets ★

  • Pastors (reach entire congregations)
  • School teachers (reach families)
  • Community leaders (reach networks)
  • Business owners

Why High-Impact Targets Matter

  • A pastor who deploys Foundation School to his congregation = Community Access (100+ beneficiaries from one conversation)
  • A teacher who recommends the SBD books to parents = ripple deployment you can't do alone
  • Include at least 2 high-impact targets in your first 10

Psychological Mode Reference

🛡 Protection Mode

"I must defend myself from further harm."

Signs: Defensive, "I'm fine," skeptical, deflects, blames others

Approach: Slow down. Validate caution. Offer free resources first. No pitch.

🔧 Relief Mode

"I know something's wrong and I need help."

Signs: Open, sharing struggles, asking questions, actively searching

Approach: Name their pain. Match resource to problem. Offer clear next step.

🚀 Hope Mode

"We're doing okay — but we want to be great."

Signs: Excited, asks "what's next?", already consuming resources

Approach: Cast vision. Show path from good to great. Invite into deeper resources.

Daily Advocacy Practice

Morning Practice — 10 Minutes

1
Pray for your list — Call your first 10 beneficiaries by name before God.
2
Review your role — Re-read the Advocate's Code. Recommit to empathy-first deployment.
3
Plan one conversation — Who will you reach out to today? What is their mode?

Evening Practice — 15 Minutes

1
Reflect on conversations — What went well? What would you do differently?
2
Update your list — Record progress, mode changes, and next steps for each beneficiary.
3
Learn from resources — Read 10 pages from any Marriage Education resource you deploy.

Weekly Reflection

Question Your Notes
How many of my 10 have I reached out to this week?
Which conversation was hardest? What made it hard?
Which person showed the most openness? What was the result?
What did I learn about myself as an advocate this week?
Who on this list should I escalate to a Community Access deployment?
