THE ADVOCATE'S CODE

Five commitments that define ethical, empathy-first marriage literacy deployment

I Commit To:

1
Truth-Telling

I will never exaggerate or promise what resources cannot deliver. I represent what I know to be true — no hype, no manipulation.

2
Empathy-First

I will listen before I recommend. Every person I encounter deserves to be heard fully before I offer any solution.

3
Access-Focus

I will prioritize getting resources into hands, not just making sales. Access is the mission; commissions are the fruit, never the goal.

4
Community-Building

I will connect people to each other, not just to products. The goal is a network of growing households, not a solo income stream.

5
Continuous Learning

I will study the resources I deploy so I can guide wisely. I cannot lead someone to a destination I have not visited myself.

An Advocate IS:

  • A guide to resources, not a guru with all answers
  • A facilitator of access, not a salesperson
  • A connector between need and solution
  • A witness to transformation — sharing what you've experienced
  • A learner who stays ahead by staying humble

An Advocate is NOT:

  • A marriage counselor (not licensed to counsel)
  • A profit-maximizer (motive is access, not income)
  • A manipulator using people's pain to make sales
  • A lone ranger — you are part of a deployment network
  • A preacher delivering lectures no one asked for

Morning Advocacy Practice (10 min)

STEP 1
Pray for your list — Call your first 10 beneficiaries by name before God.
STEP 2
Review your role — Re-read the five commitments above. Recommit to empathy-first.
STEP 3
Plan one conversation — Who will you reach out to today? What is their mode (Protection / Relief / Hope)?
