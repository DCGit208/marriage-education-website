5 Faculties Self-Assessment

Instructions: Rate yourself honestly (1-10) in each faculty. Your lowest score is your starting point.

1. Behavioral Faculty: Self-Governance

Control your reactions, not just your actions. Keep your word even when it costs you.

• Can I keep my word when it costs me? /10
• Do I maintain discipline when no one is watching? /10
• Do I govern my behavior or do circumstances govern me? /10

2. Appetitive Faculty: Desire Stewardship

Govern your appetites; they don't govern you.

• Do I control my desires or do they control me? /10
• Can I fast without collapsing? /10
• Where am I enslaved to an appetite? /10

3. Relational Faculty: Conflict Repair

Disagree without destroying. Repair within 24 hours.

• Can I apologize cleanly without excuses? /10
• Do I repair within 24 hours or ghost for days/weeks? /10
• Do I own my portion or blame-shift? /10

4. Temperamental Faculty: Emotional Maturity

Feel deeply without being ruled by feelings.

• Can I remain steady under pressure? /10
• Do I use emotions to manipulate or inform? /10
• Do I react or respond? /10

5. Moral Faculty: Responsibility

Own your portion without blame-shifting.

• Do I own my failures or deflect? /10
• Can I say "I was wrong" without qualifiers? /10
• Do I demand from others what I refuse to become? /10
