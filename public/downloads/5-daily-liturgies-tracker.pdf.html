5 Daily Liturgies Tracker (7-Day Practice)

Morning Practice (10 minutes)

  1. Declare Your Identity — Write: "I was created teleios—whole, complete, perfect in God's image. I am justified in Christ. I am complete in Him. Today I practice FROM that perfection."
  2. Identify ONE Weakness — What's your recurring struggle? Anger? Lust? Fear? Conflict avoidance?
  3. Set a Replacement Intention — Instead of [weakness], I will [opposite strength] when [trigger occurs].

Evening Practice (15 minutes)

  1. Review Your Day — Where did I practice FROM perfection? Where did I forget my identity?
  2. Record One Failure + One Success — Be honest and specific. No shame, just data.
  3. Plan Tomorrow's Replacement — What will I do differently? What's my trigger? My new response?
