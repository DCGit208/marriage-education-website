Personal Access — Direct Purchase

The simplest deployment: an individual buys through your ambassador link

When to Use It

  • Person has financial capacity to purchase
  • They see the value immediately after your witness
  • They prefer private, self-paced learning
  • They are in Relief Mode or Hope Mode

5-Step Conversation Flow

1
Assess readiness — Are they in Relief or Hope mode?
2
Match resource to need — e.g., communication struggles → Communication Fidelity
3
Share your experience — be specific, stay humble
4
Provide a clear next step — "Here's my link, it's [price], you can start tonight"
5
Offer to journey together — follow up in 2 weeks
Example Statement

"I read Sex By Design and it transformed how I understand intimacy. Here's my ambassador link if you want to grab a copy — I'd love to discuss it with you after you read it."

2

Sponsored Access — Gift Deployment

Someone with resources sponsors books/courses for those who cannot afford them

The 3 Sponsor Types

  • Direct Sponsors — individuals who sponsor specific people they know personally
  • Anonymous Sponsors — donors who fund a pool of resources you deploy as needed
  • Organizational Sponsors — churches, NGOs, businesses that sponsor bulk access
Pitch to a Sponsor

"I know a couple preparing for marriage. They can't afford the Foundation School (120,000 FCFA). Would you sponsor their enrollment? I'll journey with them to ensure they complete it. Your investment could save their marriage before it even starts."

How to Activate It (5 Steps)

1
Identify high-need beneficiaries from your first 10 list
2
Find potential sponsors — who in your network has capacity AND heart?
3
Make the connection — bridge sponsor and beneficiary
4
Facilitate the gift — process through your ambassador link
5
Follow up with both — thank sponsor; journey with beneficiary through completion
Pitch to a Beneficiary

"Someone in our community wants to gift you the Foundation School — completely free, no strings attached. They believe in you. Would you be open to accepting it?"

Community Access — Group Deployment

Highest-impact model: churches, NGOs, workplaces host group studies — 10 to 500+ people per program

The 5 Community Access Formats

1. Church Marriage Series

6–12 weeks. Facilitated by an ambassador + church leadership. Ideal for couples at all stages.

2. Pre-Marriage Cohort

8 weeks. Designed for engaged couples. High conversion to full Foundation School enrollment.

3. Workplace Workshop

1-day intensive or lunch-and-learns over 8 weeks. HR-friendly pitch: reduced absenteeism, higher retention.

4. Community Circle

6–10 people, 6–8 weeks. Neighborhood or small-group format. Low overhead, high intimacy.

5. School Assembly

Ages 16–25. Preventive deployment before marriage. Single-session or 4-week module.

Community Access Blueprint (8 Steps)

1
Choose your community — church, workplace, neighborhood, school, or online group
2
Identify the need — what problem does this community face?
3
Select the resource — match to the identified need
4
Design the format — length, frequency, location, facilitator
5
Secure buy-in — pitch to leadership (pastor, HR director, principal)
6
Recruit participants — target minimum 10
7
Launch and facilitate — use the 90-minute session structure below
8
Measure impact — testimonies, completion rates, follow-on enrollments

90-Minute Session Structure

15
Check-In — "How did last week's practice go?" Accountability and celebration.
30
Teaching — 3 key takeaways + a story or real-life example from the facilitator.
30
Discussion — Small groups of 3–4 couples with reflection questions.
15
Application — "What will you practice this week?" Written + accountability partner assigned.
Pitch to a Church (example)

"Pastor, what if we ran a 6-week marriage preparation series using the Foundation School curriculum? I can facilitate it — the church just needs to cover the books for each couple."
