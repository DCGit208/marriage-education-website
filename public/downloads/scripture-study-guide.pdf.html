Scripture Study Guide: Week 1

Theme: Created Teleios — Practice FROM Perfection

1. Genesis 1:26-28 — The Original Design

"And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness... So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them."
Key Insight: Humanity was created perfect, whole, complete (teleios). You were made in God's image—not broken, not incomplete.

Reflection: What does it mean that I was created in God's image? How does this change how I see myself?

2. Matthew 5:48 — Be vs. Become

"Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect."
Key Insight: "Be" means to exist FROM perfection, not become perfect. Holiness is your template, not your target.

Reflection: How have I been trying to "become" perfect instead of practicing FROM my original design?

3. Ephesians 4:22-24 — State vs. Duty

"That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man... and that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness."
Key Insight: Righteousness = STATE (who you are in Christ). Holiness = DUTY (how you live FROM that state).

Reflection: Am I practicing holiness TO earn righteousness, or FROM the righteousness I already possess?

4. Romans 3:23-24 — Justified Freely

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus."
Key Insight: You have already been justified. It is finished. Your standing before God is settled.

Reflection: Do I believe I'm justified freely, or am I still trying to earn my righteousness?

5. Colossians 2:10 — Complete in Him

"And ye are complete in him, which is the head of all principality and power."
Key Insight: You are complete in Christ. Not "becoming" complete—you ARE complete NOW.

Reflection: What changes if I truly believe I'm already complete in Christ?

6. 2 Corinthians 5:17 — New Creation

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."
Key Insight: You are a new creation. The old patterns are passing away; new capacities are emerging.

Reflection: What "old things" need to pass away in my life? What "new things" is Christ creating?

7. 1 John 4:17 — As He Is, So Are We

"Herein is our love made perfect, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment: because as he is, so are we in this world."
Key Insight: As Christ is, so are you—NOW, in this world. Not "will be," but ARE.

Reflection: How would I live differently if I truly believed "as He is, so am I"?

Weekly Summary

What is the Holy Spirit teaching me this week about my identity in Christ?
