Before you begin

Questions worth answering up front.

What am I deploying?

Marriage Foundation Access: a one-year educational pathway that begins with The Teleios Blueprint and Sex By Design for Dummies, with School 1 content unlocking progressively. See the official Foundation Access page for the current offer.

Is the deployment target a guaranteed result?

No. The 10-per-day and 20-days-per-month cadence is an operating target. Actual paid access, team activity, and subscriber retention vary. It does not guarantee commissions or income.

How is Scholarship Access commission calculated?

Each qualifying deployed unit uses the approved unit commission basis, shown here as an approximately $133.33 USD equivalent, separate from its $144.17 USD retail price. The direct Ambassador rate is 10% (approximately $13.33 USD); eligible Senior and Regional overrides are 5% each (approximately $6.67 USD per role) on that unit. A one-time 12-month access purchase is credited on its qualifying deployment; it should not be described as a monthly recurring commission.

Is the Ambassador onboarding bonus the same as Foundation Access commission?

No. The onboarding bonus follows a separate policy and is credited after registration-payment approval. Subscription commissions apply to qualifying Foundation Access charges.

Does the Executive Network Director receive an override?

No separate Executive override or fourth subscription commission tier is defined in the active policy. Executive leadership is based on reproducing and overseeing complete Regional Director systems.

Can I apply directly as a Senior Ambassador or Executive?

No separate public Senior Ambassador registration or Executive application is linked here. New operators can begin through the existing Ambassador pathway. Executive Network Directors use the established login.

Where can I learn about the access product?

Visit the official Foundation Access page for the current education experience, price, and enrollment details.

Does this page replace my login or dashboard?

No. It explains the mission, roles, deployment model, compensation, and official entry routes. Existing dashboards and login flows remain separate.
