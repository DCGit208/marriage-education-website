The architecture

Production creates potential value. Deployment converts it into economic reality.

The Operator Network is designed around the final mile of education: getting high-value marriage education into the hands of the people, families and communities who need it.

01

Regional organization

Regional Directors establish territory, leadership structure, standards and operating rhythm.

02

Team deployment

Senior Ambassadors develop and coordinate teams of Education Operators for disciplined monthly deployment.

03

Direct acquisition

Education Operators take Foundation Access and marriage literacy resources directly to end users.
