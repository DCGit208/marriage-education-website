## Course Description

A complete transformation of self, identity, maturity, anthropology, and human design. Learn the template of wholeness required for covenant relationships. This course unpacks the biblical and legal framework of teleios (τέλειος) — complete, whole, perfect — as the prerequisite for marriage and all meaningful relationships.

**Biblical Foundation:** Hebrews 5:14 — "Strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age (teleios), even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil."

**Legal Foundation:** UDHR 16:1 — "Men and women of full age have the right to marry and to found a family."

**Educational Mandate:** UDHR 26:2 — "Education shall be directed to the full development of the human personality."

## Course Objectives

By the end of this 6-week journey, students will:

1. **Define and embody** the biblical concept of teleios (wholeness, completeness, maturity)
2. **Identify and develop** the Four Integrated Faculties (Conative, Cognitive, Affective, Psychomotor)
3. **Assess and strengthen** the Four Attributes of Personal Development (Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental)
4. **Apply** the Axiomatic Dimensions of Wholeness (Epistemology, Axiology, Ontology, Etymology)
5. **Understand** how teleios maturity scales from personal to domestic to civil order
6. **Evaluate** their covenant readiness using biblical diagnostics
7. **Practice** formation (not information) as the path to maturity

## Pedagogical Approach

### **Formation vs. Information**
This course emphasizes **formation** (structural change) over **information** (data collection). Each week includes:

1. **Core Teaching** - Biblical, legal, and philosophical foundations
2. **Diagnostic Self-Assessment** - Honest evaluation of current state
3. **Practical Drills** - Daily practices that install capacity
4. **Case Studies** - Real examples of teleios formation
5. **Reflection Questions** - Deep personal inquiry
6. **Weekly Assignment** - Application and accountability

### **The SBD Method**
- **Practice FROM perfection, not toward it** - You were created teleios; formation reveals that reality
- **"By reason of use"** (Heb. 5:14) - Senses exercised through practice, not theory
- **Anthropocentric development** - The human person is the central subject of development (Right to Development, Art. 2:1)

## Key Principles (The Teleios Framework)

### **1. The Teleios Definition**
**Greek:** τέλειος (teleios) from τέλος (telos: end, goal, consummation)  
**Meaning:** Complete, full-grown, brought to proper end, entire, whole, having achieved its purpose

### **2. The Teleios Standard**
"Be ye therefore perfect (teleioi), even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect." — Matthew 5:48

### **3. The Teleios Prerequisite**
"Men and women of full age have the right to marry" — UDHR 16:1  
Full age = teleios maturity

### **4. The Teleios Equation**
**Wrong:** 0.5 + 0.5 = "You complete me" (two halves seeking wholeness)  
**Right:** 1 + 1 = One Flesh (two wholes uniting in covenant)

### **5. The Teleios Formation**
"By reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil" — Hebrews 5:14  
Formation through practice, not information through theory

### **6. The Teleios Syllogism**
- **Major:** Only men and women of full age may marry (UDHR 16:1)
- **Minor:** Full age = teleios (Hebrews 5:14)
- **Conclusion:** Maturity/completeness is a prerequisite for marriage

### **7. The Teleios Consequence**
When persons are whole → vows are light  
When persons are partial → vows are heavy

##Errors to Reject

### **1. Perfectionism (Caricature)**
Perfection (teleios) is not neurosis; it is ontology — the wholeness and completeness by which all truth and justice are measured.

### **2. Passivity**
Grace does not replace formation; it enables it. "By reason of use" (Heb. 5:14) = practiced habit, exercised sense.

### **3. Paper Alchemy**
A license does not transmute infancy into adulthood. Documents record; they do not form.

### **4. Age-as-Number Reductionism**
Counting birthdays ≠ teleios

### **5. Therapeutic Marriage Myth**
Marriage is not the hospital for unfinished souls; it is the sanctuary of finished persons who keep finishing.
