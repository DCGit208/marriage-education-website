Why Cameroon Needs Marriage Education

Traditional systems are breaking down. Extended family involvement, church counseling, and cultural norms no longer prepare couples for modern marriage challenges:

  • Sexual illiteracy: Most couples enter marriage with zero practical sexual education
  • Financial conflict: No frameworks for money management within covenant
  • Communication gaps: Cultural silence around marital struggles
  • Divorce rising: Even in Christian communities
  • Porn epidemic: Silent crisis destroying marriages

Marriage education addresses these systematically with proven frameworks, not just motivation.
