## Course thesis

Identity is not self-invention. Identity is **design discovery** and **covenant responsibility**.

Course 02 installed anthropology: you are teleios by design; image is given; holiness is state; likeness is formed; the Four Attributes belong to personal development; and the Four Capacities/Faculties are distinct. Course 03 applies that identity — dismantling counterfeits, rejecting better-half ontology, and writing a covenant-safe identity code. Practice expresses responsibility; it does not manufacture identity or maturity.
