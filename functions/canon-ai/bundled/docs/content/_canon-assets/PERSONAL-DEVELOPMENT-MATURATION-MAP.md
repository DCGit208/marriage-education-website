## Attribute definitions (from map)

1. **Behavioral** — Self-control via action & reaction: possession, self-command, self-possession, self-will / will power  
2. **Temperamental** — Disposition — natural disposition / characteristic tendency; nature-state  
3. **Emotional** — Development of affective states: valence, arousal & motivational intensity  
4. **Mental** — Development of the mind (faculty of reason) & intellectual processes  

---

## Capacity definitions (from map)

1. **Conative** — Commitment / striving action  
2. **Cognitive** — Learning and reasoning  
3. **Psychomotor** — Physical & mental agility  
4. **Affective** — Care: forethought / empathy
