## The Central Truth

You are not four fragments assembled by training. You are **one teleios person** whose human architecture includes Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, and Affective capacities.

Integration does not create wholeness. It is the responsible, coordinated life of the whole person: Conative commits and strives; Cognitive learns and reasons; Psychomotor coordinates physical and mental action; Affective exercises care, forethought, and empathy. The car does not become a car by driving. The teleios person does not become human by coordinating faculties. Coordination is life according to design. Practice does not manufacture being. A spouse is better whole — not your missing faculty.

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do all four capacities operate together in ordinary and covenant life?

### Key Insight
No faculty should impersonate the whole person. Strong commitment without reasoning can become reckless; reasoning without action becomes sterile; action without care becomes mechanical; care without commitment becomes sentiment. Responsible living coordinates all four. Course 04’s title retains “Training,” but training means education and responsible stewardship — not creation of faculties or teleios.

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this week you will:

- Recite and define the Four Faculties accurately
- Distinguish capacities from attributes and skills
- Map one real decision through all four capacities
- Write a Four Faculties Stewardship Charter (teleios + better whole explicit)
- Build a 30-day education/accountability plan
- Complete an Integrated Responsibility Audit under load

## Core Teaching

### 1) The authoritative distinction

| Category | Canon list | Function |
|----------|------------|----------|
| Skills | Critical Thinking, Expression, Writing, Hearing, Reading | Learned competencies |
| Attributes | Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental | Attributes of personal development/personality |
| Capacities / Faculties | Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, Affective | Powers/capacities of the person |
| Maturation pillars | Etymology, Axiology, Epistemology, Ontology | Axiomatic dimensions/lenses |

If you cannot preserve these categories, return to the Personal Development Maturation Map before advancing. Attributes ≠ Faculties.

### 2) Teleios grammar at integration

| Fragment / manufacture grammar | One-person / teleios grammar |
|--------------------------------|------------------------------|
| “Training will assemble my wholeness” | “I am teleios; integration is stewardship” |
| Equal minutes for each faculty as worth | Appropriate coordination for the duty |
| Partner supplies the faculty I refuse | Better whole — I own my capacities |
| Strong output = ontology | Fruit is fruit; being is given |

### 3) The integration sequence

For a real duty:

1. **Cognitive:** What is true? What must I learn or reason?  
2. **Affective:** Who is affected? What care, forethought, and empathy are required?  
3. **Conative:** What commitment must I make?  
4. **Psychomotor:** What physical/mental coordination will execute it?  
5. **Review:** Does the action agree with design and covenant responsibility?  

This is not always chronological. The capacities interact dynamically. The sequence is an educational tool — not an ontology machine.

### 4) Faculty dominance and faculty neglect

| Pattern | Likely fruit |
|---------|--------------|
| Conative dominates | force, stubbornness, action without listening |
| Cognitive dominates | analysis without commitment or embodiment |
| Psychomotor dominates | performance/output without reason or care |
| Affective dominates | concern without decision, boundaries, or truth |

The goal is not equal minutes for each faculty. The goal is appropriate integration for the duty.

### 5) Integration and the Four Attributes

Faculties and attributes interact: Conative capacity may be expressed through Behavioral self-command; Cognitive capacity interacts with the Mental attribute; Affective capacity must not be collapsed into the Emotional attribute; Psychomotor action may be visible behavior without becoming the Behavioral attribute. There is no simple one-to-one replacement chart. Keep ontology disciplined.

### 6) Integration and covenant

A hard marital conversation needs Cognitive (facts vs interpretation), Affective (impact + empathy without ontology surrender), Conative (commit to repair), and Psychomotor (presence, pace, speech, act). None of this manufactures maturity. “You think/motivate/act/care for me” is better-half anthropology — each person owns their design while joining in one-flesh covenant.

### 7) Hebrews 5:14 correctly read

Those “of full age” have senses exercised in the course of use. The verse identifies the persons as being of full age; it does not say activity manufactures their ontology. Therefore: maturity = state of being mature / full development; use = life, responsibility, accountability; education = becoming mindful of design and able to live accordingly; practice = expression, not creation, of personhood.

### 8) Ratiocination

- Premise 1: One teleios person possesses four capacities by design.  
- Premise 2: Integration that assumes unfinished ontology becomes self-assembly religion.  
- Premise 3: Outsourcing any faculty to a partner denies better whole.  
- Conclusion: Charter and live coordinated stewardship without manufacturing being.

### 9) Language discipline

- “I’m still assembling myself.” → “I am teleios; today I coordinate ______.”  
- “My partner is my missing will/mind/body/care.” → “Better whole — I own that capacity.”  
- “Integration drills make me whole.” → “Integration expresses wholeness already given.”

### 10) Common errors this week

1. Attribute-as-faculty collapse  
2. Equal-minutes theater instead of appropriate coordination  
3. Drills as superiority or maturity manufacture  
4. Ignoring accessibility  
5. Partner as missing faculty  
6. Strong output called “wholeness”  
7. Soft better-whole rules that leave fusion legal  

### 11) Continuity + greatness criterion

The Charter irrigates later School 1 courses (mindful → educated → espouse design → live accordingly). If manufacture grammar or faculty outsourcing still wins most evenings, extend Week 5 — coordinated responsibility of one designed person, not fragment theory or passivity.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Faculty fragmentation / manufacture
Jordan treats Course 04 as four separate self-improvement tracks and claims “integration will finally make me whole.” Drills become worth theater. Fruit: exhausted vocabulary; unchanged covenant presence.

### Case B — Better-half faculty outsourcing
Alex can define all four capacities but lives: “You think for me / motivate me / make me show up / make me care.” Faculty language masks fusion. Fruit: partner cast as missing capacity; teleios denied.

### Case C — Integrated stewardship from teleios
Sam maps one hard conversation through all four capacities, keeps category clarity (Attributes ≠ Faculties), refuses outsourcing, and locks a Charter that says practice does not manufacture being. Fruit: coordinated responsibility; better whole speech.
