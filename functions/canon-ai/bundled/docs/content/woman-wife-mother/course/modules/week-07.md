## I. Ontology — What This Week Is

Week 6 integrated **meritorious attraction**—detail, form, presentation, psychomotor rigor, and **A³** minimums as covenant-scoped discipline. Week 7 specializes the **first term** of that grammar: **Attitude**.

**Attitude**, in SBD sexual architecture, is not mood label, not personality type, not therapeutic "positive thinking," and not woke self-definition divorced from design. **Attitude** is **covenant disposition**—the settled inclination of **conative** and **affective** faculties toward one's husband as **man and one-flesh partner**, not as roommate, co-parent, project, obstacle, or extra child to manage.

Attitude is **sexual intentionality made inward** before it dresses outward (Week 8 **Attire**) or allures outward (Week 9 **Allure**). Without trained attitude, the best wardrobe becomes costume; without trained attitude, allure becomes manipulation or performance. With trained attitude, the entire **A³** grammar serves ordered covenant life—person, marriage, family (UDHR 16:3).

```
A³ = Attitude → Attire → Allure
     (+ Fitness / Form / Shape — psychomotor extension)
```

This week completes the **foundation arc** (Weeks 1–7) before Weeks 8–10 specialize attire, allure, and fitness depth. Formation flow:

```
Identity → Consciousness → Discipline → Practice → Covenant Flourishing
```

Weeks 1–6 established ontology, teleios identity, muliebrity layers, bodily stewardship, lifestyle sexuality, and meritorious attraction. **Attitude** integrates them **inwardly**—because fragmentation often hides in disposition while outward roles still perform.
