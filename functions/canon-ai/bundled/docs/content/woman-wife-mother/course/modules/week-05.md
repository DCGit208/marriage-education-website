## I. Ontology — What This Week Is

Week 4 grounded **body, nakedness, and stewardship**—the material site where muliebrity becomes visible. Week 5 names what that stewardship **feeds**: **lifestyle sexuality**—the continuous conjugal life from which the **act of sex** is harvested as fruit, not manufactured as isolated event.

**Sex** is the **product**—the conjugal act, the one-flesh culmination, the physical harvest. **Sexuality** is the **lifestyle**—the trained continuity that produces that harvest: intention, dress, scent, form, touch, gaze, responsiveness, disposition, environment order, and psychomotor presence sustained across ordinary days—not anniversaries, not vacations, not crisis make-up sessions.

SBD doctrine insists: you cannot harvest conjugal fruit from a desexualized life. Sexuality is not **hearsay**—not articles read, sermons heard, podcasts absorbed, or complaints exchanged about "needs." Sexuality is **training**—deliberate behavioral and psychomotor discipline that keeps marriage on its **conjugal-sexual basis** (Genesis 2:24; 1 Corinthians 6:16; UDHR 16:3 family order).

This week intensifies **Discipline → Practice** in the formation flow:

```
Identity → Consciousness → Discipline → Practice → Covenant Flourishing
```

**Womanhood** remains foundation; **wife** lives in woman; **mother** lives in wife. **Lifestyle sexuality** is womanhood expression in covenant—not a compartment activated when calendar allows. Lose sexuality at the woman layer and wife/mother layers become **functional shells**—efficient, tired, respectable, structurally unfaithful to design.

Many couples treat the conjugal act as **repair button**—one evening to compensate for months of roommate life. Design does not work that way. The body remembers continuity; attraction reads daily signals; children read whether marriage is alive or administrative. When lifestyle markers die, the act becomes **duty performance** or **absent altogether**—both are fruit of the same neglected tree. Week 4 trained body and nakedness; Week 5 trains **what that body does in time**—how you move through ordinary hours as one-flesh woman, not only how you appear before mirror.

### Design reasoning — compartment religion vs. trained covenant

Sunday messages about purity do not water Monday-through-Saturday marriage if **lifestyle sexuality** is untrained. That is not "lack of faith"—it is **lack of discipline**. Faith without trained embodiment is sciolism Paul refuses when he says glorify God **in your body** (1 Corinthians 6:19–20). Reason from fruit: if conjugal life is dead while religious activity is high, the break is structural—fragmentation with hymnbook.

### Motherhood trap (name it)

Children-centered home environment that **crowds out** conjugal intention is structural deformation—not virtue, not "good mom" badge. The child who never sees one-flesh priority modeled inherits fragmentation. UDHR 16:3 family order requires **marriage as conjugal basis**, not co-parenting firm with occasional date night.

### Desexualization as structural infidelity

**Desexualization**—sexual-feminine womanliness evacuated from daily covenant flesh—is **marital infidelity at structure** before affair. The union still has certificate, meals, and children while **design union** has died. Name it without affair drama: structure broke.

### Husband joined through womanhood

See husband as **one-flesh partner**, not shift supervisor. He is joined to **muliebrity in operation**—not to your task completion. Behold him as teleios man in covenant likewise—reciprocity in lifestyle sexuality, not scorekeeping.

When lifestyle sexuality dies inside marriage, counterfeit grammar wins—not because design failed, because **training** failed.

### Meritorious consciousness

Initiative and responsiveness are **merit**—trained offering, not entitlement. *"He should desire me without my lifestyle"* is certificate theology—rejected. *"I steward conjugal continuity as covenant duty"* is design alignment.

### Three witnesses
