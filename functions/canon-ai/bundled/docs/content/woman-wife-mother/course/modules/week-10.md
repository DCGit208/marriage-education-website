This week addresses **Fitness, Form, and Shape** — the **psychomotor extension** of **A³** (Attitude → Attire → Allure). If A³ is the operational grammar of muliebrity at the levels of disposition, dress, and atmosphere, Fitness/Form/Shape is the **bodily substrate** that makes all three credible. A teleios woman does not abandon her body while demanding attraction. She trains **psychomotor capacity** — strength, mobility, proportion, posture, health, and the visible form of stewardship — as covenant fidelity, not vanity sport and not therapeutic self-esteem project.

**Fitness** is functional capacity: energy, endurance, strength sufficient for life, motherhood, and longevity of conjugal joy. **Form** is how the body carries itself — posture, gait, muscle tone, the message of presence. **Shape** is the feminine architecture of the body stewarded over time — not chasing every cultural fad, but refusing neglect, gluttony, sloth, and the desexualization of a body left to decay. Together they answer a design question: *Has this woman treated her body as one-flesh property or as disposable vehicle?*

Womanhood is foundation. The wife lives in the woman. The mother lives in the wife. Therefore fitness is not "getting back pre-baby body" as vanity alone — it is **reclaiming teleios form** so the integrated woman remains present in every expression. A mother who disappears into exhaustion without bodily discipline has not sinned by bearing children; she has allowed **fragmentation** to sever psychomotor formation from muliebrity. A wife who expects fervor while rejecting training has embraced **entitlement**.

This week defeats **body abandonment** — one of the chief deformations named in SBD anthropology. It defeats **sciolism** that quotes Scripture about inner beauty while refusing embodied rigor. It defeats **woke entitlement** that treats health demands as oppression while still craving desire. It defeats **fragmentation** that says there is no time for the body because the household eats everything.

## III. Discipline — What You Must Train

Train **baseline movement**: walking, resistance, mobility work — minimum non-negotiables established this week, not someday. Frequency beats intensity for formation entry.

Train **posture and form**: shoulders, spine, core activation in standing, sitting, walking. Form is allure in structure — before scent, the body speaks.

Train **nutrition as covenant stewardship**: not diet culture, not endless indulgence. Meals that fuel teleios capacity — planned, not accidental survival.

Train **sleep and recovery**: psychomotor rigor includes rest discipline. Chronic depletion is not martyrdom; it is poor stewardship that kills form.

Train **feminine movement patterns**: glutes, hips, core, upper back — functional strength for life and feminine shape without masculinization. Choose training aligned with your body, medical clearance, and design.

Train **measurement honesty**: weight, measurements, energy, or performance markers — data for discipline, not shame. What gets measured gets trained.

Train **integration with A³**: attire fits the body you are building; allure moves through a body that carries confidence. Link psychomotor work to conjugal aim without reducing yourself to his taste only.

Train **grooming as psychomotor**: hair, skin, nails, hygiene — temple maintenance parallel to movement; not vanity theater, not neglect.

Train **gait awareness**: walk with purpose; hips stable; head level — form is allure in structure before scent.

Train **medical honesty**: work with providers where needed; training respects injury, pregnancy, season — discipline adapts, does not vanish.

Train **renunciation of comparison**: social media shape envy repented; covenant body is **your** stewardship assignment.
