## I. Ontology — What This Week Is

Week 1 named **fragmentation**—wholeness broken when womanhood is evacuated from covenant flesh. Week 2 named **teleios womanhood**—the foundation guarded through faculties, attributes, and practice from perfection. Week 3 names **muliebrity**: what the teleios woman **really is in operation**—defined precisely, trained measurably, deployed in covenant.

Nothing in SBD operates outside **design**. Sex by Design means sexuality, womanhood, wifehood, and motherhood each have **ordered purpose** tied to anatomy, Scripture, and fruit—not mood, not culture's costume, not sciolism. **Function in covenant is not the enemy of design; function flows from design.** What fails is **fragmentation**: wifehood or motherhood running **without** womanhood as foundation—roles disconnected from the teleios woman they are meant to express.

### Core definitions (state these before anything else)

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Womanhood** | The state of being an adult woman—the teleios female person established in design (Genesis 1:27). |
| **Femininity / Womanliness** | The trait of behaving in ways proper to female design—marked in the body, in nature, and in **conscious awareness** of that design as her identity. When she lives less of it, the **purpose** of her design is thwarted—sexual confusion, marital infidelity, pornography, failed espousal of womanliness, and absence of covenant training follow. |
| **Muliebrity** | The **conscious operational state** of teleios womanhood—femininity fully aware, disciplined, and deployed in covenant. Womanly strength and grace as **covenant power**: outward, inward, and conjugal layers integrated toward one-flesh flourishing in the home. |
| **Wifehood** | Womanhood in one-flesh matrimony—conjugal sexuality, due benevolence, covenant helper (Genesis 2:18); **full wife doctrine in Week 11** (Ephesians 5, *Matrimonial Order*). |
| **Motherhood** | Wifehood extended in the generative family unit—woman and wife still governing the mother expression. |

**Muliebrity** is how the teleios woman **lives** her design: deliberately, covenantally, in full female expression—without masculinization, infantilization, survival drift, desexualization, or entitlement. It is **femininity operational**—not negated, not apologized for, not replaced by logistics.

### What muliebrity is made of (grounding aspects—discipline requires content)

Muliebrity cannot be named without what **keeps it meritoriously attractive** and **measurable in practice**. These five grounding aspects are the core architecture—Weeks 4–10 train each exhaustively; Week 3 installs the map:

| Aspect | What it governs | Covenant role |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Attitude** (A³) | Disposition, intention, sexual-feminine inclination toward covenant life | Inward and conjugal muliebrity—how she **faces** marriage and home |
| **Attire** (A³) | Dress, grooming, presentation—including @Home | Outward muliebrity **visible**—dressing for nakedness in covenant, not culture's costume |
| **Allure** (A³) | Mystery, responsiveness, conjugal magnetism within one flesh | Meritorious attraction **offered**—not entitlement, not performance theater |
| **Fitness** | Form, strength, psychomotor rigor, movement across the lifespan | Body as covenant property—design readable in flesh |
| **Nutrition** | Healthy eating as stewardship of the frame God knit | Sustained energy and form for decades of covenant duty |

**A³** (Attitude, Attire, Allure) plus **Fitness** and **Nutrition** are not accessories to muliebrity—they are how muliebrity **stays attractive by merit**. Certificate does not command desire. Without these aspects trained, discipline has nothing to measure and practice has nothing to align.

This is **femininity by design**—womanliness conscious and covenant-deployed. **Wifehood**—womanhood in one-flesh matrimony—is **previewed** here and taught exhaustively in **Week 11**. This is the **positive architecture** of integrated womanhood—and the operational grammar Weeks 4–10 train in body, sexuality, A³, and form.

*Muliebrity is Your design in me—femininity conscious, outward and conjugal integrated—not counterfeit costume.*

*If the foundations be destroyed, I will not pretend the house stands—I guard muliebrity diligently (Psalm 11:3).*

*Train Attitude, Attire, Allure, Fitness, Nutrition—from teleios wholeness deployed in covenant.*

*Let my husband meet muliebrity in one-flesh life—womanhood governing wifehood and motherhood (Week 11).*

*I turn from fragmentation, desexualization, and entitlement; I align to Scripture and design—reason with me (Isaiah 1:18).*

*I steward our home as site where muliebrity teaches order, affection, responsibility, fidelity—as heir in Christ.*

*Through Christ, the perfect teleios likeness. Amen.*
