## 🎯 System Overview

The Marriage Education Ambassador Program operates on a **3-tier commission structure** designed to reward both individual sales performance and team building. The system is built on mathematical certainty—designed outcomes, not ambitious targets.

## 📊 Commission Structure

### Total Commission Payout: 20% of Revenue

| Tier | Commission Rate | Paid On | Recipient |
|------|----------------|---------|-----------|
| **Tier 1** | 10% | Ambassador's sales | Ambassador |
| **Tier 2** | 5% | Team sales (override) | Senior Ambassador |
| **Tier 3** | 5% | Network sales (override) | Regional Director |
| **TOTAL** | **20%** | Per regular ambassador sale | All 3 tiers |

**Note:** Senior Ambassador personal sales pay only 10% (they keep their commission, no override).

## 🔄 Real-Time Commission Processing

### How It Works:

1. **Sale Recorded:**
   - Customer purchases product ($30,000)
   - Sale tracked in Firestore with ambassador code

2. **Commission Calculation (Cloud Function):**
   ```javascript
   // Tier 1: Ambassador (10%)
   ambassadorCommission = saleAmount × 0.10

   // Tier 2: Senior Override (5%)
   if (ambassador.senior_code) {
     seniorCommission = saleAmount × 0.05
   }

   // Tier 3: RD Override (5%)
   if (ambassador.regional_director_code) {
     rdCommission = saleAmount × 0.05
   }
   ```

3. **Commission Credits:**
   - Ambassador: $3,000 credited to balance
   - Senior: $1,500 credited to balance
   - RD: $1,500 credited to balance

4. **Dashboard Updates:**
   - Real-time balance updates
   - Sales feed shows new transaction
   - Monthly target progress updated

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Do I earn override on senior personal sales if I'm an RD?
**A:** No. RDs earn 5% network override only on the 500 regular ambassadors' sales, not on the 50 seniors' personal sales. Seniors keep their full 10% commission on personal sales.

### Q2: What happens to my income when I'm promoted?
**A:** 
- **Ambassador → Senior:** Income increases from $3K to $18K (6x)
- **Senior → RD:** Income increases from $18K to $750K (41.67x)
- Previous commission structure continues + new override added

### Q3: Can I recruit ambassadors directly under me as an RD?
**A:** Yes. Any ambassador recruited with your RD code (`?rd=RD-XXXX-XXXX`) joins your network. They can be assigned to any of your 50 seniors for team building.

### Q4: How long does it take to reach RD level?
**A:** Typical timeline: 2-3 years
- **Year 1:** Build your 10 ambassadors, become senior
- **Year 2-3:** Recruit and develop 50 senior ambassadors
- Accelerated path: 18 months with focused recruitment

### Q5: What if one of my ambassadors gets promoted to senior?
**A:** They remain in your network. As a senior, you continue earning override on their new team of 10. As an RD, you earn network override on their team's sales.

### Q6: Is there a cap on earnings?
**A:** No. The system scales:
- RD with 100 seniors (1,000 ambassadors): $1,500,000/month
- RD with 200 seniors (2,000 ambassadors): $3,000,000/month
- Network override is 5% regardless of size

### Q7: How are commissions paid out?
**A:** 
1. Commissions credited to dashboard balance in real-time
2. Payout requests processed within 72 hours
3. Payment via bank transfer or mobile money
4. Minimum payout: $500 (to reduce transaction fees)

### Q8: What happens if I don't meet the $30K target?
**A:** No penalties. Commission is performance-based:
- Sell $15K = Earn $1,500 (10%)
- Sell $30K = Earn $3,000 (10%)
- Targets are designed outcomes, not requirements

### Q9: Can I be demoted?
**A:** No. Once promoted, your tier status is permanent. However, inactive accounts may have reduced dashboard access after 12 months of zero sales.

### Q10: How does the Foundation School affect commissions?
**A:** Foundation School is required training but doesn't affect commission rates. All ambassadors earn 10% regardless of credential status. However, CRLA certification increases deployment effectiveness and customer trust.
