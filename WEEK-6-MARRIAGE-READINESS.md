## SECTION 1: CRITICAL CLARIFICATION — WHAT TELEIOS IS NOT

Before the diagnostic, let's reject common misapplications.

### 1.1 Misapplication #1: Age-as-Number Reductionism

**Error:**  
"I'm 25, so I'm ready to marry."

**Clarification:**  
Counting birthdays ≠ teleios.

**UDHR 16(1)** says "men and women of **full age**" — not "any age."  
**Hebrews 5:14** defines full age as **teleios** — mature, complete, exercised senses.

**The Test:**  
Are your **senses exercised to discern** good and evil?  
Or are you merely **old enough** to sign paperwork?

**Teleios Response:**  
Age is a **prerequisite**, not proof. Legal adulthood opens the door; teleios maturity walks through it.

---

### 1.2 Misapplication #2: Paper-Replaces-Person Error

**Error:**  
"We have a marriage license, so we're married."

**Clarification:**  
**A license does not transmute infancy into adulthood.**  
Documents **record**; they do not **form**.

**Illustration:**

| Document | What It Records | What It Cannot Do |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Marriage License | Legal status | Form character |
| Diploma | Academic completion | Install maturity |
| Driver's License | Permission to drive | Guarantee safe driving |

**The Test:**  
Does the paper change the person?  
Or does the person give weight to the paper?

**Teleios Response:**  
Paper alchemy is a lie. Covenant is built by **character**, not by **contracts**.

---

### 1.3 Misapplication #3: Therapeutic Marriage Myth

**Error:**  
"Marriage will fix me."

**Clarification:**  
**Marriage is not the hospital for unfinished souls.**  
**It is the sanctuary of finished persons who keep finishing.**

**The Immature Belief:**

- "My spouse will heal my wounds."
- "Marriage will make me whole."
- "I'll grow in the relationship."

**The Teleios Truth:**

- "I must be healed **before** I covenant."
- "I must be whole **to enter** covenant."
- "I must grow **now**, not defer growth to 'someday.'"

**The Test:**  
Are you waiting for marriage to complete you?  
Or are you completing yourself **now**?

**Teleios Response:**  
Marriage is built **FROM** maturity, not **FOR** it.

---

### 1.4 Misapplication #4: "I Can't Help How I Feel"

**Error:**  
"Feelings justify behavior."

**Clarification:**  
**Feelings are real, but they are not always true.**

**Jeremiah 17:9:**  
> "The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?"

**The Immature Pattern:**

- "I don't feel married anymore, so I should leave."
- "I feel attracted to someone else, so it must be meant to be."
- "If it doesn't feel right, it isn't right."

**The Teleios Pattern:**

- "My feelings don't dictate my fidelity."
- "I govern my emotions; they don't govern me."
- "Truth > feelings."

**The Test:**  
Do your feelings lead you, or does truth?

**Teleios Response:**  
The teleios person **feels deeply** without **fragmenting under emotion**.

---

### 1.5 Misapplication #5: "Marriage Is What We Make It"

**Error:**  
"We can define our own relationship."

**Clarification:**  
**Marriage is designed by God. We submit to the structure; we don't redefine it.**

**Matthew 19:6:**  
> "What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder."

**The Immature Approach:**

- "We'll do marriage our way."
- "Traditional marriage doesn't work anymore."
- "We can rewrite the rules."

**The Teleios Approach:**

- "Marriage is what God made it."
- "We honor the design, not our desires."
- "We submit to the structure."

**The Test:**  
Are you honoring **ontology** (what marriage IS)?  
Or are you following **preference** (what you WANT it to be)?

**Teleios Response:**  
Covenant is **ontological**, not optional.

## SECTION 4: THE COVENANT COMPETENCY TEST

Beyond the readiness scan, ask these **9 qualitative questions**. Answer honestly.

### 1. Do I discern before I decide?

**Test (Hebrews 5:14):**  
Are your senses **exercised** to discern good and evil?  
Or do you react impulsively, emotionally, or culturally?

**Teleios Standard:**  
You pause. You test. You verify. You don't decide until you discern.

**Question:**  
Can you give a recent example where you discerned truth **before** acting?

---

### 2. Can I hold peace under contradiction?

**Test (Philippians 2:2-3):**  
> "Fulfil ye my joy, that ye be likeminded, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind. Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves."

**Teleios Standard:**  
You remain steady when challenged, contradicted, or misunderstood.

**Question:**  
How do you respond when someone criticizes you? Do you defend immediately, or do you listen first?

---

### 3. Do I practice covenant habits before I promise covenant vows?

**Test:**  
- **Fidelity:** Do you keep small promises now?
- **Communication:** Do you clarify before assuming?
- **Self-Governance:** Do you master your will, mind, heart, body?

**Teleios Standard:**  
You practice NOW what you'll vow LATER.

**Question:**  
What covenant habits are you practicing today (even if you're not yet married)?

---

### 4. Do I own outcomes rather than outsource blame?

**Test:**  
When something goes wrong, do you:

- **Immature:** Blame others, circumstances, or "bad timing"?
- **Teleios:** Take responsibility, ask "What's mine to own?" and correct?

**Teleios Standard:**  
You own outcomes. You don't deflect. You don't excuse. You take responsibility.

**Question:**  
When was the last time you owned a mistake without blaming someone else?

---

### 5. Am I whole, or am I waiting for marriage to complete me?

**Test:**  
Do you believe:

- "I need someone to make me whole" (immature)?
- "I am whole in Christ; marriage is overflow, not need" (teleios)?

**Teleios Standard:**  
You are complete in Christ. Marriage is built FROM your wholeness, not FOR it.

**Question:**  
If you never married, would you still be whole? Or do you **need** marriage to feel complete?

---

### 6. Can I love when I don't feel like it?

**Test:**  
Love is not a feeling. It's a **covenant action**.

**1 Corinthians 13:4-7:**  
> "Charity suffereth long, and is kind… beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things."

**Teleios Standard:**  
You love because you **vowed to**, not because you **feel like it**.

**Question:**  
Can you serve someone even when you're disappointed, tired, or hurt?

---

### 7. Do I honor design over desire?

**Test:**  
Do you submit to God's design for marriage (ontology)?  
Or do you try to redefine marriage to fit your preferences?

**Teleios Standard:**  
Marriage is what God made it. You honor the structure.

**Question:**  
Are there areas where you've tried to "customize" covenant to fit your desires?

---

### 8. Do my words carry weight?

**Test (Etymology):**  
When you say "I will," do you mean it?  
Or do you redefine promises later?

**James 5:12:**  
> "But let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay; lest ye fall into condemnation."

**Teleios Standard:**  
Your words mean what they mean. You don't shift definitions to escape accountability.

**Question:**  
When was the last time you kept a hard promise, even when you wanted to break it?

---

### 9. Am I self-governing, or do I require external control?

**Test:**  
Do you govern yourself from within (conscience, conviction, character)?  
Or do you need constant external pressure (rules, surveillance, threats)?

**Galatians 5:23:**  
> "Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law."

**Teleios Standard:**  
You are internally regulated. You don't need law to govern you; conscience does.

**Question:**  
Can you do what's right even when no one is watching?

## SECTION 5: YOUR 12-MONTH TELEIOS FORMATION PLAN

Based on your **Readiness Scan** and **Competency Test**, build a personalized formation plan.

### Step 1: Identify Your 3 Weakest Domains

Review your **Week 1-5 Subtotals**. Which 3 scored lowest?

**Example:**

- Week 2 (Faculties): 28/50 → Focus on integrating will, mind, heart, body
- Week 3 (Attributes): 42/75 → Focus on emotional and behavioral development
- Week 4 (Dimensions): 35/60 → Focus on epistemology and axiology

---

### Step 2: Set Domain-Specific Goals

For each weak domain, set **1 measurable goal** for the next 12 months.

**Example Goals:**

| Domain | Goal | Measurement |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Conative (Will) | Keep 100% of promises for 90 days | Track daily in journal |
| Emotional Development | Practice "quick to listen, slow to wrath" in all conflicts | Zero reactive outbursts for 60 days |
| Epistemology | Verify 3 cultural claims against Scripture weekly | Weekly audit log |

---

### Step 3: Assign Daily Practices

For each goal, assign **1 daily practice** (10-15 minutes).

**Example Practices:**

- **Conative:** Morning vow practice (make 3 small promises, keep them by evening)
- **Emotional:** Evening emotion audit (rate tenderness, resilience, governed desire)
- **Epistemology:** Daily claim verification (test 1 cultural belief against Scripture)

---

### Step 4: Monthly Review and Adjustment

At the end of each month:

1. **Review:** Did you complete daily practices 80%+ of the time?
2. **Reflect:** What growth do you see? What obstacles remain?
3. **Adjust:** Refine goals or practices as needed.

---

### Step 5: Quarterly Reassessment

Every 3 months, retake the **Teleios Marriage Readiness Scan**.

**Track Progress:**

| Quarter | Total Score | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Month 0 | 142/250 | Starting point: formation-needed |
| Month 3 | 168/250 | +26 points: improving in Weeks 2-3 |
| Month 6 | 195/250 | +27 points: strong progress, focus on Week 4 |
| Month 9 | 218/250 | +23 points: nearing readiness |
| Month 12 | 235/250 | +17 points: covenant-ready |

**Goal:** Reach **210+** (Covenant-Ready) before pursuing marriage.

---

### Step 6: Accountability Partnership

Formation requires **accountability**.

**Find:**

- A mentor (older, married, teleios)
- A peer group (fellow formation-seekers)
- A pastor or counselor (for deeper issues)

**Meet:** Weekly or bi-weekly to report progress, confess struggles, and receive correction.
