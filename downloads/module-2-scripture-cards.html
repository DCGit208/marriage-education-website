Module 2 Scripture Memory Cards

Cut out and memorize these foundational passages

How to Use: Cut along the borders to create individual cards. Memorize one card per week. Carry them with you and review daily. Quiz yourself by reading the reference first, then reciting the verse.

Genesis 2:24-25 (ESV)

"Therefore a man shall leave his father and his mother and hold fast to his wife, and they shall become one flesh. And the man and his wife were both naked and were not ashamed."
Design Truth: DESIGN ORDER: LEAVE (independence) → CLEAVE (commitment) → ONE FLESH (sexual union). "Naked and unashamed" = original ATMOSPHERE of marriage (design baseline, not spiritual ideal). Sex creates biological one-flesh reality, not metaphor.

1 Corinthians 6:16-20 (ESV)

"Or do you not know that he who is joined to a prostitute becomes one body with her? For, as it is written, 'The two will become one flesh.'... Flee from sexual immorality. Every other sin a person commits is outside the body, but the sexually immoral person sins against his own body. Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you, whom you have from God? You are not your own, for you were bought with a price. So glorify God in your body."
Design Truth: Sex = Marriage (v.16 equation)—sexual union creates ONE-FLESH REALITY (biological + covenant bond). Sex outside covenant violates your DESIGN (v.18—uniquely affects body because one-flesh bond forms). Your body is designed for covenant expression (v.19-20), not fragmentation.

1 Corinthians 7:3-4 (ESV)

"The husband should give to his wife her conjugal rights, and likewise the wife to her husband. For the wife does not have authority over her own body, but the husband does. Likewise the husband does not have authority over his own body, but the wife does."
Design Truth: CONJUGAL DUTY—bodies as covenant PROPERTY. Fidelity = FULL PARTICIPATION (not just "not cheating"). Marriage grants mutual authority over each other's bodies. Covenant requires active engagement, not passive coexistence.

Proverbs 5:18-19 + Revelation 4:11 (ESV)

"Let your fountain be blessed, and rejoice in the wife of your youth, a lovely deer, a graceful doe. Let her breasts fill you at all times with delight; be intoxicated always in her love." (Prov 5:18-19)

"Worthy are you, our Lord and God, to receive glory and honor and power, for you created all things, and by your will they existed and were created [for thy pleasure they are created]." (Rev 4:11)
Design Truth: EPICURE ORIGINATIVENESS (Rev 4:11)—pleasure originates in design ("for thy pleasure they are created"). TRUE hedonism = devotion to marital pleasure AS STEWARDSHIP within covenant (Prov 5:18-19). Sexual delight and "intoxication" with spouse is designed feature, not learned behavior.

Hebrews 13:4 (ESV)

"Let marriage be held in honor among all, and let the marriage bed be undefiled, for God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterous."
Design Truth: Marriage and the "marriage bed" (sexual intimacy) must be honored by EVERYONE—single, engaged, married. "Undefiled" = covenant expressions reserved FOR covenant. Honor marriage by practicing design order (LEAVE → CLEAVE → ONE FLESH), not cultural disorder.

Hebrews 5:14 + 1 Thessalonians 4:3-5 (ESV)

"But solid food is for the mature [teleios], for those who have their powers of discernment trained by constant practice to distinguish good from evil." (Heb 5:14)

"For this is the will of God, your sanctification: that you abstain from sexual immorality; that each one of you know how to control his own body in holiness and honor, not in the passion of lust like the Gentiles who do not know God." (1 Thess 4:3-5)
Design Truth: TELEIOS formation (Heb 5:14)—wholeness developed through constant practice. Learn self-command NOW (1 Thess 4:4)—control your body through discipline, not suppression. You ARE complete (teleios); practice FROM that perfection through formation.

Psalm 119:9, 11 + Genesis 1:27-28 (ESV)

"How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to your word... I have stored up your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you." (Ps 119:9, 11)

"So God created man in his own image, in the image of God he created him; male and female he created them. And God blessed them." (Gen 1:27-28)
Design Truth: ANTHROPOGENIC PLEASURE (Gen 1:27-28)—you're BORN sexual (inherent attributes). Sexual LITERACY develops through design reasoning (Ps 119:9)—guard your way through understanding DESIGN TRUTH. Know Scripture as fact canon about your anatomy, not religious rulebook.

Romans 12:1-2 + Matthew 5:48 (ESV)

"I appeal to you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship. Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind..." (Rom 12:1-2)

"You therefore must be perfect [teleios], as your heavenly Father is perfect [teleios]." (Matt 5:48)
Design Truth: PRACTICE FROM PERFECTION (Matt 5:48 + Rom 3:24)—you ARE perfect (created Gen 1:26-28, justified Rom 3:24), therefore PRACTICE that identity (Rom 12:1). Don't conform to cultural sexual disorder; practice your teleios identity through discipline. Renew your mind = install design reasoning.
