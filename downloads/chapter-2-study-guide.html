Chapter 2 Study Guide

Sexual Literacy Foundations

Key Concepts

  • Sexual literacy is foundational knowledge required BEFORE pursuing sexual expression (moral duty, not optional)
  • Nakedness without shame (Gen 2:25) = original ATMOSPHERE of marriage (design baseline, not spiritual concept)
  • Sexual hedonism (correctly defined) = devotion to marital pleasure AS STEWARDSHIP within covenant (Rev 4:11)
  • Epicure Originativeness: Pleasure originates in design—"for thy pleasure they are created" (Rev 4:11)
  • Anthropogenic pleasure: You're BORN sexual (Gen 1:27-28)—inherent attributes vs. covenant practice distinction
  • Sex = Marriage: 1 Cor 6:16 equation—sex creates one-flesh reality (biological + covenant bond)
  • Certificate vs. Covenant: Paper records; sex CONSUMMATES (two distinct but proper things)
  • Wholeness Math: 1 (teleios) + 1 (teleios) = 1 (one flesh)—NOT 0.5 + 0.5 (broken halves)
  • Conjugal duty (1 Cor 7:3-4): Bodies as covenant PROPERTY—fidelity is full participation, not just "not cheating"
  • Literacy before passion: Know design BEFORE practicing covenant expressions

Study Questions

  1. What is the "sexual literacy crisis" described in Chapter 2? (imagery everywhere, literacy nowhere)
  2. How does Gen 2:25 ("naked and unashamed") establish the original ATMOSPHERE of marriage?
  3. Define "sexual hedonism" CORRECTLY: devotion to pleasure as what? (stewardship, not "without consequences")
  4. Explain EPICURE ORIGINATIVENESS: Where does sexual pleasure originate? (Rev 4:11: "for thy pleasure they are created")
  5. What is ANTHROPOGENIC pleasure? (You're BORN sexual—Gen 1:27-28—inherent attributes vs. covenant practice)
  6. Explain the equation: Sex = Marriage (1 Cor 6:16). What biological reality does this create?
  7. Distinguish CERTIFICATE vs. COVENANT: Which one records? Which one consummates?
  8. What does "wholeness math" (1+1=1) require? (Two TELEIOS persons, not 0.5+0.5 broken halves)
  9. What is CONJUGAL DUTY (1 Cor 7:3-4)? (Bodies as covenant property—fidelity is full participation)
  10. Why must sexual literacy come BEFORE sexual passion? (Literacy → competency → covenant readiness)
  11. How has cultural messaging distorted DESIGN truth about sexuality?
  12. How do you develop sexual literacy as teleios formation (Heb 5:14)?

Scripture Memory (Design Canon)

  • Genesis 2:24-25 - Design Order: LEAVE (independence) + CLEAVE (commitment) + ONE FLESH (sexual union). "Naked and unashamed" = original marriage atmosphere (baseline, not spiritual ideal).
  • 1 Corinthians 6:16 - Sex = Marriage equation: "He who is joined to a prostitute becomes one body with her... 'The two will become one flesh.'" Biological reality, not metaphor.
  • 1 Corinthians 7:3-4 - Conjugal duty: "The husband should give to his wife her conjugal rights, and likewise the wife to her husband. For the wife does not have authority over her own body, but the husband does. Likewise the husband does not have authority over his own body, but the wife does." Bodies as covenant PROPERTY.
  • Revelation 4:11 - Epicure Originativeness: "For thy pleasure they are created"—pleasure originates in design.
  • Hebrews 5:14 - Teleios formation: "Solid food is for the mature (teleios), for those who have their powers of discernment trained by constant practice."
  • Hebrews 13:4 - Covenant honor: "Let marriage be held in honor among all, and let the marriage bed be undefiled."
