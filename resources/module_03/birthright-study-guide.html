🧬 Key Concept: Anthropogenic Sexuality

Definition:

Anthropogenic = From human origin/generation. You are sexual FROM BIRTH, not BECOMING sexual at puberty or through discovery.

Biblical Foundation:

"So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them." — Genesis 1:27 (KJV)

Key Distinctions:

  • ❌ Wrong: Sexuality is discovered at puberty or chosen through identity exploration
  • ✅ Correct: Sexuality is INHERITED at conception, observable at birth (male/female anatomy)
  • ❌ Wrong: Children are "asexual" until adolescence awakens sexuality
  • ✅ Correct: Children are sexual (male/female by design) without sexual DESIRE or activity

Practical Application:

Acknowledging birthright sexuality means:

  • Accepting your male or female design as created reality (not social construct)
  • Recognizing design precedes doctrine (body teaches before Bible does)
  • Understanding anatomy itself testifies to complementarity (lock-and-key)

🏛️ Three Stewardship Responsibilities

Responsibility Description Example
1. ACKNOWLEDGE Accept design reality as it is (not as culture redefines it) Recognize male/female complementarity in anatomy
2. PROTECT Guard design from violation, corruption, or misalignment Refuse pornography, adultery, sodomy, all porneia
3. APPLY Use design according to its intended purpose (covenant marriage) Sexual union within one-man/one-woman permanent covenant

⚠️ Warning: Stewardship Failure

Hosea 4:6: "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge..."

Literacy precedes morality. Without design-literacy, you cannot sort pleasure into ALIGNED vs MISALIGNED categories.

🔐 Anatomy as Participation

Lock-and-Key Complementarity:

Male and female anatomy demonstrates purposeful design:

  • Male anatomy (convex): Designed for penetration and seed delivery
  • Female anatomy (concave): Designed for reception and conception
  • Functional integration: One-flesh union (Genesis 2:24, 1 Cor 6:16)
  • Reproductive purpose: "Be fruitful and multiply" (Genesis 1:28)

Critical Distinction:

This is NOT symbolic metaphor, but literal reality.

The physical design of anatomy is EVIDENCE of participation—the body itself testifies to complementarity. This is observable, measurable, demonstrable reality (not religious interpretation).

One-Flesh Reality:

"What? know ye not that he which is joined to an harlot is one body? for two, saith he, shall be one flesh." — 1 Corinthians 6:16 (KJV)

The sexual act CREATES the one-flesh bond. Sex doesn't consummate marriage—it IS marriage. The certificate records what the act creates.

🌟 Pleasure Originates in Design (Not Danger)

Paradigm Shift:

Most sexual education is danger-focused (STDs, pregnancy risk, abuse prevention). Week 3 teaches design-focused literacy.

Why Design-First Literacy Matters:

  • Positive Framework: Teaches WHAT sexuality is FOR (covenant intimacy, reproduction, one-flesh union)
  • Sorting Wisdom: Provides ALIGNED vs MISALIGNED categories (not just "don't do this")
  • Freedom from Fear: Replaces shame-based prohibitions with design-based understanding
  • Epistemological Priority: Design precedes doctrine (Romans 1:20—creation reveals truth)

Key Principle: Literacy Precedes Morality

You cannot properly assess sexual behavior as right/wrong without first understanding design. Literacy (knowing what sexuality IS) must precede morality (knowing what to DO with it).

🧩 Personal Maturity Interlocks with Sexual Literacy

Four Attributes of Teleios Maturity:

  1. Behavioral Maturity: Consistent, reliable actions aligned with covenant commitments
  2. Temperamental Maturity: Emotional regulation, self-control, patience
  3. Emotional Maturity: Empathy, vulnerability, conflict resolution
  4. Mental Maturity: Critical thinking, wisdom, long-term planning

"Full Age" = Legal Age + Teleios Maturity:

UDHR Article 16:1: "Men and women of full age, without any limitation due to race, nationality or religion, have the right to marry and to found a family."

"Full age" does NOT mean only biological/legal age (18+). It means legal age PLUS:

  • Educational preparation
  • Economic readiness
  • Sexual literacy (design understanding)
  • Teleios maturity (all four attributes developed)
