📜 Genesis 1:27-28 (KJV) — Creation Design

"So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them. And God blessed them, and God said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth."

🔍 Verse-by-Verse Breakdown:

"God created man in his own image":

  • Imago Dei: Humanity uniquely bears God's image (not animals)
  • Design Precedes Doctrine: Physical creation reveals truth (Romans 1:20)
  • Purposeful Creation: Not random evolution; intentional design

"Male and female created he them":

  • Binary Complementarity: Two sexes, not spectrum or continuum
  • Anthropogenic Sexuality: Created male/female FROM BIRTH (not becoming)
  • Anatomical Distinction: Observable, measurable, biological reality
  • Functional Design: Male (convex) + female (concave) = complementarity

"Be fruitful, and multiply":

  • First Command: Reproduction is primary design purpose (not optional)
  • Reproductive Design: Sexuality oriented toward procreation
  • Covenant Context: One man + one woman = reproductive capacity
🎯 Design Principle #1: Sexuality is BIRTHRIGHT (anthropogenic), not choice, discovery, or social construct. You are created male or female by design.

📜 Genesis 2:24-25 (KJV) — One-Flesh Union

"Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh. And they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not ashamed."

🔍 Verse-by-Verse Breakdown:

"A man leave his father and his mother":

  • Public Departure: Marriage is public, not secret
  • New Household: Establishes independent family unit
  • Adult Maturity: Leaving requires teleios readiness (full age)
  • Social Recognition: Community witnesses the covenant

"And cleave unto his wife":

  • "Cleave" (Hebrew: דָּבַק dabaq): To cling, adhere, be joined permanently
  • Permanence: Not temporary, conditional, or trial relationship
  • Exclusivity: "His wife" (singular, not plural)
  • Covenant Bond: Binding commitment before sexual union

"And they shall be one flesh":

  • Sexual Union: Physical joining creates one-flesh reality
  • Ontological Change: Two become one (not merely symbolic)
  • Design Mechanism: Lock-and-key anatomy enables union
  • New Testament Confirmation: 1 Cor 6:16—"He which is joined...is one body"

"And were both naked...and were not ashamed":

  • Pre-Fall Innocence: No shame in design (before sin entered)
  • Design Goodness: Sexuality created "very good" (Genesis 1:31)
  • Covenant Context: Nakedness within marriage is unashamed
  • Shame Origin: Post-Fall (Genesis 3:7), not design itself
🎯 Design Principle #2: Sexual union (one-flesh) occurs within permanent, public, exclusive covenant. The act CREATES the bond (sex = marriage), and the certificate RECORDS it.

🏛️ Protological Principles (First Things)

Protology = study of origins/first things. These principles are foundational and non-negotiable.

1. Design Architecture Precedes Moral Law:

Romans 1:20: "For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made..."

Application: The body teaches before the Bible does. Anatomical complementarity is EVIDENCE, observable before reading scripture.

2. Binary Complementarity (Not Spectrum):

Genesis 1:27 establishes TWO sexes (male and female), not spectrum, continuum, or "gender fluidity." Intersex conditions (DSDs) are disorders, not third sexes.

3. One-Flesh Union Requires Covenant:

Genesis 2:24 sequence: Leave (public) → Cleave (covenant) → One flesh (sexual union). Sexual union OUTSIDE this sequence violates design (fornication/porneia).

4. Reproduction is Design Purpose:

"Be fruitful and multiply" (Genesis 1:28) is the FIRST command. Permanent sterilization with contraceptive intent rejects design purpose.

5. Shame is Post-Fall, Not Design:

"Not ashamed" (Genesis 2:25) shows sexuality itself is good. Shame entered through sin (Genesis 3:7), not through sexual design.

🔬 Three Witnesses to Design Reality

Week 3 teaches epistemological order: How we KNOW truth.

1. REVELATION (Genesis 1-2):

God's revealed word establishes design architecture. Genesis 1:27 ("male and female") is not poetic metaphor but created reality.

2. CREATION DESIGN (Romans 1:20):

"The invisible things...are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made..." Anatomy itself testifies—lock-and-key complementarity is OBSERVABLE, MEASURABLE evidence.

3. FRUIT/HISTORY (Matthew 7:16):

"Ye shall know them by their fruits." Societies that honor design (covenant marriage, binary sexuality) produce stability. Societies that reject design produce chaos (STDs, abortion, broken families).

🎯 Epistemological Priority: Design precedes doctrine. The body teaches before the Bible does. You can observe lock-and-key anatomy WITHOUT reading Genesis, but Genesis EXPLAINS what you observe.
