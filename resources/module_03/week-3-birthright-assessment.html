Part 1: True or False

10 questions | 2 points each | 20 points total

Question 1
Anthropogenic sexuality means you were BORN sexual by design (Genesis 1:27), not BECOMING sexual through puberty or cultural conditioning.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Anthropogenic (Greek: anthropos = human, genic = origin) sexuality means sexuality originates IN being human from birth. Genesis 1:27 "male and female created he them"—sexual identity is birthright, not choice.
Question 2
The three stewardship responsibilities are: (1) Acknowledge design, (2) Celebrate differences, (3) Share with others.
✅ Correct Answer: False
The three stewardship responsibilities are: (1) ACKNOWLEDGE design (accept reality), (2) PROTECT design (guard from violation), (3) APPLY design (live according to purpose). Not "celebrate" or "share."
Question 3
Lock-and-key anatomy (male convex/female concave) is literal participation evidence of complementarity, not merely symbolic metaphor.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Section 3.3 argues lock-and-key anatomy is NOT metaphor. Male anatomy fits precisely with female anatomy for reproductive function—observable design testifies to complementarity (Romans 1:20).
Question 4
"Full age" (UDHR Article 16:1) means reaching legal age of consent (18+) and biological puberty.
✅ Correct Answer: False
"Full age" = legal age PLUS teleios maturity (Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental), PLUS educational/economic readiness, PLUS sexual literacy. Legal age is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT.
Question 5
According to 1 Corinthians 6:16, the sexual act creates one-flesh reality; the marriage certificate records what sex accomplishes.
✅ Correct Answer: True
1 Cor 6:16: "He which is joined to an harlot is one body." Sex CREATES one-flesh reality. Chapter 3: "Sex doesn't consummate marriage—it IS marriage." Certificate records; sex consummates.
Question 6
Pleasure originates in design (Revelation 4:11 "for thy pleasure"), so sexual literacy should teach design-first, not danger-first.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Rev 4:11: "For thy pleasure they are and were created." Pleasure is foundational to design. Design literacy gives positive framework for sorting ALIGNED vs MISALIGNED, not just prohibition.
Question 7
Porneia (fornication) refers only to sex before marriage, while moicheia (adultery) refers to sex after marriage.
✅ Correct Answer: False
Porneia (G4202) is UMBRELLA term for ALL unlawful sexual relations (adultery, incest, sodomy, harlotry). Moicheia (G3430) is SPECIFIC: adultery between men/women. All adultery is porneia, but not all porneia is adultery.
Question 8
The four attributes of teleios maturity are: Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, and Mental development.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Section 3.5 and The Teleios Blueprint establish four attributes: (1) Behavioral (self-control), (2) Temperamental (emotional stability), (3) Emotional (empathy), (4) Mental (discernment). All required for covenant readiness.
Question 9
Nakedness in Genesis 2:25 ("not ashamed") was a spiritual concept, not a literal physical state.
✅ Correct Answer: False
Genesis 2:25 is LITERAL: "Both naked...not ashamed" describes actual physical state. Pre-Fall nakedness was default, not metaphor. Protological (first things) fact establishes design baseline.
Question 10
Hosea 4:6 ("My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge") applies to sexual literacy—ignorance leads to destruction in marriages and families.
✅ Correct Answer: True
Hosea 4:6 is the stewardship warning: "Destroyed for lack of knowledge." Applies directly to sexual literacy. Ignorance of design principles leads to covenant violations, broken families, and generational harm. Literacy precedes morality.

Part 2: Multiple Choice

15 questions | 3 points each | 45 points total

Question 11
What does "anthropogenic sexuality" mean?
✅ Correct Answer: B
Anthropogenic (anthropos = human, genic = origin) sexuality: Born sexual from conception. Genesis 1:27 "male and female created he them"—birthright, not choice or development. Inherent attribute, not acquired behavior.
Question 12
The three stewardship responsibilities for birthright design are:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Three responsibilities: (1) ACKNOWLEDGE design (accept male/female complementarity), (2) PROTECT design (guard from porneia), (3) APPLY design (live according to lock-and-key purpose). Section 3.2 framework.
Question 13
Lock-and-key anatomy demonstrates:
✅ Correct Answer: B
Section 3.3: Lock-and-key is NOT metaphor but observable reality. Male anatomy (external, penetrative) + female anatomy (internal, receptive) = functional complementarity. Design testifies to purposeful creation (Romans 1:20).
Question 14
"Full age" (UDHR Article 16:1) for marriage readiness means:
✅ Correct Answer: D
"Full age" = comprehensive readiness. Legal age (18+) is necessary but NOT sufficient. Must add: teleios maturity (Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental), educational preparation, economic stability, sexual literacy. Section 3.5.
Question 15
1 Corinthians 6:16 teaches that:
✅ Correct Answer: B
"He which is joined to an harlot is one body." If sex with harlot creates one-flesh, then sex IS the covenantal act (not certificate). Certificate records what sex accomplishes. Sex doesn't consummate marriage—it IS marriage.
Question 16
Revelation 4:11 ("for thy pleasure they are and were created") teaches that:
✅ Correct Answer: A
Rev 4:11 establishes pleasure as foundational to creation ("for thy pleasure"). Section 3.4: Literacy should teach design-first (ALIGNED vs MISALIGNED pleasure), not danger-first (fear/prohibition). Pleasure is gift; stewardship is responsibility.
Question 17
Porneia (Greek G4202) means:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Dake Study Bible (Matthew 5:32): Porneia = all manner of unlawful sexual relations. It's the umbrella term. Moicheia (adultery) is specific type. All adultery is porneia, but not all porneia is adultery (e.g., sodomy is porneia but not adultery).
Question 18
The four attributes of teleios maturity are necessary because:
✅ Correct Answer: B
Section 3.5: Sexual literacy and personal maturity are INSEPARABLE. Behavioral (self-control for fidelity), Temperamental (patience), Emotional (empathy/affection), Mental (doctrinal clarity). Without maturity, literacy becomes theory without practice.
Question 19
Genesis 2:25 ("naked and not ashamed") establishes:
✅ Correct Answer: A
Genesis 2:25 is protological (first things) FACT: nakedness was original design baseline. Shame entered at Genesis 3:7 (post-Fall). Nakedness + covenant = design; nakedness outside covenant = violation. Context determines alignment.
Question 20
Hosea 4:6 ("My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge") teaches that:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Hosea 4:6 is the stewardship warning: ignorance (lack of knowledge) leads to destruction. Sexual illiteracy causes covenant violations (adultery, pornography), weak families, societal decay. Literacy is prerequisite for moral action—design education protects.
Question 21
Romans 1:20 ("invisible things...clearly seen...by the things that are made") means:
✅ Correct Answer: B
Romans 1:20: Creation testifies to design ("by the things that are made"). Lock-and-key anatomy is observable evidence without Scripture. Design precedes doctrine—the body teaches before the Bible does. Three witnesses: Revelation, Creation, Fruit/History.
Question 22
ALIGNED stewardship of birthright design includes:
✅ Correct Answer: A
Section 3.6: ALIGNED stewardship = covenant marriage, one man/one woman, lock-and-key anatomy respected, reproductive design honored, permanence (no serial monogamy), nakedness protected (no pornography). MISALIGNED = porneia (adultery, fornication, sodomy).
Question 23
The Behavioral attribute of teleios maturity focuses on:
✅ Correct Answer: D
Four attributes: (1) Behavioral = self-control in ACTIONS (commitments, routines, finances), (2) Temperamental = stress/emotion management, (3) Emotional = empathy/vulnerability, (4) Mental = critical thinking/discernment. Behavioral is about what you DO.
Question 24
Genesis 1:27 ("male and female created he them") establishes:
✅ Correct Answer: C
Genesis 1:27: "Male and female" = BINARY (two sexes), COMPLEMENTARY (lock-and-key), BIRTHRIGHT (created he them), DESIGN-BASED (not cultural construct). First recorded truth about humanity is sexual identity. Protological foundation.
Question 25
The primary argument in Section 3.3 (Anatomy as Participation) is:
✅ Correct Answer: B
Section 3.3: "How am I a part of it? BY ANATOMY." Lock-and-key design is literal (not metaphor). Male convex/female concave testifies to complementarity. You participate in sexual epicure simply by being born male/female. Design precedes doctrine.
