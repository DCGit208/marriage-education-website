📌 Purpose: Assess Your Teleios Maturity

Week 3 Teaching Principle: "Full age" (UDHR 16:1) = legal age + teleios maturity. You cannot faithfully steward birthright sexuality without personal maturity.

Four Attributes Framework: Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental maturity (from The Teleios Blueprint course).

How to Use: Answer honestly (5-point scale for each question), calculate scores per attribute, identify growth areas, create 30-day strengthening plan.

Q3: I manage finances responsibly (budget, save, avoid debt).

Q4: I keep my word and can be trusted with sensitive information.

Q5: I complete tasks on time without procrastination.

Can you think critically, plan long-term, and make wise decisions?

Q16: I think critically (question assumptions, evaluate evidence).

Q17: I plan for the long-term (5-10 year vision).

Q18: I seek wisdom from mentors, books, and experience.

Q19: I make decisions based on principles, not impulses.

Q20: I learn from mistakes and adjust behavior accordingly.

MENTAL MATURITY SCORE: _____ / 25

📊 SCORING RUBRIC: Interpret Your Results

90-100 (TELEIOS READY): Covenant-ready maturity. You demonstrate consistent excellence across all four attributes. Continue maintaining this level.
70-89 (APPROACHING TELEIOS): Significant maturity with some growth areas. Identify weakest attribute and create targeted plan. 6-12 months intensive development recommended.
50-69 (DEVELOPING MATURITY): Moderate maturity with identified gaps. NOT covenant-ready yet. Focus on lowest-scoring attribute first. 12-24 months intentional growth needed. Consider The Teleios Blueprint course.
30-49 (IMMATURE): Significant immaturity across multiple attributes. Marriage would be premature and likely harmful. Seek mentorship immediately. 2-3 years intensive development required before covenant consideration.
0-29 (CRISIS IMMATURITY): Urgent need for intervention. You are NOT ready for covenant marriage and should not pursue relationships. Seek professional counseling and spiritual mentorship. Focus entirely on personal healing and growth.

⚠️ Important: Legal age (18+) is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT. "Full age" = legal + teleios (score 70+). Do not pursue marriage if scoring below 70.
