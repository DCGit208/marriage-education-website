Why Africa Needs Marriage Education Infrastructure

Traditional systems are failing. Modern challenges are accelerating.

The Crisis:

  • Sexual illiteracy: Most couples have never received practical sexual education
  • Porn epidemic: Smartphone access + cultural silence = silent marriage destruction
  • Financial conflict: No frameworks for money management within covenant
  • Communication breakdown: Cultural norms prevent honest marital dialogue
  • Divorce normalization: Even in Christian and traditional communities
  • Youth marriage aversion: Young Africans delay or avoid marriage due to witnessing dysfunction

The Opportunity:

Africa's family structure still values marriage—but urgently needs frameworks to make marriages work. Unlike Western contexts where marriage is often seen as optional, African cultural and religious contexts still prioritize covenant—creating ideal conditions for systematic marriage education adoption.
