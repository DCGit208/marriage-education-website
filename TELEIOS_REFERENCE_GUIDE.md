## FAQ FRAMEWORK

### Standard Teleios FAQs (Must Appear in Chapter 1)

**Q1: "If marriage requires wholeness, what if we're still growing?"**

**A:** Marriage does not require wholeness. **Being human requires wholeness.** Marriage presumes it.

Growth is lifelong, but **maturation** (full age - Hebrews 5:14) means you possess enough self-command, steadiness, empathy, and discernment to keep covenant. If these are missing, seek training NOW—before vows.

---

**Q2: "If I am already whole, why do I still need education and discipline?"**

**A:** Because **identity must be known, embodied, and stewarded.**

You are already complete in Christ (Colossians 2:10). Education doesn't create that reality—it **reveals** it. Discipline doesn't earn wholeness—it **expresses** it. 

The Teleios Program exists to awaken you to who you already are, not to make you into something you're not.

---

**Q3: "Isn't this just 'fake it till you make it'?"**

**A:** No. "Fake it till you make it" is performance anxiety—pretending to be what you're not until you earn it.

Teleios teaching is **identity declaration**—acting according to what you **already are** in Christ. You're not faking wholeness. You're remembering it.

Romans 3:24: "Being justified freely by his grace..." You ARE justified. Practice flows FROM that truth.

---

**Q4: "What if I don't feel whole?"**

**A:** Feelings follow truth. They don't determine it.

You may not *feel* whole because you've forgotten your identity. That's why education exists—to **reveal** what Christ secured. As you remember and practice FROM your justified identity, feelings align.

Feeling is not the foundation. **Being** is.
