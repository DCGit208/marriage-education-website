---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Trigger → pause → choose

Same chain as Course 10: body → premise → urge → act. Interrupt before act.

### 2) Pause practice (60–90 sec)

Breathe; name (Week 1 word); ask “design-aligned next act?”; then move.

### 3) 5-day challenge

Before any sharp reply, spend, exit, or accuse: pause once. Log successes and failures.

### 4) Analogy: brake

Speed without brakes is not freedom. Feeling without regulation is not honesty.

### 5) Better whole

Ask for help regulating; do not outsource the brake permanently.

### 6) Continuity

Regulated people meet conflict differently (Week 3).
