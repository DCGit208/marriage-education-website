## The Central Truth

**Your identity must be safe for covenant.**  
If your “I am” requires another person to complete, regulate, applaud, or excuse you, it is not ready for marriage weight. This week you integrate design identity, image/likeness practice, and false-identity vows into an **Identity Code** you can keep.

## Week Overview

### Core Question
Is my identity safe for covenant?

### Key Insight
Perfection/teleios is not a destination denied by “real life”; it is design-state. Covenant-safe identity means being mindful and educated, espousing that design, and living responsibly—without making a partner the manufacturer of personhood. Truth, fidelity, and repair are fruits, not the ontology.

- Write an 8-rule Covenant-Safe Identity Code

### 2) What “covenant-safe” means

Safe does **not** mean:

- finished healing  
- zero desire struggle  
- never needing support  

Safe **does** mean:

- you own your formation  
- you can repair without destroying the house  
- you do not ontologize wounds, lust, success, or loneliness  
- you can keep private fidelity when unseen  
- you can receive a partner as covenant witness, not as product  

### 3) The Covenant-Safe Identity Code (8 rules)

Write eight rules. Use this scaffold; customize language so it is yours:

1. **Design first** — I define myself from design, not from emotion of the hour.  
2. **Teleios state** — I am whole by design; activity does not manufacture maturity.  
3. **Image / holiness / likeness** — Image is given; holiness is state; likeness is formed living aligned to design.  
4. **No false masters** — I name and refuse my primary false identity daily.  
5. **Truth under pressure** — I tell the truth when silence would protect my brand.  
6. **Fidelity as structure** — Desire is governed; loyalty is not a mood.  
7. **Repair within 24 hours** — Impact owned; structural change named.  
8. **Better whole, not better half** — I will not use marriage or dating to manufacture my personhood.

Married learners may add a household line under rule 7. Unmarried learners keep rule 8 explicit in dating choices.

### 4) Stewardship vs self-obsession

Identity mastery is not endless self-focus. Stewardship means:

- enough self-knowledge to stop harming others  
- enough practice to keep promises  
- enough humility to remain mindful, educated, and accountable  

If identity work becomes a hobby that delays love’s duties, it has become another false identity: the “always becoming” professional student.

### 5) Bridge to Course 04

Course 04 addresses the four **capacities/faculties**: Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, and Affective. They are distinct from the Four Attributes. You enter ready when:

- design identity is practiced, not only quoted  
- primary false identity has a living vow  
- Identity Code exists and has been tested one week  

Do not enter 04 hoping activities will invent identity or capacities. The faculties are powers/capacities of the person; Course 04 educates you to recognize, espouse, and responsibly live them.

### 6) Sample Code (do not copy blindly — rewrite)

1. Design first — emotion informs; it does not name me.  
2. Teleios state — I am whole by design; practice does not manufacture me.  
3. Image/holiness/likeness — I receive image, recognize holiness as state, and align likeness through responsible living.  
4. No false masters — I refuse my primary false identity when triggered.  
5. Truth under pressure — I speak impact and ownership within 24 hours.  
6. Fidelity as structure — I govern desire; I do not audition for supply.  
7. Repair within 24 hours — intention is not enough; impact is owned.  
8. Better whole — I do not seek a missing half; marriage unites two whole persons in one flesh.

## Case Contrast

**Beautiful code, unchanged life.**  
Eight rules in a notes app. Same recruitment of partners. Same secrecy. Identity Mastery became content.

**Lived code.**  
Fewer words. Faster repairs. False identity named in real time. Dating/marriage stops being a rescue fantasy. The person is safer to covenant with — including for themselves.

## Daily Drills (7-day Code Week)

### Morning (10 min)
Read all 8 rules. Circle one rule to prove today. Speak Week 3 vow.

### Midday (3 min)
*Which rule is under pressure right now?* One micro-obedience.

### Evening (15 min)
- Rule proven  
- Rule broken  
- Repair  
- One line update to Code if wording was vague  

### Day 7
Finalize 8 rules. Write 30-day plan. Share Code with one witness if possible.
