## The Central Truth

**Your identity must be safe for covenant.**  
If your “I am” requires another person to complete, regulate, applaud, or excuse you, it is not ready for marriage weight. This week you integrate design identity, image/likeness practice, and false-identity vows into an **Identity Code** you can keep. Perfection/**teleios** is not a destination denied by “real life”; it is design-state. Practice does not manufacture being. A partner is better whole, not the manufacturer of personhood.

### 3) What “covenant-safe” means

Safe does **not** mean: finished healing; zero desire struggle; never needing support.

Safe **does** mean: you own your formation; you repair without destroying the house; you do not ontologize wounds, lust, success, or loneliness; you keep private fidelity when unseen; you receive a partner as covenant witness, not as product or missing half.

### 4) The Covenant-Safe Identity Code (8 rules)

Write eight rules. Use this scaffold; customize language so it is yours:

1. **Design first** — I define myself from design, not from emotion of the hour.  
2. **Teleios state** — I am whole by design; activity does not manufacture maturity.  
3. **Image / holiness / likeness** — Image is given; holiness is state; likeness is formed living aligned to design.  
4. **No false masters** — I name and refuse my primary false identity daily.  
5. **Truth under pressure** — I tell the truth when silence would protect my brand.  
6. **Fidelity as structure** — Desire is governed; loyalty is not a mood.  
7. **Repair within 24 hours** — Impact owned; structural change named.  
8. **Better whole, not better half** — I will not use marriage or dating to manufacture my personhood.

Married learners may add a household line under rule 7. Unmarried learners keep rule 8 explicit in dating choices.

### 5) Stewardship vs self-obsession

Identity mastery is not endless self-focus. Stewardship means enough self-knowledge to stop harming others; enough practice to keep promises; enough humility to remain mindful, educated, and accountable. If identity work becomes a hobby that delays love’s duties, it has become another false identity: the “always becoming” professional student — chase grammar in academic clothing.

### 6) Attributes ≠ Faculties (bridge hygiene)

Before Course 04:

| Layer | Canon list | Job |
|-------|------------|-----|
| Attributes | Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental | Personal development / personality |
| Capacities / Faculties | Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, Affective | Powers of the person |

Your Identity Code governs who you are and what you refuse. Course 04 educates stewardship of capacities. Do not enter 04 hoping activities invent identity, faculties, or teleios. The car drives because it is a car.

### 7) Bridge to Course 04

You enter ready when: design identity is practiced, not only quoted; primary false identity has a living vow; Identity Code exists and has been tested one week. Do not enter hoping Course 04 will invent the person Weeks 1–3 refused to invent.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Beautiful code, unchanged life
Eight rules in a notes app. Same recruitment of partners. Same secrecy. Identity Mastery became content. Fruit: vocabulary without trust.

### Case B — Code as self-hatred ladder
Rules used as evidence of worthlessness after every miss. Ontology collapse. Partner recruited as judge/regulator. Fruit: brittle house; teleios denied.

### Case C — Lived code from teleios
Fewer words. Faster repairs. False identity named in real time. Dating/marriage stops being a rescue fantasy. Explicit better whole. The person is safer to covenant with — including for themselves. Ready for Course 04 as practitioner.

## Seven-Day Drill — Code Week

| Day | Rule circled | Micro-obedience | Rule broken? | Repair | Better-half temptation? |
|-----|--------------|-----------------|--------------|--------|-------------------------|

**Morning (10 min):** Read all 8 rules. Circle one to prove today. Speak Week 3 vow.  
**Midday (3 min):** *Which rule is under pressure?* One micro-obedience.  
**Evening (15 min):** Rule proven · rule broken · repair · one line update if wording was vague.  
**Day 7:** Finalize 8 rules. Write 30-day plan. Share Code with one witness if possible.
