# Week 2: Image & Likeness
## Matching Confession to Character

> **Week 2 of 4** | 7–9 hours | Formation Level  
> **Sources:** Gen 1:26–28 · Eph 4:22–24 · Col 3:10 · 1 Pet 1:16 · Course 02 Week 2  
> **Rule:** Image is given; holiness is state; likeness is formed. Teleios is not manufactured by activity.

---

## The Central Truth

**Many people claim image while refusing likeness.**  
They confess dignity and design — then live without character that agrees with the confession. Identity Mastery addresses that gap: **become mindful and educated, espouse who you are by design, and live accordingly**. You are **teleios** by design; put off / put on does not invent maturity — it aligns expression with what is already true.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
Where do I claim “image” but refuse “likeness”?

### Key Insight
Image without likeness is design unespoused. Likeness talk without image is performance theater. Teleios identity holds the order: receive image; recognize holiness as state; form likeness by responsible living. Practice does not create maturity. Better whole forbids using a partner as the manufacturer of character you refuse to put on.

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this week you will:

- Restate image vs likeness with personal examples and teleios grammar
- Locate one confession–character gap with four-column costs
- Run a 7-day put off / put on loop tied to that gap
- Update three testable design statements from Course 02 / Week 1
- Refuse better-half expectations while confessing teleios wholeness
- Complete a Confession–Character Gap Audit under load

## Core Teaching

### 1) Quick frame (from Course 02 — now applied)

| | Image | Likeness |
|--|-------|----------|
| Question | What kind of being am I? | Does my formed character agree with who I am? |
| Status | Given | Formed |
| Verb | Receive | Align / live accordingly |
| Failure | Self-erasure / self-creation | Performance without practice |
| Relation to teleios | Design-state given | Expression agreeing with design-state |

If Course 02 was tourism, Week 2 is the exam. Attributes (Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental) and Faculties (Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, Affective) serve likeness; they do not invent image.

### 2) Teleios grammar for the gap

| Chase / manufacture grammar | Practice-from-teleios grammar |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------|
| “I will become holy when my streak is clean” | “Holiness is state; today likeness agrees” |
| “Drills make me a real person” | “Drills express responsible personhood” |
| “My spouse will form my character” | “Better whole — I own my put-on” |
| Shame after miss = new ontology | Miss = repair without identity deletion |

### 3) The confession–character gap

A confession–character gap looks like:

- “I am a person of truth” + habitual image management  
- “I am designed for fidelity” + secret lust identity  
- “I am responsible” + unfinished promises  
- “I am whole by design” + still recruiting a “better half” to manufacture completeness  

The gap is not fixed by better slogans. Education and accountable living expose whether likeness agrees with design. They do not manufacture teleios.

### 4) Put off / renew / put on (operating system)

| Step | Prompt | Output |
|------|--------|--------|
| Put off | What unlike pattern am I refusing today? | Named behavior |
| Renew mind | What design truth replaces the lie? | One sentence (teleios-safe) |
| Put on | What likeness act will I execute? | Measurable act + time |

Example:

- Put off: scorekeeping in conflict  
- Renew: “Repair is fruit of likeness and responsibility; I am teleios — punishment-as-weapon refuses design.”  
- Put on: one ownership sentence within 24 hours  

### 5) Sexed design without derail

Image includes male/female creation order (Gen 1:26–28). Likeness includes stewarding that design without cultural preference rewriting ontology. Fashion is not the enemy; self-invention is. Full masculinity/femininity lanes deepen later. Do not treat sexed design as optional décor.

### 6) Holiness = state, not achievement

“Be ye holy; for I am holy” (1 Pet 1:16) uses **be** as state/identity language, not “become” as a performance ladder. Holiness is not trained into existence. Likeness is the formed character through which life agrees with image and holy design-state. Misery is not the manufacturing plant of holiness.

### 7) Design statements — upgraded

From Course 02 / Week 1 lines, lock three testable statements:

1. “I am designed to …”  
2. “I am responsible for …”  
3. “In likeness, I am aligning my life to …”  

Each must be falsifiable this week. If you cannot fail the statement by inaction, it is too vague. Include teleios and better whole where romance/completion grammar still hides.

### 8) Domains where gaps hide

| Domain | Common claim | Common refusal |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Speech | “I am honest” | Soft lies / image management |
| Desire | “I am faithful” | Private indulgence |
| Time | “I am responsible” | Unfinished promises |
| Conflict | “I am peacemaking” | Silent punishment |
| Body | “I steward my design” | Neglect or exhibition as identity |
| Money | “I am a steward” | Status spending as selfhood |
| Romance | “I am whole” | Better-half / complete-me bargains |

Your Week 2 gap is usually here — not in abstract theology.

### 9) Ratiocination

- Premise 1: Image is given; likeness is formed alignment.  
- Premise 2: Activity that treats likeness as ontology-manufacture reinstalls chase.  
- Premise 3: Claiming teleios while recruiting a better half is contradictory anthropology.  
- Conclusion: Close one gap this week without pretending drills invent the person.

### 10) Better whole in likeness practice

Using a spouse or dating prospect as the put-on you refuse (“they’ll make me faithful / honest / present”) is better-half anthropology. Support and witness are good; substitution is not. Likeness is your responsibility as a teleios person.

### 11) Common errors this week

1. Re-studying Course 02 instead of drilling one gap  
2. Vague put-ons (“be better”) instead of timed acts  
3. Using likeness language to shame a spouse — your gap first  
4. Treating male/female design as optional  
5. Treating holiness or maturity as something drills manufacture  
6. Keeping “better half / complete me” expectations while confessing teleios  
7. Confusing Attributes with Faculties when naming the gap

### 12) Language discipline

- “I’m becoming a better person through this drill.” → “I am teleios; this drill aligns likeness.”  
- “I’m broken until I close the gap.” → “The gap is real; my ontology is not trash.”  
- “When they change, I’ll match my confession.” → “Better whole — I put on today.”

### 13) Continuity into Week 3

Matched confession without naming false identities leaves the old master rehearsing in secret. Week 3 names the lie and installs a replacement vow.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Image rhetoric
Fluent Genesis language. Soft life. No repair. Identity stays inspirational. Partner is quietly cast as the one who will “make me consistent.” Fruit: vocabulary without household safety.

### Case B — Likeness as self-hatred
Closes gaps with contempt fuel: “I’m trash until the streak is clean.” Ontology collapse. Misses become identity deletion. Fruit: brittle holiness; household eggshells; teleios denied in practice.

### Case C — Likeness practice from teleios
Fewer slogans. Daily put on. After conflict, ownership arrives before bedtime. Explicit: “I am teleios; this act agrees with design; my spouse is better whole, not my character factory.” Fruit: the house feels safer before the vocabulary impresses anyone.

## Seven-Day Drill — Put Off / Put On Loop

| Day | Put off | Renew (teleios sentence) | Put on (act + time) | Evidence | Repair? |
|-----|---------|--------------------------|---------------------|----------|---------|

**Morning (10 min):** Read three design statements; write put off / renew / put on for today’s primary gap.  
**Midday (2 min):** *Am I living the claim or performing it?*  
**Evening (12 min):** Evidence for likeness · evidence of gap · repair for tomorrow.  
**Day 7:** Rewrite the three statements so they match what you practiced — then raise the bar one notch for Week 3. Witness review if possible.
