## Course thesis

The faculties are the four **capacities/powers of the person**:

1. **Conative** — commitment / striving action  
2. **Cognitive** — learning and reasoning  
3. **Psychomotor** — physical and mental agility  
4. **Affective** — care: forethought and empathy  

They are not the Four Attributes. Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, and Mental belong to personal development/personality (Course 02 Week 3). Course 04 educates the learner to recognize, espouse, and responsibly live the four capacities without teaching that activity manufactures teleios.

---

## Doctrine guardrails

- Teleios/maturity = design-state / full development; practice does not manufacture it.
- The car drives because it is a car; driving does not make it a car.
- Formation path: **mindful → educated → espouse design → live accordingly**.
- Image is given; holiness is state; likeness is formed alignment to design.
- Better whole, not better half.
- Capacities/faculties are distinct from attributes and skills.
- Course title retains “Training,” but training means education and responsible stewardship—not creation of the faculties.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of five weeks, the learner will:

1. Name and define the Four Faculties from the PD map.
2. Distinguish faculties from attributes and skills.
3. Reject the doctrine that practice creates maturity or capacities.
4. Audit responsible expression of each faculty.
5. Write a Four Faculties Stewardship Charter and 30-day plan.
