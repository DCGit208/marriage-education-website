## The Central Truth

Secure relatedness is the root of relational **stability**.  
“Attachment style” language in this Intensive means **pattern under load** — not a new ontology (“I am anxious / avoidant as my being”). You are **teleios** by design; patterns are stewardable data, not baptismal names.

Anchor and rope: stability is tied, not floating. The ship is still a ship by design. Anchoring does not invent the vessel — practice does not manufacture being. You and the other remain **better wholes**; co-regulation is care, not ontology supply.

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do we map stability patterns without baptizing them as identity — and without using the other as our only anchor?

### Key Insight
Insecurity often shows as protest (pursue), distance (withdraw), or chaos (disorganized swings). Mapping is for rails — not for permanent labels that excuse harm or demand the partner become a human sedative. Week 1’s covenant stone requires Week 2’s living rope, or vows remain speech.

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this week you will:

- Map your primary stability pattern under relational load without identity baptism
- Write one insecurity zone with cue → body → story → move → cost
- Install a daily stability ritual that names teleios and better whole
- Run a 5-day attachment check-in with honest pattern labels
- Complete an Attachment & Stability Audit under load, not branding
- Share one pattern with partner or witness without fusion demand

## Core Teaching

### 1) Pattern map (descriptive, not ontological)

| Pattern | Under threat I tend to… | Design counter |
|---------|-------------------------|----------------|
| Protest / pursue | Escalate contact, protest, flood | Pause + clear ask + return-time |
| Distance / withdraw | Shut down, exit, go cold | Honesty + return-time kept |
| Chaotic swing | Both extremes in short cycles | MDS + witness (Course 07) |
| Steady / secure-ish | Stay present, repair | Strengthen on purpose — do not coast |

You may blend. Name primary under fatigue. The map is Cognitive honesty, not a personality religion.

### 2) Illegal ontology sentences (catch and rewrite)

- “I am anxious attachment.” → “Under load I protest; I will steward a pause-and-ask.”  
- “I’m just avoidant — that’s who I am.” → “I withdraw; I will name return-time and keep it.”  
- “My style requires you to never leave the room.” → “I am teleios; I ask for help as a better whole, not as a half.”

Attachment vocabulary is allowed as **pattern language**, forbidden as being-language. Course 11 preview: you do not become a person by securing a style — you already are one.

### 3) Ratiocination (non-negotiable)

- Premise 1: Teleios design-state precedes relational practice; practice aligns expression.
- Premise 2: Stability patterns are habits under threat — observable and trainable.
- Premise 3: Pattern-as-ontology excuses harm or fuses the other into regulator.
- Conclusion: Map for stewardship; refuse identity baptism; keep better whole.

### 5) Stability ritual (daily — under 4 minutes)

1. Morning: “I am teleios; today I will ______ for stability. I will not recruit ______ to complete my being.”  
2. Midday: 60-second body check (Psychomotor) — jaw, chest, gut.  
3. Evening: Did I pursue / withdraw / chaos? One sentence. Repair if needed same day when possible.

Ritual without honesty is branding. Honesty without ritual is weather report.

### 7) Analogy: anchor and rope

The rope is practice — check-ins, return-times, clear asks, witness. The ship is teleios by design. Anchoring does not invent the vessel; cutting the rope and calling it “freedom” is not maturity. Floating identity (“whoever I feel today”) sinks covenant from Week 1.

### 8) Married track and unmarried track

**Married:** Share pattern without demanding spouse become sole regulator. Agree return-times. Use governance meeting (Course 09) to review the 5-day log once.

**Unmarried:** Map patterns before fusion accelerates. Do not use dating intensity to baptize protest as “passion” or withdrawal as “mystery.” Better whole dating means: clear asks, paced contact, no ontology panic when the other has a life.

Both tracks: co-regulate as care; refuse better-half regulation.

### 9) Continuity: Week 1 → Week 2 → Week 3

Covenant without stability is speech. Stability without Week 3 regulation snaps under Emotional surge. Courses 08–09 already taught return-time; this week makes them intensive: daily, logged, identity-clean.

### 10) Partner / witness practice (script)

> “Under load my primary pattern is ______. I am not asking you to become my ontology. I am asking for ______ (specific care) while I keep my own rails. I remain a better whole; so do you.”

### 11) Evidence bar — School 1 greatness

Evidence: pattern map without “I am my style”; insecurity zone with premise rewrite; ritual kept majority of days; 5-day log; one share without regulator demand. Labels that block repentance are counterfeit literacy.

### 12) Protest as “love” and distance as “peace”

Protest baptized as passion floods and calls it depth. Distance baptized as maturity starves and calls it wisdom. Design: clear ask + presence + return-time. Intensity is not covenant proof; steadiness under load is.

### 13) What this week is not

Not therapy cosplay as identity. Not anti-emotion. Not “never need anyone.” Not permission to abandon when uncomfortable. It is anti-**pattern-as-fate** and anti-**partner-as-sedative**.

### 14) Preview of Course 11

Practice-from-teleios already answers here: you anchor because you are a designed person under load — not because securing a style will finally make you real.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Label as fate
Alex says “I’m avoidant” and skips repair, return-time, and honesty. Courses 08–09 tools gather dust. Fruit: loneliness with psychological vocabulary; partner/date becomes the villain for needing contact.

### Case B — Protest as love
Mira floods, monitors, and escalates; calls it passion and “secure wanting.” The other is recruited as regulator. Fruit: exhaustion, fake intimacy, better-half fusion.

### Case C — Anchored stewardship
Sam maps pattern, keeps ritual five days, uses return-time, shares without demanding ontology supply, and repairs protest/withdraw same day when possible. Fruit: steadiness that serves Week 1 covenant.
