## The Central Truth

Secure relatedness is the root of relational **stability**.  
“Attachment style” language here means **pattern under load** — not a new ontology (“I am anxious/avoidant as my being”). You are teleios by design; patterns are stewardable.

Anchor and rope: stability is tied, not floating.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do we map stability patterns without baptizing them as identity?

### Key Insight
Insecurity often shows as protest (pursue), distance (withdraw), or chaos (disorganized moves). Mapping is for rails — not for permanent labels that excuse harm.

### Learning Outcomes
- Map your primary stability pattern under relational load  
- Identify one insecurity zone with cues  
- Install a daily stability ritual  
- Run a 5-day attachment check-in  
- Pass an Attachment & Stability Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Pattern map (descriptive, not ontological)

| Pattern | Under threat I tend to… | Design counter |
|---------|-------------------------|----------------|
| Protest / pursue | Escalate contact, protest | Pause + clear ask |
| Distance / withdraw | Shut down, exit | Return-time + honesty |
| Chaotic swing | Both extremes | MDS + witness (Course 07) |
| Steady / secure-ish | Stay present, repair | Strengthen on purpose |

You may blend. Name primary under fatigue.

### 2) Insecurity zone

Write: cue → body signal → story (Mental premise) → move → cost.  
Illegal premise example: “Distance means I am worthless” (ontology collapse).

### 3) Stability ritual (daily)

1. Morning: “I am teleios; today I will ______ for stability.”  
2. Midday: 60-second body check (Psychomotor)  
3. Evening: Did I pursue/withdraw/chaos? Repair if needed  

### 4) 5-day check-in log

| Day | Load 1–5 | Pattern | Ritual kept? | Repair? |
|-----|----------|---------|--------------|---------|

### 5) Analogy: anchor and rope

The rope is practice (check-ins, return-times). The ship is still a ship by design. Anchoring does not invent the vessel.

### 6) Partner practice

If bonded: share pattern without demanding the other become your regulator. Better whole: co-regulate as care, not as ontology supply.

### 7) Continuity

Stability without emotional regulation (Week 3) snaps when Affective/Emotional surge hits. Install thermostat next.
