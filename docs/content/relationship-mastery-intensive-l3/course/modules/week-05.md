## Core Teaching

### 1) Conflict is not failure of covenant

Finite persons under load will collide on timing, tone, money, sex, parenting, in-laws, devices, and dreams. Course 09 already named relatedness types and governance. Level 3 mastery refuses the fantasy that “good couples don’t fight.” Good couples fight with form. Accidental relatedness fights with archives, volume, and character assassination.

Conflict without protocol reverts to contract reflex: win, punish, or exit. Covenant mindset (Week 1) without conflict rails is speech. Install both.

### 2) Signature errors (name two)

Common Level 3 still-repeating moves: contempt openers; archive ammunition; exits without return time; refusing to own 10% first; winning the point while losing the person; volume/sarcasm; triangulation; motive prosecution; devices in heat; fake pause (stonewall with no return).

For your two errors, write four cost columns: Body · Bond · Work/calling · Inner life. If you cannot name costs, you are still romanticizing chaos as “passion” or “honesty.”

### 3) Martial skill metaphor (and its limit)

White belt flails when struck. Black belt uses form under adrenaline. Form is love’s technique — not coldness. The analogy limit: your partner is not an enemy to defeat. Protocol is how teleios persons steward force so power serves design rather than ego.

### 4) Conflict protocol (household card)

Write this on one card both can see:

1. **Cue** — raised voice / shutdown / contempt word / heart-rate spike  
2. **Pause** — X minutes; who says the code word (one word, no speech)  
3. **Return** — exact time (not “later”; not “when I cool off forever”)  
4. **Own** — each names their 10%+ without counterattack first  
5. **Mirror** — one paraphrase each (Week 4 radio)  
6. **Ask** — one clear request each (not a lecture)  
7. **Repair** — next act + time (feeds Week 6 structure)  
8. **If stuck** — table to governance meeting (Course 09 W6); do not litigate until 2 a.m.

Protocol without return time is abandonment. Return without ownership is performance. Ownership without next act is fog.

### 5) Pause — own — repair (sequence)

- **Pause** without abandonment — body leaves the escalation loop; vow stays.  
- **Own** without fog — specific act and impact; no “I’m trash” ontology deletion (Course 11 grammar already forbids this; practice it early).  
- **Repair** without bribery gifts that skip ownership — next rail, not distraction flowers-as-absolution.

Emotional regulation (Week 3) is the brake; mirror listening (Week 4) is the radio; this week is martial form when both are tested.

### 6) Pattern governance, not mood management

Mastery governs **patterns**, not one-off moods. Ask: What is the repeating sequence? Who escalates first? Who exits without return? Who brings archives? Pattern cards beat “we’ll try harder” wishes. Better wholes refuse to treat the other’s nervous system as their permanent regulator — and refuse to outsource their own pause to the other’s calm.

### 7) Power submits to protocol (Course 09 W3)

Protocol fails if the powerful party skips it. Money, status, rhetorical skill, emotional volume, or “I’m the spiritual one” can all become exemptions. Mastery includes submitting your power to the card. If only the weaker party must follow protocol, you have a tyranny manual, not a rule of love.

### 8) Ratiocination (non-negotiable)

- Premise 1: Covenant-grade bonds face real conflict under load.  
- Premise 2: Load without protocol reverts to reaction, archives, and power theater.  
- Premise 3: Teleios persons steward force with form; better wholes refuse fusion and contempt as “intimacy.”  
- Conclusion: Conflict mastery is pause–own–repair under fire — optional skill made obligatory by covenant seriousness.

### 10) What this week is not

Not permission for cruelty as “honesty.”  
Not endless processing without decisions.  
Not endurance of abuse without wisdom and moral limits (Week 1 guardrail still holds).  
Not winning-as-mastery.  
Not manufacturing a new self through conflict — you are already teleios; you are aligning expression.

### 11) Language under fire + continuity

Catch and rewrite war-talk (“You always…”, exit-as-weapon, motive prosecution). Mental architecture rebuilds accidental relatedness overnight if left sloppy.

Protocol creates ruptures that still need Week 6 structure — pause well and you can still carry revenge debt. Bridge repair: structural release ≠ trust bricks ≠ fog feelings.

Fusion-fighting says disagreement is abandonment. Better-whole conflict says two image-bearers can mirror different Cognitive accounts and keep covenant without ontology completion mid-argument.

If reaction wins most evenings, stay until the card is believable under fatigue — not only on a calm Sunday.
