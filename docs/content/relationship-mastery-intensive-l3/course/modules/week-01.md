# Week 1: Covenant Mindset
## Design Commitment — Not Convenience Contract

> **Week 1 of 8** | 8–10 hours | Foundation Stone  
> **Sources:** Malachi 2:14 · Course 08–09 · better whole · UDHR 16 frame

---

## The Central Truth

Covenant is a **design commitment**, not a contract of convenience.  
Contracts optimize exit and exchange. Covenant binds persons under truth for a purpose larger than mood. The foundation stone determines whether the house stands.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
What distinguishes covenant mindset from contract mindset?

### Key Insight
Many people use covenant words with contract reflexes: stay while useful, leave when costly, renegotiate identity midstream. Mastery begins by naming the difference and writing non-negotiables.

### Learning Outcomes
- Define covenant vs contract in design language  
- List covenant non-negotiables for your key bond track  
- Identify where you still run contract mindset  
- Write a Covenant Vow  
- Pass a Covenant Mindset Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Definitions

| | Contract | Covenant |
|--|----------|----------|
| Core | Exchange + exit options | Binding under design purpose |
| Motive | Advantage / comfort | Fidelity + fruit |
| When hard | Renegotiate or leave | Repair and keep faith (wisdom intact) |
| Person | Counterparty | Image-bearer under vow |

Covenant does **not** mean endure abuse without wisdom. Design includes moral limits; fidelity is not a suicide pact.

### 2) Non-negotiables (write 5–7)

Examples: truth-telling; sexual loyalty (if marriage track); no contempt as habit; repair within X; governance meeting cadence; better-whole (no fusion demand).

### 3) Contract mindset tells

- “I’ll keep vows while they make me happy”  
- Secret exit fantasies as primary coping  
- Scorekeeping as justice  
- Using “covenant” to trap the other without your own rails  

### 4) Covenant Vow template

> Before God and design truth, I commit to ______ as ______.  
> My non-negotiables are ______.  
> When load rises I will ______, not ______.  
> I will not recruit this person to complete my ontology.  
> I espouse design and live accordingly.

### 5) Analogy: foundation stone

Everything rests here. Fancy conflict skills on a convenience foundation still crack.

### 6) Better whole

Covenant joins wholes. It does not fuse halves into one incomplete organism.

### 7) Continuity

Covenant mindset without stability practices (Week 2) is speech. Install both.
