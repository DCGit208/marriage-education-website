# Week 8: Relationship Rulebook
## Rules Create Freedom — Wishes Create Drift

> **Week 8 of 8** | 9–11 hours | Capstone  
> **Sources:** Weeks 1–7 · Course 09 W6 · Proverbs 24:3

---

## The Central Truth

Every great relationship is governed by **rules**, not wishes.  
A rulebook is order that creates freedom to love without constant renegotiation. Wishes are private hopes with no calendar.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
What belongs in a living relationship rulebook?

### Key Insight
Five clear rules beat twenty forgotten ones. Write them, share with a partner (or witness), commit to one rule this week with weekly review — Intensive becomes lifestyle.

### Learning Outcomes
- Write a Relationship Rulebook (5 rules minimum)  
- Share it with a partner or wise witness  
- Commit to one rule with evidence this week  
- Install weekly rule review  
- Complete Level 3 closing charter

### 2) Five rules (examples — write yours)

1. We tell hard truth within 24 hours — no silent wars  
2. Conflict: code word → pause → return time  
3. Phones away for dinner and the intimacy ritual  
4. Repair ritual before sleep when possible; else scheduled within 24h  
5. Weekly 45-minute governance meeting  

### 3) Share and consent

Rules that only one party wrote are decrees. Mastery seeks mutual adoption for adult bonds. Unilateral rules still govern *your* side (what you will/won’t do).

### 4) One rule this week

Pick the highest-leverage rule. Log daily Y/N. Review Sunday.

### 5) Weekly review forever (minimum 30 days)

10–15 minutes: Which rules held? Which drifted? One adjust?

### 6) Analogy: rulebook

Games without rules are brawls. Love without rules becomes accidental relatedness again (Course 09 W1).

### 7) Better whole

Rulebooks serve two wholes. They are not parental control scripts for a spouse-as-child.

### 8) Final ratiocination

- Premise 1: Covenant-grade bonds face load.  
- Premise 2: Load without rules reverts to contract reflex and pattern chaos.  
- Premise 3: A short living rulebook + review sustains mastery.  
- Conclusion: Order is freedom to keep faith.

### 9) Closing charter

Integrate: covenant vow, stability pattern, regulation, communication, conflict protocol, forgiveness process, presence ritual, five rules + review cadence.

### 10) Continuity

School 1 continues (Perfect Self, Emotional Literacy, Temperament). Carry the rulebook forward; do not shelf it with the certificate.
