## The Central Truth

Every great relationship is governed by **rules**, not wishes.  
A rulebook is order that creates freedom to love without constant renegotiation. Wishes are private hopes with no calendar. You remain **teleios** by design; rules steward expression under load — they do not manufacture being. Two **better wholes** keep a short living rulebook under weekly review so Intensive becomes lifestyle, not a certificate on a shelf.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Rules create freedom (doctrine)

Games without rules are brawls. Love without rules becomes accidental relatedness again (Course 09 W1). Order is not tyranny when it serves design purpose: truth, repair, presence, and covenant fidelity. Wish-only relatedness says “we’ll see how we feel.” Mastery says “we already decided how we will love when we don’t feel like it.”

### 2) Intensive map → rule sources

| Week | Feeds rule on… |
|------|----------------|
| 1 | Covenant non-negotiables / vow language |
| 2 | Stability / return-time / attachment pattern under design |
| 3 | Emotional pause protocol (Emotional attribute brake) |
| 4 | Mirror listening / no broadcast combat |
| 5 | Conflict card: pause–own–repair; power submits |
| 6 | Forgiveness ≠ trust ≠ fog; mend cadence |
| 7 | Presence / devices / intimacy ritual |

Your five rules must show this integration — not five random preferences.

### 3) Five rules (examples — write yours)

1. We tell hard truth within 24 hours — no silent wars.  
2. Conflict: code word → pause → return time → own 10% → mirror → one ask → next act.  
3. Phones away for dinner and the intimacy ritual.  
4. Repair ritual before sleep when possible; else scheduled within 24h; trust bricks separate from forgiveness speech.  
5. Weekly 45-minute governance meeting (Course 09 W6 cadence).  

Add a sixth if needed — still prefer short and kept over long and ignored.

### 4) Rule quality checklist

Each rule must be:

- **Observable** — a stranger could tell if it happened  
- **Timed** — when / how often / return time  
- **Owned** — who initiates; what happens if missed  
- **Type-honoring** — married ≠ dating ≠ friendship scripts  
- **Better-whole safe** — not parental control of a spouse-as-child  
- **Teleios-safe** — miss → repair, not identity deletion or worth-panic

### 5) Share and consent

Rules that only one party wrote are decrees. Mastery seeks mutual adoption for adult bonds. Unilateral rules still govern *your* side (what you will/won’t do) — especially for singles or asymmetric readiness. Do not baptize control as “spiritual headship” or “boundaries” when it is fear of equal agency.

### 6) One rule this week (highest leverage)

Pick the rule that would have saved the most pain in the last 90 days. Log daily Y/N. Review Sunday. Evidence beats intention. If you cannot keep one rule for seven days, do not pretend a forty-rule manual is maturity.

### 7) Weekly review forever (minimum 30 days on calendar)

10–15 minutes:

1. Which rules held?  
2. Which drifted?  
3. One adjust (tighten, clarify, or retire a dead rule)?  
4. Any backlog/repair item from Week 6 still open?  
5. Presence ritual (Week 7) — kept?

Review is governance, not court. Contempt in review violates Week 1–5. Better wholes audit patterns, not souls.

### 8) Analogy: rulebook

A sport without a rulebook is not freedom — it is injury waiting. A household without a rulebook is constant renegotiation under fatigue. Analogy limit: love is not a sport to win; rules serve persons, not the reverse.

### 9) Ratiocination (non-negotiable)

- Premise 1: Covenant-grade bonds face load.  
- Premise 2: Load without rules reverts to contract reflex and pattern chaos.  
- Premise 3: A short living rulebook + weekly review sustains mastery for teleios persons living as better wholes.  
- Conclusion: Order is freedom to keep faith — Intensive ends; mastery continues.

### 10) What this week is not

Not a tyranny manual (40 rules, zero mutuality).  
Not wish lists without calendars.  
Not shelving the Intensive with a certificate selfie.  
Not using rules to complete ontology or fuse identities.  
Not replacing wisdom in abuse/moral-limit cases with “just keep the rules.”

### 11) Capstone charter requirements (Level 3 — required)

Your closing charter (800–1,200 words) **must** include:

1. **Covenant mindset** — vow / non-negotiables (Week 1)  
2. **Stability pattern** — return-time / attachment-under-design note (Week 2)  
3. **Emotional regulation** — pause card / Emotional attribute brake (Week 3)  
4. **Communication** — mirror listening commitment (Week 4)  
5. **Conflict protocol** — household card summary (Week 5)  
6. **Forgiveness process** — release ≠ trust ≠ fog (Week 6)  
7. **Presence ritual** — device rule + type limits (Week 7)  
8. **Five+ rules** + weekly review cadence (Week 8)  
9. **Teleios sentence** — practice from design; misses get repair, not identity deletion  
10. **Better whole sentence** — no fusion completion; partner/friend is not ontology  
11. **Weakest week + 30-day rail** — honest, not branded  
12. **Witness** — who heard the charter; date of first review meeting  

Missing any of 1–12 = unfinished Course 10. Polish without these is poster mastery.

### 12) Continuity into School 1 next courses

Perfect Self (Course 11), Emotional Literacy (Course 12), Temperament Intelligence (Course 13) assume this rulebook lives. Carry it forward. Do not restart from accidental relatedness after the Intensive glow fades.

### 13) Language, power, and greatness

Rewrite wish-talk (“we’ll just know”), anti-order romance myths, and “I missed a rule so I am trash.” Teleios persons repair misses; better wholes refuse fusion-by-rules.

As in Week 5: the powerful party must submit to review. One-sided audit is a control script. Unilateral self-rules still bind *you* without pretending the other consented.

Show a short living rulebook, one evidenced rule, and a kitchen-real charter. If Day 30 has no review on the calendar, the Intensive was tourism.
