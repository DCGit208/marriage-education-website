## The Central Truth

Intimacy is built by **presence**, not by pressure.  
Candlelight is intentional. Phones, hurry, and performance sex/emotion are counterfeits. Full attention is the Psychomotor and Affective gift of a whole person.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How is intimacy formed without pressure or fusion?

### Key Insight
Barriers to presence are usually concrete: devices, unresolved backlog, fear, pornography formation, hustle identity. Name barriers, install an intimacy ritual, run a 7-day no-phone dinner (or equivalent presence block).

### Learning Outcomes
- Identify barriers to presence  
- Create an intimacy ritual (fit to bond type)

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Intimacy defined

**Intimacy** = fitting closeness of knowledge, affection, and (where covenant) body — under type limits.  
Not: forced vulnerability theater. Not: unlimited access as proof of love.

### 2) Barriers

Devices; contempt residue; fatigue; secrecy; performance anxiety; using sex to regulate; avoiding eye contact; multitasking “together.”

### 3) Intimacy ritual

Example (married): 20 minutes face-to-face, phones out, one appreciations + one honest sentence + optional touch fitting consent.  
Example (friend/family): weekly walk without phones; one deep question.

Write yours; keep type-honoring (Course 09 W1).

### 4) Full attention daily

One block ≥15 minutes: no screens, one person, body oriented toward them. Log it.

### 5) 7-day no-phone dinner

Or breakfast/coffee if dinner impossible. Same rule: device elsewhere. Presence is the point, not culinary perfection.

### 6) Desire and pressure

Pressure (“prove you love me tonight”) violates honor. Presence invites; it does not coerce. Desire stewarded under design (Course 06 W11).

### 7) Analogy: candlelight

You light the candle on purpose. Ambient fluorescent “we’re in the same room scrolling” is not a feast.

### 8) Better whole

Intimacy is not completing your missing half. It is two wholes sharing fitting nearness.

### 9) Continuity

Presence needs a rulebook (Week 8) so rituals survive busy seasons.
