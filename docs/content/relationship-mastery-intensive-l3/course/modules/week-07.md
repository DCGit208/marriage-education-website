## The Central Truth

Intimacy is built by **presence**, not by pressure.  
Candlelight is intentional. Phones, hurry, and performance sex/emotion are counterfeits. Full attention is the Psychomotor and Affective gift of a whole person. You remain **teleios** by design; nearness does not complete unfinished ontology. Two **better wholes** share fitting closeness under type limits — desire does not rewrite what a bond *is*.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Intimacy defined (design)

**Intimacy** = fitting closeness of knowledge, affection, and (where covenant) body — under type limits.  

It is **not**: forced vulnerability theater; unlimited access as proof of love; using sex or emotional dump to regulate; collapsing friendship into romance by intensity alone; completing your missing half.

Course 09 W1 remains law: type limits are honor, not prudery. Intensity does not upgrade type.

### 2) Presence vs pressure

| Presence | Pressure |
|----------|----------|
| Attention offered | Nearness demanded |
| Invitation + consent | Prove-you-love-me tonight |
| Phones down, body oriented | Same room, split attention |
| Pace honors the other | Pace serves anxiety |
| Desire stewarded | Desire as entitlement |

Pressure manufactures compliance or freeze. Presence builds knowledge that can be trusted.

### 3) Barriers (name yours)

Devices; contempt residue; unrepaired backlog (Week 6); fatigue; secrecy; performance anxiety; using sex to regulate; avoiding eye contact; multitasking “together”; pornographic formation that trains fantasy over person; hustle identity that treats presence as inefficiency.

For top two barriers, write costs: Bond quality · Body · Calling · Inner life.

### 4) Desire ≠ ontology (non-negotiable)

Desire reports attraction, longing, or appetite. It does not mint personhood, rewrite sexed design, or redefine bond type (Course 02 / Course 06 W11). “I want” is not “therefore this must be.” Teleios persons steward desire; they do not baptize every urge as destiny. Pressure often hides ontology panic: “If you don’t want me right now, I am unfinished.” Better whole refuses that lie.

### 5) Intimacy ritual (write yours; type-honoring)

**Married example:** 20 minutes face-to-face, phones out of room, one appreciation + one honest sentence + optional touch fitting consent and energy.  

**Dating example:** weekly undistracted walk or meal; no sexual pressure as “proof”; clear type speech.  

**Friend/family example:** weekly walk without phones; one deep question; no romance theater.  

Write start time, duration, location, device rule, and exit if load is red (reschedule — do not fake presence).

### 6) Full attention daily

One block ≥15 minutes: no screens, one person, body oriented toward them. Log it. Attention is not ambient co-existence. Fluorescent “we’re in the same room scrolling” is not a feast.

### 7) 7-day no-phone dinner (or parallel)

Or breakfast/coffee if dinner is impossible. Same rule: device elsewhere. Presence is the point, not culinary perfection. If you live alone, run the ritual with a friend/family member or — on designated days — a presence block with God and journaling that refuses phone escape (still log; do not fake a household you don’t have).

### 8) Analogy: candlelight

You light the candle on purpose. Ambient half-light with notifications is not Sabbath nearness. Analogy limit: romance aesthetics without truth and repair are decoration on a cracked table.

### 9) Ratiocination (non-negotiable)

- Premise 1: Fitting intimacy requires attention from whole persons.  
- Premise 2: Pressure, devices, and type confusion counterfeit closeness.  
- Premise 3: Teleios persons steward desire; better wholes refuse fusion-completion via nearness.  
- Conclusion: Intimacy is presence under design limits — trained, not demanded.

### 10) After conflict and forgiveness

Weeks 5–6 clear the room. Unreleased revenge and unrepaired acts sit at the table as third parties. Do not demand candlelight while prosecution continues. Presence after structural release is different from presence as distraction from backlog.

### 11) Sexual presence (where covenant applies)

Married track: presence includes honored body — not performance metrics, not coercion, not “owed” sex as conflict payoff. Consent and kindness are design. Singles/dating: type limits forbid using sexual intensity to manufacture covenant. Desire stewarded ≠ desire denied as shame ontology; it means order.

### 12) Language discipline

Forbidden sentences (catch and rewrite):

- “If you loved me you’d…” → “I miss presence; can we schedule ______?”  
- “We’re fine; we’re in the same house” → “We shared space; we did not share attention.”  
- “Desire means we must…” → “Desire is real; type and vow still govern.”  
- “I need you to complete me tonight” → “I am teleios; I ask for fitting nearness, not ontology.”

### 13) Continuity into Week 8

Presence needs a rulebook so rituals survive busy seasons. Week 8 installs living rules + weekly review + Level 3 charter integrating Weeks 1–7. Without rules, candlelight dies in Q4 calendar chaos.

### 14) What this week is not

Not forced vulnerability on a timer.  
Not unlimited disclosure as virtue.  
Not anti-desire.  
Not phone-phobia as new legalism while bitterness remains.  
Not fusion intimacy wearing romance vocabulary.

### 15) School 1 greatness criterion (Week 7)

A great School 1 graduate can put the phone down and stay. If seven dinners reveal you cannot attend without managing, performing, or escaping, do not rush the rulebook as branding. Stay until presence is believable under fatigue.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Pressure intimacy
Riley demands depth and sex on demand; partner freezes. “Prove you love me” replaces invitation. Fruit: compliance or distance; desire becomes fear.

### Case B — Parallel play
Avery shares couch and phones; calls it closeness. No ritual; no eye contact; backlog untouched. Fruit: loneliness with company.

### Case C — Presence
Sam runs no-phone dinners seven days, keeps a 20-minute ritual, quieted Week 6 backlog enough to attend, honors type limits, asks without coerce. Fruit: knowledge grows; intimacy becomes trainable.
