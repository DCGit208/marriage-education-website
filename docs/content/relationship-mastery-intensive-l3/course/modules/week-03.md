## The Central Truth

Regulated emotion is the difference between chaos and peace.  
A thermostat is **active**. Venting without governance is weather without walls. You do not become holy by numbness; you steward the **Emotional attribute** because you are teleios — feelings report; they do not rule.

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do we regulate emotion without shame ontology or preference gospel?

### Key Insight
Triggers are predictable. Regulation routines (pause–name–govern–repair) turn storms into navigable weather. “I was just being honest” is often ungoverned discharge.

### Learning Outcomes
- List emotional triggers with body/story/move  
- Create a regulation routine  
- Practice pause-and-breathe in conflict  
- Keep a 7-day emotional log  
- Pass an Emotional Regulation Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Attribute clarity

- **Emotional attribute** — how feeling states move and get governed  
- **Affective faculty** — capacity to care / value  

Do not collapse them. Regulation trains the attribute; care still matters.

### 2) Trigger map

| Trigger | Body | Premise | Urge | Design move |
|---------|------|---------|------|-------------|
| | | | | pause / name / ask / exit-with-return |

### 3) Regulation routine (under 3 minutes)

1. Pause (Behavioral)  
2. Breathe / ground (Psychomotor)  
3. Name feeling + premise (Cognitive/Mental)  
4. Choose one governed act (Conative)  
5. If harm done — repair same day  

### 4) Pause and breathe in conflict

Agree with partner: either may call a 10–20 minute pause with return time. Breathing is not avoidance when the return is kept (Week 2 / Course 09).

### 5) 7-day emotional log

| Date | Trigger | Intensity | Routine used? | Outcome |
|------|---------|-----------|---------------|---------|

### 6) Analogy: thermostat

Furnace without thermostat burns the house. Feelings without governance burn the bond. Thermostat ≠ turning heat off forever.

### 7) Better whole

“Calm me down” as permanent job for the other is fusion. Ask for help; own the thermostat.

### 8) Continuity

Regulated people can learn communication mastery (Week 4). Dysregulated brilliance still wounds.
