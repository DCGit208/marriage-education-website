## The Central Truth

Mastery in communication is mastery in relationship.  
A two-way radio fails if one side only transmits. Mirror listening — accurate reflection before rebuttal — is the skill that turns speeches into shared reality.

## Week Overview

## Core Teaching

### 1) Effectiveness audit

Score recent hard talks:

- Did they feel heard (ask them)?  
- Was one ask clear?  
- Tone vs content match?  
- Devices present?  
- Repair after miss?  

### 2) Poor habit → design habit

Pick one: interrupting → wait+note; advice-rush → ask permission; sarcasm → specific request; monologue → 3-sentence turns; mind-reading → questions.

### 3) Mirror listening protocol

1. Speaker: 60–90 seconds  
2. Listener: paraphrase content + feeling guess  
3. Speaker: confirm or correct  
4. Only then: response / ask  

Mirror ≠ agreement. Mirror = Cognitive honor.

### 4) 3-day challenge

Minimum two mirrored conversations/day (can be short). Log accuracy corrections — those teach more than smooth days.

### 5) Analogy: two-way radio

Static, talking over, or one-way broadcast all kill mission. Feedback loop is oxygen at mastery level.

### 6) Load rule

Under Emotional surge (Week 3): pause first, then mirror. Mastery is sequenced.

### 7) Better whole

“You should already know” dies here. Whole persons use words and mirrors.

### 8) Continuity

Mirrored understanding still meets conflict. Week 5 installs martial skill — protocol, not reaction.
