## Course thesis

Levels 1–2 installed heart and systems. Level 3 is the **intensive**: covenant-grade capacity — mindset, stability, emotional governance, communication and conflict mastery, structural forgiveness, intimacy as presence, and a living rulebook.

Mastery is **likeness under load**, not a new ontology. You remain teleios by design; this course trains covenant-grade expression.

---

## Doctrine guardrails

- Teleios = design-state; practice does not manufacture being.
- Better whole — no fusion intimacy or better-half regulation.
- Covenant ≠ convenience contract; also ≠ control cult.
- “Attachment” language = stability patterns under design — not identity ontology (“I am my style”).
- Emotional regulation = **Emotional attribute** stewardship (Course 06 W4), not Affective faculty collapse.
- Forgiveness = structural release + repair path — not foggy feeling and not denial of justice/wisdom.
- Intimacy built by presence; desire does not rewrite ontology (Course 02 / 06 W11).
- Rulebook = governance freedom (Course 09 W6), not tyranny.
