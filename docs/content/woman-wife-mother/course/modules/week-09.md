## III. Discipline — What You Must Train

Train **scent discipline**: select one or two covenant fragrances for @Home and @Night contexts. Apply with consistency — not randomness. Scent is memory and anticipation; train it.

Train **vocal warmth**: record yourself speaking to your husband (or practice alone). Notice flatness, sharpness, or hurry. Train lower warmth, slower pace, and intentional tone at key moments — greeting, goodnight, invitation.

Train **movement awareness**: how you walk, sit, rise, and cross a room. Teleios movement is neither masculine brusqueness nor infantile collapse. Practice graceful entry and purposeful proximity.

Train **anticipation rituals**: small signals that sex is not an event bolted onto exhaustion — a touch on the shoulder, changed attire at night, lighting, music, timing. These are not manipulations; they are **liturgy of invitation**.

Train **bitterness excision**: allure cannot coexist with chronic resentment you refuse to address. Discipline includes confession, forgiveness, and structural repair — or honest escalation to pastoral help. Do not perform allure over untreated contempt.

Train **responsiveness capacity**: allure includes receiving as well as offering — warmth when he reaches, eye contact when he speaks, body language that says present. Train reception as discipline, not mood.

Train **gaze discipline**: sustained eye contact at greeting and invitation — not phone, not task screen. Eyes carry erotic field before touch.

Train **timing intelligence**: allure respects rhythm — invitation when exhaustion is structural vs when exhaustion is excuse. Discern; do not manipulate with false emergencies.

Train **@Day continuity**: allure is not @Night-only. Brief warm text, scent on departure return, touch on reunion — lifestyle tree watered through the week.

Train **holy secrecy discipline**: prepare attuned surprise without announcement — note, treat, fragrance change — offer without ledger.

Train **renunciation of porn imagination**: when fantasy is generic or coercive, repent and redirect to covenant attunement data about **your** husband.
