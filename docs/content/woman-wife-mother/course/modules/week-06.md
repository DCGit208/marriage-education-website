## II. Consciousness — What You Must See

You must see attraction as **covenant signal**, not cosmetic garnish on functional marriage. When a wife abandons meritorious discipline while demanding conjugal attention, or when a husband consumes critique without forming virility, **marital infidelity has occurred at the level of structure**—design union drifting while roles remain.

### Merit versus entitlement (see both errors)

**Therapeutic entitlement** — *"He should love me unchanged"* without stewardship—certificate theology rejected by design and fruit.

**Despotic demand** — husband's coercive criticism without reciprocity or his own virility formation—also rejected. Attraction collapse is often **mutual structural drift**.

**Desexualization** — attraction treated as optional after children or years—roommate marriage with occasional duty.

**Fragmentation** — believing service output replaces embodied presence—wife without woman visible.

**Sciolism** — discussing "romance" without defining terms or training psychomotor habits—talk without embodiment.

### A³ consciousness (integrated preview)

You must see each element as **trainable**, not mood:

**Attitude** — disposition toward husband as **man** in covenant—not coworker, not child-supervisor, not resentment default. Sexual-feminine intention without manipulation. Week 7 specializes; this week installs precursor: one daily intention stated aloud.

**Attire** — dressing **for** nakedness in covenant context (Week 4, canon §VI)—fit, repair, context (@Home, @Day). Not burying design; not market seduction. Presentation signals whether wifehood lives in woman.

**Allure** — erotic covenant signal within one flesh—scent, movement, gaze, proximity—not pornographic performance, not permanent frump as false humility. Holy specificity Song of Solomon models.

**Fitness / Form / Shape** — psychomotor extension (Week 4, Week 10)—health, strength, posture, sustainable form. Attraction without health collapses; health without conjugal intention stays functional.

### Covenant-scoped beauty

The standard is not magazine cover. It is **teleios presentation for one-flesh union**—best stewardship of your actual frame, age, and context. Compete with your own neglect, not with strangers on screens.

### Motherhood decay pattern (name it)

Maternal self-sacrifice that sacrifices **womanhood** produces wives husbands no longer see as women—only as children's manager. Homemaking and childcare are legitimate when flowing from integrated wholeness; **attraction abandoned while output rises** is fragmentation, not virtue.

### Reciprocal virility consciousness

Husband forms **virility** in parallel—manly covenant presence, bodily stewardship, lifestyle sexuality, meritorious discipline—not supervising wife's formation. Reciprocity: self-report, mutual admiration grammar, no correction theater.

### Long horizon consciousness

Meritorious attraction is **lifespan discipline**. The woman who trains at thirty inherits conjugal ground at fifty. The woman who defers until "later" trains entitlement, not merit. Reason from fruit (Isaiah 1:18): decades tell truth.

### Three witnesses

Evaluate attraction claims: **Scripture** (Song, Proverbs, 1 Peter priority), **design** (one-flesh biology, polarity), **fruit** (conjugal life, husband's covenant gaze, children's inheritance). Feelings are data—not doctrine.
