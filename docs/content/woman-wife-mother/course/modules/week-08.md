## I. Ontology — What This Week Is

This week addresses **Attire** — the second term in the **A³** operational grammar of muliebrity: **Attitude → Attire → Allure**. Attire is not fashion as vanity, not self-expression as entitlement, and not the costume of fragmented womanhood. Attire is the **visible grammar of covenant sexuality** — the way a teleios woman dresses her body in acknowledgment that she belongs, in one flesh, to her husband and to the design God gave her.

Womanhood remains the foundation. The wife lives in the woman. Therefore attire is never "wife costume" layered over a neglected woman. It is muliebrity made legible in cloth, cut, color, and context. A woman who dresses only for utility, only for approval from the world, or only when she "feels like it" has not understood attire as formation. She has treated clothing as accident — and accident is not covenant.

Attire sits between inward disposition (Attitude, Week 7) and outward atmosphere (Allure, Week 9). It is psychomotor and cognitive at once: you choose garments, maintain them, coordinate them, and wear them with intention. This is **meritorious attraction** applied to the wardrobe — disciplined effort toward design conformity, not despotic demand from a husband and not passive entitlement that says appearance should not matter.

The **MV House** wardrobe taxonomy gives structure without reducing womanhood to shopping. Canonical contexts — **Casual @Home**, **Casual @Day**, **Casual @Night**, **Corporate**, **Official/Ceremony** — are not lifestyle brands. They are **covenant service categories**: each context asks what your body communicates in that setting, to your husband, to your household, and to the world, while remaining integrated with the **nakedness genre** (Week 4). You dress **for** nakedness — stewarding the body that will be unveiled in one-flesh intimacy — not **against** it by hiding, punishing, or desexualizing yourself in cloth.

This week sits in the **A³ arc** (Weeks 6–10) before Week 11's integration summit. Formation flow:

```
Identity → Consciousness → Discipline → Practice → Covenant Flourishing
```

Weeks 6–7 established meritorious attraction and **Attitude**. **Attire** makes muliebrity **legible** before Week 9 **Allure** completes the atmospheric grammar. Week 10 **Fitness, Form, and Shape** supplies the psychomotor substrate that makes attire credible on the body you steward.

## II. Consciousness — What You Must See

You must see that every garment is a statement — whether you intend it or not. Unintentional dress is not neutral; it is unformed. The woman who lives in yoga pants and neglect at home while expecting conjugal fervor at night has split her sexuality from her lifestyle. She has treated attire as irrelevant to the tree while still wanting the fruit. **Sex is the product; sexuality is the lifestyle.** Attire is a branch of that tree.

You must see the difference between **MV House** as covenant service and fashion as vanity. Vanity seeks admiration from an audience undefined by covenant. MV House intelligence asks: *What does this context require of a teleios woman who belongs to one man?* At home, attire is not slovenliness disguised as comfort. At day, it is not disappearance into androgyny or provocative chaos. At night, it is not exhaustion that communicates "you are last." Corporate and Official contexts extend muliebrity into public stewardship without erasing femininity or collapsing into role-only function.

You must see **integration law** at work: lose womanhood to "mother" and you dress like a caretaker who has no body. Lose womanhood to "wife" as function and you dress like a household manager who has no erotic presence. The integrated woman dresses as **one person** across contexts — adjusting register, not identity.

You must see your husband as covenant partner, not wardrobe supervisor. He may have preferences; reciprocity means he forms his presentation too. Your attire formation is **your discipline**, offered in due benevolence (1 Cor 7:3–4), not performed under inspection.

You must see what you have called "modesty" may sometimes be **desexualization** — hiding a body you have abandoned rather than stewarding a body you honor. Modesty in SBD is ordered revelation, not erasure of design.

You must see **§F1 marriage ontology** in cloth: **Marriage = Sexuality = Nakedness**. Attire stewards the body that belongs to one flesh. **Sex** is fruit; **sexuality** is lifestyle; wardrobe is a daily branch of that tree (Week 5).

When maternal output rises while @Home presentation collapses, you are watching the **motherhood decay pattern**—fragmentation in cloth, not virtue. **Meritorious attraction** in wardrobe means disciplined presentation for one covenant man, not market comparison or entitlement without maintenance. Judge progress by three witnesses—**Scripture**, **design** (nakedness genre), and **fruit** (conjugal atmosphere, children's inheritance). Mood is data, not doctrine.

### Design reasoning — therapeutic "dress for yourself alone"

Therapeutic culture treats attire as **private self-expression** without covenant aim. SBD rejects this as **sciolism**: undefined authenticity that severs wardrobe from one-flesh life. You dress as **teleios woman in covenant order**—not as performance for undefined audience, not as punishment of body, not as entitlement that expects devotion while you disappear. Reason from fruit: does current wardrobe build lifestyle sexuality or starve it?

### Design reasoning — modesty vs desexualization

**Modesty** in SBD is **ordered revelation**—stewarding nakedness genre, not erasing sexual distinction (Deut 22:5 read as design honor, not contempt for beauty). **Desexualization** hides a body you have abandoned or punishes flesh rather than training it. Many women call desexualization "modesty" because church language never distinguished them. Name your wardrobe honestly.

## III. Discipline — What You Must Train

Train **wardrobe consciousness**: audit what you own against the five MV House contexts. Identify gaps where you have clothes for function but not for covenant presence — especially @Home and @Night.

Train **daily selection discipline**: each morning (or the night before), select attire with intention. Name the context. Name the message. This is not obsession; it is liturgy of the body.

Train **maintenance rigor**: clean, pressed, repaired garments signal that your body and your marriage are not afterthoughts. Psychomotor discipline includes laundry, mending, and replacing worn items — lifestyle, not sprint.

Train **feminine register without infantilization**: teleios attire honors curves, dignity, and movement. Reject masculinization (dressing as unsexed worker by default) and infantilization (dressing as childish or helpless). Reject survival mode (whatever is clean enough) as a permanent wardrobe philosophy.

Train **husband-attuned intelligence** without outsourcing your formation: notice what honors one-flesh attraction in your marriage. Document preferences as data for your discipline, not as commands you obey without consciousness.

Train **budget and planning discipline**: meritorious attraction is not extravagance. It is intentional acquisition — one context at a time if needed — aligned with covenant, not impulse or social comparison.

Train **@Night register discipline**: attire at end of day signals whether wifehood remains present or exhaustion communicates *you are last*. @Night is covenant context, not collapse default.

Train **corporate/official extension**: muliebrity in public stewardship — feminine distinction without androgyny, without provocative chaos. Same woman, different register.

Train **integration pause before dressing**: ten seconds — *Womanhood governs attire today* — then select with Context / Message / Adjustment.

Train **purge discipline**: garments that train bitterness, body hatred, or disappearance leave active wardrobe — formation through removal.

### Faculty architecture — why attire is trainable

Week 2 named **four faculties**. Attire is where **psychomotor** and **cognitive** faculties govern marriage outwardly:

| Faculty | Attire function | Deformation signal |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Psychomotor** | Body clothed, groomed, maintained with intention | Survival dress, neglect, body hatred in cloth |
| **Cognitive** | Context selection—MV House register, not fashion tribe | Sciolism: "appearance shouldn't matter" without definition |
| **Affective** | Warmth and availability signaled in fabric and fit | Bitterness, disappearance, punitive slovenliness |
| **Conative** | Daily selection discipline—liturgy of the body | "Whatever is clean" as permanent philosophy |

Attire is not shopping-only. A wife who buys garments while @Home remains chronically neglected has trained **acquisition without embodiment**. Integration requires all faculties—Week 8 weights psychomotor and cognitive; Week 9 extends to **allure** atmosphere.

### Meritorious attraction requires attire

Week 6 trained meritorious attraction; Week 7 trained inward **Attitude**. **Meritorious attraction** collapses when attire signals disappearance: husband reads structure before he reads mood. Attire is **merit outward**—the second A³ term without which allure becomes manipulation or noise. Certificate does not command desire; neither does attitude without wardrobe that stewards the body in covenant contexts.

### Civilizational consciousness — what children read in your dress

The home is the **first university** (UDHR 16:3 chain). Children do not primarily learn marriage from sermons—they learn from **what mother wears at home** and how she speaks about her body. A mother who despises her body in cloth while demanding respect in speech teaches **fragmentation**—womanhood abandoned while roles perform. Covenant woman dressing with intention @Home teaches **integration**—muliebrity legible in the primary context. Attire formation is therefore not private vanity work; it is **generational repair**.
