### The Wife — Womanhood in One-Flesh Matrimony

**The Wife** is **womanhood in one-flesh matrimony** — muliebrity **deployed** in covenant. State wifehood **positively** from design and Scripture:

### What wifehood IS (integrated expression)

1. **Conjugal-sexual covenant life** — marriage **is** conjugal-sexual covenant (§F1). Wifehood lives **lifestyle sexuality** continuously — sexuality as tree, sex as fruit — not episodic duty bolted onto exhaustion.

2. **Due benevolence** — full sexual covenant obligation (1 Corinthians 7:3–4). Bodies belong to one another in one flesh. Benevolence extends into atmosphere (Week 9 allure), presentation (Week 8 attire), and disposition (Week 7 attitude).

3. **Covenant helper** — *ezer kenegdo* (Genesis 2:18): helper **fit for him** — strong complement facing him in design, not subordinate machine. Helper-life includes homemaking, wisdom, household order — **expressions flowing from womanhood**, not replacements of it.

4. **Submission** — Ephesians 5:22–24 disposition toward husband as head in Christ's order (full exposition below). Submission is **positive covenant grammar** — wifehood aligned to structural order, not identity erased.

5. **Meritorious attraction** — disciplined embodied care maintaining covenant signal (Week 6). Certificate does not command desire; training does.

6. **Nakedness genre** — stewarded revelation and concealment in one flesh (Week 4). **Marriage = Sexuality = Nakedness** (§F1).

7. **One-flesh priority** — *"Therefore shall a man leave father and mother, and cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh"* (Genesis 2:24). Wife expression is covenant priority within the family system — not submerged by mother role.

These are **one integrated wife** — not separable compartments where one can be chosen and others abandoned. Fragmentation is when wifehood runs **without womanhood governing** — output, scheduling, or conjugal episodes without teleios muliebrity as foundation.

When womanliness leaves covenant flesh — desexualization, contempt, managerial replacement of conjugal disposition — **marital infidelity has occurred at the level of structure** before any affair. Name fact; return to training.

### Ephesians 5:22–24 — Divine Matrimonial Order (TO WIVES)

*Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord. For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body. Therefore as the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands in every thing.*

### Structural order — not slavery

Ephesians 5 establishes **Divine Matrimonial Rule** — structural order in one flesh. Submission here is **covenant disposition**: the wife's alignment to headship as Christ's church aligns to Christ. SBD reads this as **design architecture**, not cultural oppression to discard nor tyranny to weaponize.

**Submission is positive.** Femininity includes covenant submission as wifehood expression — taught here at summit, not as embarrassment, not as optional add-on for "traditional" homes only. Design assigns it; teleios women **train** it as Baron discipline.

### *As unto the Lord* — manner and degree

**As** (*hos*) governs **manner**: submit to husband **in the way** you submit to the Lord — reverent, willing, ordered, not grudging performance. It also governs **proportion** in the analogy: church's subjection to Christ is the model for wife's subjection to husband **in the matrimonial structure God assigned** — not subjection to every man, not subjection to children, not erasure of teleios personhood.

Submission is not **silence in abuse** — design never commands sin. Submission is **covenant order** within one-flesh marriage between teleios spouses.

### Headship — Christ and church pattern

*"The husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church."*

Headship is **structural**, not sentimental. Christ's headship over the church is the analogue — sacrificial, life-giving, sanctifying (vv. 25–27 continue to husbands). Wife's submission answers **that** kind of headship — not domination theater, not passivity enabling cruelty.

**Reciprocity (cross-read, not wife-only):** husbands are charged to love as Christ loved the church — sacrificial headship (Ephesians 5:25–29). Integration summit focuses **TO WIVES**; husband's parallel rule belongs to virility formation. Neither spouse uses Ephesians 5 as inspection weapon against the other.

### *In every thing* — scope within covenant

*"As the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands in every thing."*

**In every thing** means **comprehensive disposition within matrimonial order** — not blind obedience to evil, not abdication of conscience. It means wifehood is not perpetual negotiation against husband's headship in every domestic decision; it means **aligned disposition** — helper fit for him, conjugal partner, submitted in structure God assigned.

Reason from design and fruit (Isaiah 1:18): homes that abandon Ephesians 5 wife grammar while retaining certificate often produce **ordered dysfunction** — co-management, resentment default, structural drift. Homes that train submission as covenant discipline alongside husband's sacrificial headship produce **integration** — polarity without war, helper-life without fragmentation.

### The Mother — Wifehood Extended

**The Mother** is **wifehood extended** in the generative family unit — UDHR Article 16, paragraph 3: the family as fundamental group unit of society.

Motherhood is not superior to wifehood; it lives **inside** it. The mother lives in the wife; the wife lives in the woman. A mother who operates without womanhood and wifehood foundation has collapsed into **fragmentation** — children's manager without conjugal woman present.

**Non-desexualized motherhood:** bodily stewardship continues; @Home attire and warmth toward husband (age-appropriate) refuse using children as barrier to one-flesh priority. Children need parents in **covenant**, not martyrs in resentment. They inherit integration or fragmentation by watching whether **woman remains in mother**.

Single-parent and widow seasons still require **womanhood foundation** — teleios first; covenant expressions adapt to circumstance without erasing design.

Homemaking, nurture, and generative labor are **legitimate expressions** when flowing from integrated wholeness — Proverbs 31 models **one woman** as merchant, maker, mother, wife, steward.
