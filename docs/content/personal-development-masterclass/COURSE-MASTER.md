## Course thesis

Courses 02–05 installed anthropology, identity, faculties, and developmental law.  
Course 06 is the **integration intensive**: personal development as the responsible expression of design across the domains of ordinary life.

This MasterClass does **not** manufacture teleios.  
It trains the learner to become mindful, educated, and accountable in vision, identity, mental and emotional attributes, then (in later weeks) temperament, habits, time, money, speech, relationship, sexuality, and covenant readiness.

---

## Doctrine guardrails

- Teleios/maturity is design-state / full development; activity does not create being.
- Holiness is state (`be holy`); likeness is formed alignment.
- Image is given; identity is not self-invention.
- Better whole, not better half.
- **Attributes** = Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental (Course 02 Week 3).
- **Capacities/Faculties** = Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, Affective (Course 04).
- Mental Discipline (Week 3) is the **Mental attribute**, not the Cognitive faculty.
- Emotional Formation (Week 4) is the **Emotional attribute**, not the Affective faculty.
- Vision does not invent a self; vision **sees** design and orders life accordingly.
- Calling is stewardship of given design, not a branded career myth.

## Learning outcomes (Weeks 1–8)

By the end of Weeks 1–8, the learner will:

1. Write a Teleios Vision statement from design, not from self-invention.
2. Distinguish identity (given) from calling (stewardship of what is given).
3. Steward Mental, Emotional, and Temperamental attributes without identity collapse.
4. Install habit infrastructure and first-hour / weekly time order.
5. Treat money as alignment test and stewardship — not as worth.
6. Hold attribute vs faculty distinction without collapse.
