# Week 10: Relationships & Repair
## Repair Is Covenant Practice — Not Proof You Were Never Whole

> **Week 10 of 12** | 8–10 hours | Covenant Practice  
> **Sources:** Matthew 5:23–24 · Matthew 18:15 · Week 4 same-day repair · Course 05 gap protocol · better whole

---

## The Central Truth

Relationships are not perfection projects. They are **truth + repair** projects among whole persons.  
Repair does not mean you were created incomplete. Repair means likeness was violated in practice and must be restored under design.

“Own, apologize, act” is the week’s spine — without groveling theater, without blame fusion, without better-half ontology.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How is repair practiced without better-half ontology?

### Key Insight
Slow repair is often pride or freeze wearing “I need time.” Fast fake repair is often image management. Covenant repair is specific, timely, and followed by changed action.

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this week you will:

- Audit repair speed on your last three harms
- Name one live relationship requiring repair
- Practice own–apologize–act once this week with evidence
- Refuse fusion (“we’re broken”) and exile (“you’re the problem”)
- Write a Repair Covenant Rule

## Core Teaching

### 1) Whole persons, damaged practices

Canon order:

- You are teleios by design.
- You can still wound.
- Wound requires repair of practice and trust — not reinvention of ontology.

Illegal moves:

- “If I need to apologize, my identity is fake.”
- “If you loved me, you wouldn’t need repair.”
- “Repair means we were never whole.”

### 2) Own — apologize — act

| Step | Meaning | Counterfeit |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Own** | Name the conduct specifically | “Sorry you feel that way” |
| **Apologize** | Confess the wrong without excuse pile | Apology + “but you…” |
| **Act** | Visible next change + invite check | Words only; repeat pattern |

Matthew 5:23–24 prioritizes reconciliation initiative. Matthew 18:15 prioritizes directness over gossip.

### 3) Repair speed audit

Track last three harms you caused:

| Harm | Days until owned | Specificity | Act followed? | Trust fruit |
|------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|-------------|

Slow speed usually signals: shame spiral (Week 4), Mental premise (“I’m trash”), or Temperament withdraw (Week 5).

### 4) Better whole in repair

Fusion: “We’re a mess” — erases distinct agency.  
Exile: “You’re the only problem” — erases your part.  
Better whole: “I own my part; I invite your part; we do not complete each other’s ontology.”

A spouse may forgive. A spouse cannot be your conscience chip or your missing will.

### 5) Repair vs scorekeeping

Repair seeks restored trust. Scorekeeping seeks leverage.  
If “apology” is stored as ammunition, it was strategy, not repentance.

### 6) When you are the harmed party

You are not required to fake peace. You are required to:

- speak truth without contempt (Week 9),
- refuse silent bitterness as virtue,
- allow process under Law of Time without denying debt.

Forgiveness is not the same as immediate restoration of all access. Design still includes boundaries that protect flourishing (Course 02).

### 7) Analogy: bone set vs bone ignored

Week 3’s bone-setting analogy returns: misaligned patterns can “function” until load. Repair is resetting alignment under truth — uncomfortable, then load-bearing again.

### 8) Link to Course 05 DGRP

If the same harm repeats, escalate from event-repair to **gap protocol**: stage/law breach, minimum structure, 30-day evidence. Event apologies without gap repair become confessional theater.

### 9) Household and beyond

Repair applies to spouse, sibling, friend, colleague — with wisdom about safety. This week requires **one live repair**, not twelve theoretical ones.

### 10) Forgiveness, trust, and access

These are related, not identical:

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Forgiveness | Releasing vengeance claim; refusing to weaponize the debt |
| Trust | Evidenced expectation based on fruit |
| Access | Nearness privileges that may be paced while trust rebuilds |

Demanding instant full access after chronic betrayal is not holiness; it can be naivety. Withholding forgiveness as leverage is not holiness; it is scorekeeping.

### 11) Gossip as anti-repair

Talking *about* the person to third parties while refusing Matthew 18 directness multiplies harm. Exception: safety, abuse, and wise counsel — not venting clubs.

### 12) MasterClass bridge

Week 10 is where Weeks 4, 5, and 9 become public. If repair still fails, return to Course 05 gap protocol before adding covenant weight (Week 12).

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Delay as doctrine
Ken waits until “ready,” by which time contempt has hardened. Freeze baptized as maturity.

### Case B — Flood apology
Priya apologizes dramatically and changes nothing. Theater without act.

### Case C — Clean repair
Joel: “I interrupted and dismissed you Tuesday. I was wrong. Tonight I will listen 15 minutes without defending. Check me Friday.” Trust begins to rebuild because act is visible.
