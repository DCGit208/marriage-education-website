## The Central Truth

Money is a **test of order and alignment**, not a measure of ontological worth.  
Poverty does not make you less image-bearing. Wealth does not manufacture teleios. Both can be lived in design or in drift.

The catalogue stub is right: money is a test of order, not of worth. This week installs that as practice — tracking, leak-naming, giving/saving structure — without prosperity superstition or shame theology.

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How does money test order without defining worth?

Financial chaos is often Mental premise + Emotional surge + Habit + Time disorder wearing a spreadsheet. Fixing numbers without fixing those layers is temporary. Fixing those layers without truthful numbers is fantasy.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Ontology first

You are not your balance sheet.  
Image is given. Holiness is state. Likeness includes how you handle property and promise.

Illegal premises:

- “I am what I earn.”
- “I am what I lack.”
- “When I have more, I will become generous / honest / ordered.”

Ratiocination:

- Premise 1: Worth-as-wealth is false ontology.
- Premise 2: False ontology drives either pride or despair in money behavior.
- Conclusion: Stewardship begins by refusing money as identity.

### 2) Money as alignment detector

Where money goes, alignment shows.

| Pattern | Often reveals |
|---------|----------------|
| Hidden spending | Secrecy loops; fear of truth |
| Impulse under shame | Emotional attribute ungoverened |
| Under-earning with skill | Conative refusal or fear premise |
| Overwork for status goods | Vision as vanity (Week 1) |
| No giving with surplus | Axiology disordered |

1 Timothy 6:10 warns of *love* of money — not of money’s existence. The love is the mis-ranking.

### 3) Order applied to cashflow

Week 7’s order becomes Week 8’s ledger:

1. See (track)
2. Rank (needs, vows, generosity, margin)
3. Sequence (give/save before optional)
4. Review (weekly money meeting)

Proverbs 3:9 — honor with substance — is stewardship language, not a tip for getting rich.

### 4) Leaks: cue and payoff

A leak is repeated outflow that violates your stated design budget.

Name leaks like habits (Week 6):

- Cue (fatigue, conflict, boredom, envy scroll)
- Payoff (numbing, status, false comfort)
- Design replacement (cheaper true need + time/relationship repair)

Without cue/payoff, “budget harder” fails.

### 5) Giving / saving / spending — minimum rule

Write a rule that fits your season (percentages are tools, not righteousness):

> Of every income unit, I will ______% give, ______% save/margin, remainder spend under categories ______, and I will not ______ without a 24-hour pause.

Constraints must include household fidelity: secret debt, secret accounts, and status competition that humiliates a spouse are alignment failures.

### 6) Money meeting

A money meeting is not a fight appointment. It is an **epistemic session**: numbers, premises, next acts.

Agenda (20–30 minutes):

1. Facts (what came in / went out)
2. Leaks named without contempt
3. One repair / one cut / one build
4. Prayer or closing true sentence (design, not doom)

If single: same meeting with a trusted friend or mentor — anti-delusion architecture (Course 05 accountability).

### 7) Analogy: instrument panel, not altar

Money is an **instrument panel**.  
You watch gauges to fly the plane you already are responsible to fly.  
You do not worship the gauges, and you do not ignore them and call it faith.

### 8) Better whole and money fusion

- “My spouse handles money so I stay innocent” — often abdication.
- “I earn so I rule unilaterally” — often domination.
- Covenant money is shared truth + owned responsibility appropriate to roles, without erasing either person’s agency.

### 9) Contentment vs complacency

Contentment (1 Tim 6:6) is alignment of desire with design.  
Complacency is refusal to labor, skill, or repair.  
Do not baptize laziness as holiness, or greed as “provision mindset.”

### 10) Debt, secrecy, and covenant speech

Debt is not always sin; secrecy about debt almost always damages covenant trust.

Rules for this week:

- If married/engaged: no new consumer debt without disclosed agreement.
- If already in debt: bring numbers into the money meeting without narrative fog.
- If ashamed: shame is a feeling report (Week 4); concealment is a choice — govern both.

### 11) Generosity without performance

Giving can become vanity or control. Design-aligned giving is:

- planned (not only emotional spikes),
- truthful (not image management),
- non-coercive toward others,
- compatible with household vows.

If giving requires lying to a spouse or neglecting owed provision, it is misaligned zeal.

### 12) Mid-course checkpoint (Weeks 5–8)

Write one page:

1. Temperament counter-habit — evidence it fired under load
2. Habit stack — what stabilized
3. Time budget — what you cut
4. Money — one leak closed and one truth spoken

This page is the bridge into Weeks 9–12 (speech, repair, sexuality, covenant readiness).

### 13) Teleios starting point (money edition)

Money tests order; it does not define worth. You are teleios by design whether the account is thin or thick. Prosperity does not invent holiness. Poverty does not erase image.

Chase grammar: “When I have more, I will become generous / honest / ordered.”  
Practice grammar: “I am teleios; stewardship of substance is owed in this season’s numbers.”

If money speech still sounds like identity (“I am my raise / I am my lack”), Week 1 vision and Week 2 identity are not yet in your mouth under load.

### 14) Worked example — envy scroll leak

Cue: late phone + comparison feed.  
Premise: “I’m behind; a purchase will close the gap.”  
Payoff: brief status relief.  
Cost: margin, secrecy risk, household trust.  
Replacement: phone out after 9 → write one teleios sentence → 24-hour pause on non-budget buys → name the desire in the money meeting without shame fog.  
Ontology line: “A purchase cannot manufacture the self I already am.”

### 15) Better whole and money

Abdication (“you handle it so I stay innocent”) and domination (“I earn so I unilaterally rule”) both violate better whole. Shared truth + owned responsibility appropriate to roles — without erasing either person’s agency, and without treating a spouse as the missing conscience for numbers.

### 16) Evidence bar for Week 8

Produce: 7-day expense log, two leaks with cue/payoff/replacement, written give/save/spend rule, money meeting notes (one cut, one build, one truth repaired), and the Weeks 5–8 checkpoint page. Numbers without truth remain fantasy; truth without numbers remains fog.

### Case A — Worth = wealth
Chris upgrades lifestyle with each raise and calls it blessing. Household peace falls. Alignment failed; ontology was money.

### Case B — Shame paralysis

Eli tracks 7 days, cuts one envy leak, sets give/save rule, holds a calm money meeting. Numbers improve slowly; trust improves faster because secrecy ended.
