## The Central Truth

Vision is not invention of a self.  
Vision is **seeing what is already true by design**, then ordering life accordingly.

You do not “build” teleios by writing a vision board. You were created teleios. The MasterClass begins with sight: if you cannot see design, you will spend twelve weeks optimizing drift.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
What must I see before I can live accordingly?

### Key Insight
Small vision is usually not lack of ambition. It is **failure to see ontology**. People set goals that assume they are incomplete, then treat achievement as the manufacturer of worth. Teleios vision starts from completeness and aims at responsible expression.

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this week you will:

- Define Teleios Vision without collapsing it into branding or manifestation
- Distinguish seeing design from inventing a future identity
- Map current life against a design-aligned vision (person, household, work, covenant)
- Complete a Vision Coherence Audit
- Write a Teleios Vision Statement and a 12-week sight rule

## Core Teaching

### 1) What vision is in this canon

**Vision** here means: the mind’s responsible apprehension of design, fruit, and direction.

It is:

- **epistemic** — you see what is true
- **axiological** — you rank what is worthy
- **practical** — you order time and action toward that ranking

It is not:

- a mood of inspiration
- a five-year fantasy resume
- “manifesting” a better self into existence
- a substitute for identity (Course 03 already established identity as given)

Proverbs 29:18 is often abused as “without a dream you perish.” The issue is not dreamlessness. The issue is **unrestrained life** where design is not seen and therefore not obeyed.

### 2) The error of “build the future you”

The catalogue stub said: *You cannot build what you cannot see.*  
Keep the sight requirement. Correct the ontology.

You do not build a human. You **steward** a human you already are.

| False vision | Teleios vision |
|--------------|----------------|
| I will become whole by achieving X | I am teleios; X must agree with design |
| Vision creates identity | Vision serves identity |
| The map invents the territory | The map describes the territory |
| Bigger goals prove maturity | Coherent goals prove sight |

Ratiocination:

- Premise 1: What a person is precedes what a person plans.
- Premise 2: Plans that contradict being produce misformation even when they “succeed.”
- Conclusion: Vision must be tested against design, not against applause.

### 3) Four lenses of Teleios Vision

Use the maturation pillars as sight-lenses (not as slogans):

1. **Ontology** — What kind of being am I seeing? (image, teleios, sexed, covenantal)
2. **Epistemology** — How do I know this vision is true? (Scripture, nature, fruit, reason)
3. **Axiology** — What does this vision rank as worthy? (truth, fidelity, responsibility)
4. **Etymology** — Do the words I use name reality (`be`, holy, perfect) or fashion?

If a “vision” cannot survive these four tests, it is preference with a motivational soundtrack.

### 4) The boundary function of vision

Vision **sets a boundary**. That is its first practical gift.

If you see yourself as a whole person under design:

- you will not recruit a “better half” to complete you
- you will not treat marriage as a repair clinic
- you will not use money, sex, or speech as identity factories
- you will refuse goals that require you to live as someone you are not

Vision that does not exclude is not vision. It is appetite.

### 5) Civilizational scaling of sight

Course 05 taught Individual → Household → Society.

Teleios Vision must be writable at all three scales:

| Scale | Vision question |
|-------|-----------------|
| Person | What does responsible expression of my design look like in ordinary weeks? |
| Household | What order, truth, and care would others actually experience? |
| Society | If this vision were multiplied, would families and institutions stabilize or fragment? |

A personal vision that destroys household order is not teleios. It is private ambition wearing formation language.

### 6) Analogy: lighthouse, not construction crane

A lighthouse does not manufacture the coastline. It **shows** it so ships do not wreck.

Teleios Vision is lighthouse work.  
The MasterClass is not a crane assembling a person from parts.

The car drives because it is a car.  
Vision does not install the engine. Vision keeps you from driving off the map.

### 7) How you know a vision is true (epistemic test)

Apply the Course 05 four-part test to every aim:

1. **Design coherence** — Does this agree with what a human is (image, teleios, sexed, covenantal)?
2. **Moral coherence** — Does it preserve truth, fidelity, responsibility, and non-harm as covenant order?
3. **Relational fruit** — Would household trust increase if this were lived for ninety days?
4. **Civilizational fruit** — If multiplied, would families stabilize or fragment?

Example claim: “My vision is to become someone worth loving.”

- Design coherence: false (worth is given in image; love is not earned by reinvention)
- Moral coherence: false (treats persons as projects)
- Relational fruit: anxiety, performance, better-half hunting
- Civilizational fruit: unstable households built on validation

Conclusion: that “vision” is incomplete-person theology with a calendar. Retire it.

### 8) MasterClass continuity — what Weeks 2–12 inherit from sight

Week 1 is not decorative. Everything later assumes this boundary:

| Later week | What Week 1 forbids |
|------------|---------------------|
| Identity & Calling | Calling as identity manufacture |
| Mental / Emotional | Thought and feeling as ontology |
| Habits / Time / Money | Optimization without design |
| Speech / Repair / Sexuality | Technique without who |
| Covenant Readiness | Readiness as “finally becoming enough” |

If you skip sight, the MasterClass becomes twelve weeks of competent misformation.

### 9) Common vision distortions

1. **Vision as escape** — naming a future so you need not repent the present.
2. **Vision as vanity** — aims that only make sense if someone is watching.
3. **Vision as outsourcing** — “when I marry / get hired / get platformed, then I will live.”
4. **Vision as vagueness** — holy language with no measurable stewardship.
5. **Vision as cruelty** — goals that require treating a spouse, child, or self as raw material.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Vision as self-invention
Jordan writes a 5-year map: income, physique, influence, “better partner.” None of it names design, holiness as state, or household fidelity. The map is a shopping list. When a relationship fails, Jordan updates the shopping list rather than the ontology.

### Case B — Vision as religious fog
Amara says “God will show me” and refuses to specify any ordered practice. Sight without responsibility is not humility. It is evasion of the very faculties Course 04 named.

### Case C — Teleios Vision
Levi writes: “I am teleios by design. For twelve weeks I will live from that: truthful speech, ordered first hour, one repaired relationship, no better-half outsourcing.” The aims are smaller on paper and larger in reality because they agree with being.

## Seven-Day Drill — Sight Practice

**Purpose:** educate vision as seeing, not as wishing.

### Day 1 — Ontology first
Write 10 lines beginning with “I am…” that do **not** mention job, income, or marital status. If you cannot, Course 02–03 are not yet in your mouth.

### Day 2 — Kill one false vision
Name one aim that assumes you are incomplete. Write why it fails the four-lens test. Retire it in writing.

### Day 3 — Person map
Draw (or list) your teleios expression: body, mind, speech, work, rest, sexuality, money — as stewardship domains, not identity sources.

### Day 4 — Household map
What would one person in your household experience in 90 days if your vision were true? Be behavioral, not poetic.

### Day 5 — Epistemic check
For your top three aims, answer: How do I know this is design and not fashion?

### Day 6 — Boundary
Write three things your vision **forbids** (not only what it desires).

### Day 7 — Vision statement
Complete:

> Because I am teleios by design, I will see ______, refuse ______, and live accordingly for the next 12 weeks by ______.

### Evidence rubric (Days 1–7)

For each day’s work, mark:

- **Claimed** — what you wrote as vision language
- **Tested** — which of the four epistemic tests it passed or failed
- **Bounded** — what this vision now forbids
- **Lived** — one act today that agrees with the vision (not a future act)

Sight without a lived act is still fantasy with better vocabulary.
