# Week 11: Sexual Stewardship
## Desire Under Design — Not Preference as Ontology

> **Week 11 of 12** | 8–10 hours | Design Stewardship  
> **Sources:** Course 02 boundaries teaching · Genesis 2:24–25 · 1 Corinthians 6:16–20 · Matthew 5:27–28 · better whole

## The Central Truth

Desire is real. Desire is powerful. Desire **does not rewrite ontology**.  
Sexuality belongs to design: sexed nature, covenant order, one-flesh fidelity. Stewardship governs desire as design responsibility — not as unlimited self-expression and not as a private toy.

You do not become human by sexual technique or by sexual repression-as-identity. You are human by design. You practice loyalty from that design.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Design installs limits (Course 02 recall)

Because humans are designed, morality and sexuality are not open-ended preference zones.

- **Moral limits** — right/wrong answer to design (truth, fidelity, responsibility, non-harm as covenant order), not “my truth.”
- **Sexual limits** — sexed nature, covenant order, one-flesh fidelity. Desire is real; desire does not rewrite ontology.
- **Flourishing** — living inside limits produces fruit; crossing them damages persons, covenant, household.

Flourishing is the fruit of the rule, not the manufacturer of the rule.

### 2) Desire as covenant engine — rightly ordered

The stub said: desire is a covenant engine, not a private toy. Keep that.

Rightly ordered desire:

- moves toward one-flesh fidelity (married) or chastity/loyalty to future covenant (unmarried),
- refuses pornographic formation of imagination,
- refuses using persons as appliances.

Misordered desire:

- trains the mind to consume without covenant,
- treats spouse as regulator or vending machine,
- treats abstinence seasons as identity (“I am my frustration”).

### 3) Better whole sexually

½ + ½ fantasy: “I need someone to complete my sexual self.”  
Better whole: two whole persons enter covenant; sex expresses union; it does not assemble personhood.

Also refuse: spouse-as-technician for your ungoverned appetite, or spouse-as-enemy of your desire. Affective care and conative loyalty are yours to steward.

### 4) Sexual loyalty code — how to write it

A loyalty code is not a kink contract and not a shame manifesto. It is design vows:

1. **Who I am** — teleios, sexed, image-bearing (not my urges)
2. **What I will not practice** — name compromises without graphic entertainment
3. **What I will practice** — loyalty habits (eyes, devices, speech, schedule, repair)
4. **Whom I answer to** — God, covenant partner, and one accountability structure where wise
5. **Repair** — same-day ownership if lines are crossed (Week 10)

### 5) Compromise: cue, payoff, replacement

Same habit logic as Week 6:

| Field | Example structure |
|-------|-------------------|
| Compromise | (name specifically to yourself/accountability) |
| Cue | fatigue, conflict, boredom, secrecy window |
| Payoff | numbness, fantasy control, revenge |
| Cost | trust, clarity, one-flesh honor |
| Replacement | walk, call, sleep, repair conversation, device rule |

Without replacement, “try harder” fails.

### 6) Imagination and Matthew 5:27–28

Adultery of the heart is not “noticing beauty.” It is **entertaining possession** of what is not yours under covenant. Steward imagination: refuse rehearsals of betrayal.

Mental attribute (Week 3) applies: premise → fantasy → act risk. Interrupt at premise.

### 7) Married and unmarried tracks

**Married:** loyalty includes pursuing one-flesh good, refusing weaponized withholding as contempt, refusing coercion, repairing sexual hurts with Week 10 honesty.

**Unmarried:** loyalty includes chastity as design honor — not as inferior waiting room identity — and refusing “practice partners” as ontology experiments.

Both tracks refuse pornographic formation.

### 8) Analogy: fire in the fireplace

Desire is **fire**. Fireplace (covenant design) warms the house. Fire on the floor burns it.  
You do not pretend fire is fake. You do not relocate the fireplace to match every spark.

### 9) Shame vs conviction

Conviction: “This act violated design; repair and re-align.”  
Shame-as-ontology: “I am trash; hide forever.”  
Week 4: govern feeling; Week 10: repair. Do not confuse them.

### 10) Pornography and formation of appetite

Pornography is not “visual preference.” It is training: consumption without covenant, persons as product, secrecy as habitat.

Stewardship response:

- name it as compromise (not as identity),
- cut access cues (devices, times, places),
- replace payoff (numbness) with true needs (sleep, repair, presence),
- accountability that asks for evidence, not slogans.

### 11) One-flesh ethics in brief

Genesis 2:24–25 and 1 Cor 6:16 bind sexual union to profound joining. Casual sex is not a neutral experiment; it is ontological confusion practiced with bodies. Married sex is not a chore chart only — it is covenant speech in the body, requiring mutual honor, not coercion.

### 12) Continuity into Week 12

Sexual loyalty logs are readiness evidence. Romantic intensity is not.

### 13) Teleios starting point (sexuality edition)

Desire does not mint personhood. You are teleios by design — sexed, covenantal, responsible — before any loyalty log is perfect. Sexual compromise is likeness failure to be repaired; it is not proof you are unfinished ontology. Sexual performance or repression-as-identity both fail the starting point.

Illegal: “My desire is my truth.”  
Illegal: “I am my frustration / my purity streak.”  
Legal: “I am teleios; desire is real; design limits govern practice today.”

Better whole sexually: a spouse is not the missing half of your sexual self, not a permanent regulator of appetite, and not an enemy of desire. Two whole persons enter covenant; sex expresses union; it does not assemble being.

### 14) Attributes and faculties under desire load

- **Mental attribute** — premises that entertain possession (Matthew 5:27–28)
- **Emotional attribute** — surge, loneliness, contempt, numbness
- **Behavioral attribute** — whether eyes, hands, devices obey
- **Temperamental attribute** — secrecy windows, withdrawal, charm splits
- **Affective faculty** — care for the other’s good with forethought (not use-as-appliance)
- **Conative faculty** — loyalty commitment when desire peaks
- **Cognitive faculty** — honest naming of compromise without euphemism fog
- **Psychomotor faculty** — body present in covenant or removed from cue environments

Collapsing “sexuality problems” into preference talk erases this map.

### 15) Worked example — device cue

Compromise: late phone in bed → pornographic formation.  
Cue: fatigue + conflict residue + secrecy window.  
Payoff: numbness; fantasy control.  
Cost: one-flesh honor, clarity, trust.  
Replacement: charger outside bedroom; 10-minute walk or written true sentence; sleep; same-day ownership if the line is crossed.  
Accountability: wise check that asks for evidence, not slogans.  
Ontology: “I am not becoming human by winning a streak; I am stewarding design.”

### 16) Evidence bar for Week 11

Produce: written loyalty code, one compromise with cue/payoff/replacement, Days 1–7 of the 14-day log, one device/context rule kept, one accountability touchpoint. Romantic intensity, tears, or private vows without logs do not count as readiness evidence for Week 12.
