## The Central Truth

Desire is a **test of mastery**, not a justification for chaos.  
Impulse is desire accelerated. Self-command intercepts the chain before act — without pretending desire is unreal and without treating desire as ontology (“I am my urge”).

You buffet the body (1 Cor 9:27) as stewardship of a temple you already are — not as self-hatred manufacturing holiness. You are **teleios** by design; desire does not rewrite that fact, and shame does not invent a better ontology by scorched-earth self-talk. Practice ≠ manufacture being.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Two false gospels

1. **Preference gospel** — “If I want it, it is authentic / allowed.”  
2. **Shame gospel** — “If I want it, I am corrupt as a person.”

Canon: desire can be misordered while image remains; governance is likeness. Preference gospel makes desire ontology. Shame gospel makes desire identity death. Teleios grammar refuses both.

### 2) Teleios grammar for desire week

| Illegal desire grammar | Teleios desire grammar |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| I am my urge | Urge tests stewardship; I remain design-state |
| Wanting = permission | Wanting = data; design = law |
| Wanting = damnation | Wanting = test; act is judged, person is image |
| Partner must stop me | Better whole — accountability helps; I own the pause |
| Buffeting invents holiness | Buffeting expresses temple stewardship |

If your impulse log becomes a worth-scoreboard or a self-erasure diary, stop and rewrite the vow.

### 3) Impulse chain

Trigger → Sensation → Premise → Urge peak → Act / Redirect

Interrupt points:

- **Premise** (Mental — Course 06 continuity): test the story desire tells  
- **Peak** (pause; delay; leave context): refuse courtroom at surge  
- **Act** (replacement from Week 2 systems): run the pre-decided rail  

Ratiocination:

- Premise 1: Desire is real and powerful in a teleios person under load.
- Premise 2: Desire is not ontology and not automatic permission.
- Premise 3: Uninterrupted impulse trains chaos into household and body.
- Conclusion: Mastery is early interrupt + redirect from design — not numbness, not preference.

### 4) Pause-and-redirect routine

1. Name: “This is impulse X.”  
2. Pause: 90 seconds / leave room / lock screen  
3. Test: design-allowed or design-forbidden?  
4. Redirect: pre-written replacement act  
5. Log: one line  

No debate club during peak. Debate after redirect. Peak is a bad courtroom; pre-decide when calm.

### 5) Desire stewardship vow

Structure:

> I am teleios by design. Desire does not name me. When ______ triggers, I will pause and ______ instead of ______. If I fail, I will repair by ______ within ______. I will not recruit a better half to be my bit and rein.

Align with Course 06 sexual loyalty where relevant; include food, spend, speech, screen, sexual — selective mastery is preference gospel in costume.

### 6) Paul’s “I keep under my body”

Not body hatred. Rule of the person over appetite. Psychomotor and Behavioral in covenant with Conative commitment. The body is not enemy; ungoverned appetite is sabotage of design responsibility.

### 7) Analogy: horse and bit

Desire is a strong horse. Self-command is bit and rein.  
No bit → chaos. Killing the horse → numbness false virtue. Rein → directed power. Teleios persons do not become holy by pretending they have no horse.

### 8) Better whole

“Stop me when I impulsively…” can be accountability.  
“You are responsible for my impulses” is fusion. Own the pause. A spouse is not your permanent brake pedal. Affective care from others is gift; Behavioral interrupt remains yours.

### 9) Spend, speech, screen — same chain

Impulse is not only sexual or culinary. Mid-argument speech, revenge purchases, and “just one more” scroll use the same chain. Apply pause-and-redirect across domains so you do not become selectively “masterful” while leaking contempt or debt.

### 10) Delay as weapon

Sometimes the redirect is simply delay: 10 minutes, leave the room, drink water, text a witness. Delay is not denial of desire; it is refusal to let peak write law. Delay buys Cognitive space without pretending Affective signal never existed.

### 11) Slip without ontology collapse

A slip is an act. Repair is ownership + rail. Collapse (“I am my failure”) restarts binge cycles. Course 06 repair logic applies: same-day own–apologize–act where another was harmed. Miss protocol from Week 2 continues here: log reason → repair → continue — no Monday mythology.

### 12) Faculties under impulse load

| Faculty / attribute | Stewardship move |
|---------------------|------------------|
| Mental | Name premise desire is selling |
| Emotional | Feel urge without rewriting identity |
| Conative | Keep the pause vow |
| Behavioral | Execute redirect / refuse act |
| Affective / desire | Desire reports; does not rule |
| Psychomotor | Leave the room; move the body |

### 13) Language discipline

- “I had to — I wanted it that badly.” → “Wanting peaked; I still choose under design.”
- “I’m disgusting for wanting this.” → “Urge is data; I remain teleios; I will redirect.”
- “If they really loved me they’d control this for me.” → “Witnesses help; I own the bit as a better whole.”

### 14) Continuity into Week 4

Intercepting impulse once is skill. Intercepting across days is consistency. Week 4 builds the metronome so pause-and-redirect is not a lucky one-off but reliable fruit a household can trust.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Impulse as authenticity
Riley acts every urge and calls it honesty. Chaos follows in money, speech, and intimacy. Preference gospel wears “realness.” Fruit: household bracing; trust erosion; teleios unused.

### Case B — Impulse as damnation
Ava hides urges, then binge-fails. Shame ontology blocked early interrupt; secrecy became the system. Fruit: spiral, fusion apology after slip, partner as temporary regulator.

### Case C — Mastery under test from teleios
Jordan logs triggers, pauses, redirects to walk + water, repairs slips same day. Desire remains; chaos shrinks. Vow names teleios and better whole. Fruit: calm under peak; Week 4 consistency becomes possible.
