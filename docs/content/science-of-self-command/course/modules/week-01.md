## The Central Truth

You cannot lead others until you can lead yourself.  
Self-governance is not becoming a new species of human. It is the teleios person accepting responsibility for appetite, attention, speech, and action — without recruiting a better half to supply will.

A city without walls is the proverb’s image (Prov 25:28): ungoverned spirit is exposure. Walls do not invent the city; they protect what is already there.

## Week Overview

### Core Question
What does it mean to lead yourself as a teleios person?

### Key Insight
Self-neglect is often baptized as busy, spiritual, or “authentic.” Cost shows up in household trust, health, work fruit, and covenant readiness. Naming the cost is the first act of command.

### Learning Outcomes

## Core Teaching

### 1) Self-governance defined

**Self-governance** = responsible rule of your own powers and attributes under design truth.

It includes:

- deciding and keeping commitments (Conative)
- testing premises (Cognitive / Mental)
- showing up in body (Psychomotor)
- caring with forethought — including for your future self and household (Affective)
- self-control in action and reaction (Behavioral)

It excludes:

- manufacturing worth through control
- dominating others to feel governed
- using a spouse as external hard drive for your will

### 2) Ontology vs governance

| Domain | Truth |
|--------|--------|
| Ontology | You are teleios by design |
| Governance | You may still live ungoverned |
| Repair | Governance failure requires rails, not identity deletion |

Ratiocination:

- Premise 1: Leadership of others assumes predictable self-rule.
- Premise 2: Marriage/family multiply ungoverned patterns.
- Conclusion: Self-command is prerequisite deployment, not optional polish.

### 3) The cost of self-neglect

Inventory costs in four columns:

1. **Body** — sleep, food, health debt  
2. **Household** — trust, presence, repair delay  
3. **Work/calling** — unfinished vows, reputation  
4. **Inner life** — Mental loops, Emotional surge, secrecy  

If you cannot name costs, you are still romanticizing drift.

### 4) Better whole

“My partner keeps me in line” is outsourced governance.  
Support and accountability are good. Substitution is not. You remain the first leader and first follower of your own life.

### 5) Analogy: captain, not cargo

You are the **captain** of your vessel by design responsibility.  
Mood, feed, and appetite try to make you **cargo**.  
Self-governance is refusing to ship yourself as freight to every impulse.

### 6) Governance check-in (daily)

Morning (3 minutes):

1. What am I responsible to govern today? (one domain)
2. What is the first visible act?
3. What would sabotage look like?

Evening (3 minutes):

1. Did I govern or drift?
2. Cost if drifted?
3. Repair within 24 hours?

### 7) Self-control vs self-hatred

Harshness is not holiness. Self-hatred collapses ontology (“I am trash”) and then calls the collapse discipline. True self-command is firm and clean: name the act, install the rail, repair the miss — without rewriting your design status.

If your “governance” requires contempt as fuel, it will burn out or become cruelty to household. Contempt is not Conative strength.

### 8) Public leadership, private walls

Many people lead teams, ministries, or classrooms while living as an open city (Prov 25:28). The mismatch is not a branding problem; it is a trust problem. Households and teams eventually discover whether your walls exist.

Self-governance week is not anti-leadership. It is anti-hypocrisy: build walls before you demand followership.

### 9) Faculties under one captain

Ungoverned life often looks like faculty civil war:

- Conative starts vows the Cognitive never stress-tested
- Affective surges override Mental premises
- Psychomotor shows up late while Behavioral explodes

Self-governance is the captain’s coordination — not silencing faculties, ordering them.

### 10) What this week is not

Not a productivity challenge. Not a guilt campaign. Not “become someone else.”  
It is accepting that teleios persons still drift, and drift has costs you must stop romanticizing.

### 11) Continuity into Week 2

Check-ins without systems become journaling theater. Week 2 installs irrigation so mood is not the carrier of command.

## Case Contrasts

Eli coaches others and cannot keep sleep or speech. Authority without self-rule is theater.
