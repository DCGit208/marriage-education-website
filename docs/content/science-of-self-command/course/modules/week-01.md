## The Central Truth

You cannot lead a household if you cannot lead yourself.  
Self-governance is not becoming a new species of human. It is the **teleios** person accepting responsibility for appetite, attention, speech, and action — without recruiting a better half to supply will.

A city without walls is the proverb’s image (Prov 25:28): ungoverned spirit is exposure. Walls do not invent the city; they protect what is already there. Practice does not manufacture being. Practice aligns expression under load.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Self-governance defined

**Self-governance** = responsible rule of your own powers and attributes under design truth.

It includes:

- deciding and keeping commitments (Conative)
- testing premises (Cognitive / Mental)
- showing up in body (Psychomotor)
- caring with forethought — including for your future self and household (Affective)
- self-control in action and reaction (Behavioral)

It excludes:

- manufacturing worth through control
- dominating others to feel governed
- using a spouse, parent, or boss as external hard drive for your will

### 2) Teleios grammar (non-negotiable for Course 07)

In this canon, teleios / perfect = **design-state / full development** — the human as designed, not a flawless streak record.

| Chase / control grammar | Practice-from-teleios grammar |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Become worthy by dominating myself | Express design under responsible rule |
| Mood as meter of “discipline” | Rails as meter of stewardship |
| Self-hatred as fuel | Responsibility as fuel |
| Partner as outsourced will | Better whole — I remain first captain |
| Practice invents the self | Practice reveals and aligns the self |

If your private sentences still sound like the left column, you will weaponize Weeks 2–5 against yourself or against your household.

### 3) Ontology vs governance

| Domain | Truth |
|--------|--------|
| Ontology | You are teleios by design |
| Governance | You may still live ungoverned |
| Repair | Governance failure requires rails, not identity deletion |

Ratiocination:

- Premise 1: What a person *is* precedes what a person *practices*.
- Premise 2: Leadership of others assumes predictable self-rule; marriage/family multiply ungoverned patterns.
- Premise 3: Practice that assumes unfinished ontology turns self-command into self-creation religion.
- Conclusion: Self-command is prerequisite deployment from teleios identity — not optional polish and not worth manufacture.

### 4) The cost of self-neglect

Inventory costs in four columns:

1. **Body** — sleep, food, health debt  
2. **Household** — trust, presence, repair delay  
3. **Work/calling** — unfinished vows, reputation  
4. **Inner life** — Mental loops, Emotional surge, secrecy  

If you cannot name costs, you are still romanticizing drift as freedom or “high capacity.”

### 5) Better whole

“My partner keeps me in line” is outsourced governance.  
Support and accountability are good. Substitution is not. You remain the first leader and first follower of your own life. Course 08–10 will forbid better-half fusion in relationship speech; this week forbids it at the will root.

### 6) Analogy: captain, not cargo

You are the **captain** of your vessel by design responsibility.  
Mood, feed, and appetite try to make you **cargo**.  
Self-governance is refusing to ship yourself as freight to every impulse. Faculties are crew; they are not the owner of the helm.

### 7) Governance check-in (daily)

Morning (3 minutes):

1. What am I responsible to govern today? (one domain)
2. What is the first visible act?
3. What would sabotage look like?

Evening (3 minutes):

1. Did I govern or drift?
2. Cost if drifted?
3. Repair within 24 hours?

### 8) Self-control vs self-hatred

Harshness is not holiness. Self-hatred collapses ontology (“I am trash”) and then calls the collapse discipline. True self-command is firm and clean: name the act, install the rail, repair the miss — without rewriting your design status.

If your “governance” requires contempt as fuel, it will burn out or become cruelty to household. Contempt is not Conative strength. `Be holy` is state language; misery is not the manufacturing plant.

### 9) Public leadership, private walls

Many people lead teams, ministries, or classrooms while living as an open city (Prov 25:28). The mismatch is not a branding problem; it is a trust problem. Households and teams eventually discover whether your walls exist.

Self-governance week is not anti-leadership. It is anti-hypocrisy: build walls before you demand followership. Course 07 exists so that later relationship courses do not install communication and repair on an ungoverned person.

### 10) Faculties under one captain

Ungoverned life often looks like faculty civil war:

- Conative starts vows the Cognitive never stress-tested
- Affective surges override Mental premises
- Psychomotor shows up late while Behavioral explodes

Self-governance is the captain’s coordination — not silencing faculties, ordering them. Behavioral attribute in disciplined operation is the visible fruit; Conative commitment is the vow behind it.

### 11) Language discipline

- “I need someone to keep me accountable forever.” → “I will use witnesses; I remain captain.”
- “I’m broken, so governance is for other people.” → “I am teleios; this pattern missed design; I install a rail.”
- “When I become disciplined enough…” → “I am teleios; today I govern ______.”

### 12) Continuity into Week 2

Check-ins without systems become journaling theater. Week 2 installs irrigation so mood is not the carrier of command.

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Public leader, private drift
Eli coaches others and cannot keep sleep or speech. Authority without self-rule is theater. Household walks on eggshells; teams eventually discover the open city. Fruit: brittle charisma, delayed repair, quiet contempt at home.

### Case B — Shame paralysis
Maya sees neglect and concludes she is worthless. Ontology collapse blocks governance. She waits to “feel like a real person” before installing walls. Fruit: spiral, secrecy, partner recruited as regulator.

### Case C — Owned governance from teleios
Noah names screen neglect, costs to marriage, installs check-in + device curfew. He does not claim a new self — he claims walls for the city he already is. Witness reviews Day 7. Fruit: calm responsibility; better whole speech; readiness for Week 2 systems.
