You are your **first follower** and **first leader**.

## Core Teaching

### 1) First follower

If you will not follow your own stated rails, you are not leading yourself. You are performing intention.

Self-leadership means:

- you keep MDS (Week 4)
- you run systems (Week 2)
- you interrupt impulse (Week 3)
- you check in (Week 1)

### 2) Self-Leadership Code — structure

Keep short enough to memorize under load:

1. Ontology line (teleios by design)  
2. Governance domains (body, speech, time, desire, repair)  
3. Non-negotiable daily acts  
4. Load rules (what changes when fatigued — never “nothing”)  
5. Repair standard  
6. Better-whole line (no outsourced will)  
7. Witness / review cadence  
8. Covenant-facing fruit marker  

Example rule shape:

> I do not negotiate sleep after midnight except emergency — and I name emergencies honestly.

### 3) Accountability upgrade

Accountability is anti-delusion architecture (Course 05). Upgrade means:

- specific metric (not “be better”)
- scheduled check (not vibes)
- truth permission (witness may contradict you)

### 4) Role models without idolatry

Choose a model for **practices**, not aura:

- What rail do they keep?
- What do they repair openly?
- What load rule do they use?

Transfer one practice into your code this week.

### 5) Leadership log

Daily:

| Act of self-leadership | Domain | Load level | Kept? | Repair |
|------------------------|--------|------------|-------|--------|

Leadership acts are not speeches. They are kept rails and repaired misses.

### 6) Link to covenant readiness

Course 06 asked for readiness evidence. Course 07 supplies the self-command layer that makes readiness durable. If the code is fiction, do not claim readiness.

### 7) Analogy: constitution

Your code is a **personal constitution**. Feelings are parties in parliament. Constitution constrains parties. Amend deliberately in weekly review — not mid-impulse.

### 8) Final ratiocination

- Premise 1: Households and teams suffer when leaders lack self-rule.
- Premise 2: Self-rule is evidenced by code + logs under load.
- Premise 3: Practice does not invent the person; it proves stewardship.
- Conclusion: Lead yourself with a code — then your leadership of others is not hypocrisy.

### 9) Soft codes and cruel codes

A soft code is slogans without metrics. A cruel code is perfectionism that treats misses as identity death. Aim for firm, few, repairable rules — a constitution, not a gulag and not a mood board.

### 10) 30-day plan (minimum)

1. Keep MDS daily  
2. One domain system from Week 2  
3. Impulse log three peak days per week  
4. Weekly witness (15 minutes)  
5. One household fruit marker others can notice  

### 11) From Course 07 into School 1 relationship track

Self-command is the hinge into relationship courses. Heart of Relationship and later tracks assume a person who can govern speech, desire, and repair without outsourcing will. If the code is fiction, pause progression and reinstall rails.

### 12) Closing ontology line

You finish this course as you began School 1: teleios by design. Self-command did not create you. It trained you to live as who you are under load — mindful, educated, accountable — before you ask a family to follow.
