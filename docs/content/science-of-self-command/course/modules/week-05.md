## Core Teaching

### 1) First follower

If you will not follow your own stated rails, you are not leading yourself. You are performing intention.

Self-leadership means:

- you keep MDS (Week 4)
- you run systems (Week 2)
- you interrupt impulse (Week 3)
- you check in (Week 1)

Leadership of self is not a new personality. It is the integration of Weeks 1–4 into a constitution you will actually obey.

### 2) Teleios grammar (capstone non-negotiable)

| Illegal self-leadership grammar | Teleios self-leadership grammar |
|---------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Code invents the adult | Code stewards the adult already given |
| Soft slogans without metrics | Firm, few, repairable rules |
| Cruel perfectionism as holiness | Miss → repair without identity death |
| Household follows my brand | Household trusts my kept word |
| Spouse is my will | Better whole — first follower is me |

Ratiocination:

- Premise 1: Households and teams suffer when leaders lack self-rule.
- Premise 2: Self-rule is evidenced by code + logs under load — not speeches.
- Premise 3: Practice does not invent the person; it proves stewardship.
- Conclusion: Lead yourself with a code from teleios identity — then leadership of others is not hypocrisy.

### 3) Self-Leadership Code — structure

Keep short enough to memorize under load:

1. Ontology line (teleios by design)  
2. Governance domains (body, speech, time, desire, repair)  
3. Non-negotiable daily acts (MDS)  
4. Load rules (what changes when fatigued — never “nothing”)  
5. Repair standard  
6. Better-whole line (no outsourced will)  
7. Witness / review cadence  
8. Covenant-facing fruit marker  

Example rule shape:

> I do not negotiate sleep after midnight except emergency — and I name emergencies honestly.

Soft codes are slogans without metrics. Cruel codes treat misses as identity death. Aim for firm, few, repairable rules — a constitution, not a gulag and not a mood board.

### 4) Accountability upgrade

Accountability is anti-delusion architecture (Course 05). Upgrade means:

- specific metric (not “be better”)
- scheduled check (not vibes)
- truth permission (witness may contradict you)

Accountability that never becomes self-leadership remains adolescence. The upgrade serves your first-follower status; it does not replace it.

### 5) Role models without idolatry

Choose a model for **practices**, not aura:

- What rail do they keep?
- What do they repair openly?
- What load rule do they use?

Transfer one practice into your code this week. Celebrity cosplay is Affective fantasy; transferable rails are Behavioral stewardship.

### 6) Leadership log

Daily:

| Act of self-leadership | Domain | Load level | Kept? | Repair |
|------------------------|--------|------------|-------|--------|

Leadership acts are not speeches. They are kept rails and repaired misses. Log under fatigue days especially — those are the evidence that matter for covenant weight.

### 7) Link to covenant readiness

Course 06 asked for readiness evidence. Course 07 supplies the self-command layer that makes readiness durable. If the code is fiction, do not claim readiness. Heart of Relationship (Course 08) will install design, trust, communication, and repair — and will assume you can keep word without better-half fusion as will architecture.

### 8) Analogy: constitution

Your code is a **personal constitution**. Feelings are parties in parliament. Constitution constrains parties. Amend deliberately in weekly review — not mid-impulse. Constitutions protect a people already present; they do not invent the nation. Teleios persons need constitutions; constitutions do not mint teleios.

### 9) Soft codes and cruel codes — expanded

Soft code failure: beautiful PDF, zero metrics, no witness.  
Cruel code failure: twenty rules, contempt fuel, identity collapse on miss.  
Living constitution: 8–12 rules, MDS, miss protocol, weekly witness, one household fruit marker others can notice.

### 10) 30-day plan (minimum)

1. Keep MDS daily  
2. One domain system from Week 2  
3. Impulse log three peak days per week  
4. Weekly witness (15 minutes)  
5. One household fruit marker others can notice  

Thirty days is Law of Time without delay excuse. Start imperfect; review weekly; amend deliberately.

### 11) From Course 07 into School 1 relationship track

Self-command is the hinge into relationship courses. Heart of Relationship and later tracks assume a person who can govern speech, desire, and repair without outsourcing will. If the code is fiction, pause progression and reinstall rails. Do not smuggle ungoverned patterns into “relationship work” and call it formation.

### 12) Language discipline

- “I’m a leader because people follow me.” → “I lead myself first; then claims are non-hypocritical.”
- “This code will make me finally worthy.” → “I am teleios; this code expresses responsibility under load.”
- “Keep me honest forever.” → “Witness on schedule; I remain first follower as a better whole.”

### 13) Closing ontology line

You finish this course as you began School 1: teleios by design. Self-command did not create you. It trained you to live as who you are under load — mindful, educated, accountable — before you ask a family to follow. Better whole remains: no spouse as outsourced will.
