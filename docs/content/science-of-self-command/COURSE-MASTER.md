## Course thesis

You cannot lead a household if you cannot lead yourself.  
Self-command is the responsible governance of appetite, impulse, attention, and action — the **Behavioral attribute** in disciplined operation, powered by **Conative** commitment, informed by **Cognitive** reason, embodied in **Psychomotor** presence, and guided by **Affective** care.

This course does **not** manufacture teleios.  
You are teleios by design. Self-command is likeness: living as who you are under load, before you ask others to trust your leadership.

---

## Doctrine guardrails

- Teleios/maturity = design-state; practice does not create being.
- Self-control is stewardship of the Behavioral attribute — not holiness manufacture (`be holy` is state).
- Desire tests mastery; desire does not rewrite ontology (Course 02 / 06 W11 continuity).
- Better whole: no spouse/parent/leader as outsourced will.
- Discipline is system + commitment, not mood.
- Consistency expresses maturity under Law of Repetition/Time; streaks do not invent the person.
- “Science” here means ordered, evidence-based stewardship — not reductionism that denies image.
