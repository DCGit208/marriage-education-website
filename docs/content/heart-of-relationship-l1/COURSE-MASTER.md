## Course thesis

Relationships are **design systems**, not emotional accidents.  
Level 1 installs the heart of relational intelligence: what a relationship *is*, how trust and safety are structured, how communication carries design, and how repair prevents collapse.

This course does **not** manufacture teleios persons by pairing them.  
Two whole persons steward relatedness. Marriage and friendship deploy wholeness; they do not assemble missing halves.

---

## Doctrine guardrails

- Teleios/maturity = design-state; practice does not create being.
- Better whole, not better half — no fusion ontology.
- Relationship is ordered relatedness under design, not vibe coincidence.
- Trust is structure (predictability + truth + repair), not a mood.
- Communication is stewardship of speech and presence (Course 06 W9 / Course 07 continuity).
- Repair is maintenance of covenant/friendship order — not shame theater and not fusion apology.
- Image is given; relational identity is not invented by chemistry.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of 4 weeks, the learner will:

1. Write a Relationship Design Statement (purposes + design limits).
2. Install one trust-building ritual with daily evidence.
3. Replace two destructive communication patterns; complete a no-interruption challenge.
4. Create and practice a Repair Ritual in one live relationship.
