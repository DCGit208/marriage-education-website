## The Central Truth

Boundaries protect **honor** and preserve relationship health.  
A fence defines space and safety; it is not an act of war. Boundary-less “love” is often access addiction. Boundary-as-weapon is often fear dressed as virtue.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do boundaries protect honor without hostility?

### Key Insight
Honor means treating persons as image-bearing, type-fitting, and non-consumable. Boundaries are the practical edges of that honor — for you and for them.

### Learning Outcomes
- Identify two boundaries to establish  
- Audit where you violated others’ boundaries  
- Write an Honor Code for relationships  
- Practice honoring boundaries in two relationships  
- Pass a Boundaries & Honor Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Boundary defined

**Boundary** = a design-aligned limit on access, time, speech, body, money, or decision rights — stated and kept.

Not: silent resentment. Not: punishment. Not: identity wall (“I am my fence”).

### 2) Honor defined

**Honor** = recognizing the other’s dignity and type-limits; refusing to use, invade, or demean.

Honor without boundaries becomes porous. Boundaries without honor become cruelty.

### 3) Two boundaries to establish

Write each as:

> When ______, I will ______. If crossed, I will ______.

Examples: device-free meals; no late confessions that ambush sleep; no third-party gossip about the bond; work hours vs family hours.

### 4) Violation audit (yours)

Where have you:

- Pushed past “no”?  
- Used urgency to override limits?  
- Treated their private life as content?  
- Entered rooms (literal or digital) uninvited?  

Own two specifically. Repair using Course 08 ritual.

### 5) Honor Code (short)

1. I name limits cleanly  
2. I keep my word on access  
3. I do not punish honesty with retaliation  
4. I do not invade to feel close  
5. I honor type (Week 1)  
6. Better whole — I do not fuse  

### 6) Analogy: fence

Fences make neighbors possible. No fence → trampling. Fortress with no gate → isolation cult. Design wants gated honor.

### 7) Better whole

“If you loved me you’d have no boundaries” is fusion theology.  
Love with edges is adult relatedness.

### 8) Practice this week

In two relationships: (1) state one boundary without apology fog; (2) honor one of theirs visibly (thank + keep).

### 9) Continuity

Boundaries create the field where power must be held responsibly (Week 3).
