# Week 3: Power & Responsibility
## Balance the Seesaw — Or Someone Falls

> **Week 3 of 6** | 8–10 hours | Governance of Power  
> **Sources:** Week 2 · Romans 13 principles (stewardship frame) · better whole · Course 07

---

## The Central Truth

Power without responsibility creates **abuse**.  
Responsibility without power creates **resentment**.  
Healthy relatedness balances the seesaw: authority matched to accountability, load matched to voice.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How is power held with responsibility?

### Key Insight
Every bond has power dynamics — money, knowledge, charisma, decision rights, emotional leverage. Pretending “we’re equal in every way” often hides unowned power. Name it; yoke it to responsibility.

### Learning Outcomes
- List relationship power dynamics in key bonds  
- Name one area of abused or neglected responsibility  
- Create a power-with-responsibility action plan  
- Run a 3-day power audit  
- Pass a Power & Responsibility Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Power defined (relational)

**Power** = capacity to influence outcomes, access, mood, or decisions in a bond.

Sources: role, resources, expertise, attractiveness/attention, narrative control, gatekeeping of kids/money/sex/time.

Power is not evil. Ungoverned power is.

### 2) Responsibility defined

**Responsibility** = duty to use power for design-aligned good of the bond and persons — truth, protection, provision of what your role owes, repair when you harm.

### 3) Two failure modes

| Mode | Pattern | Fruit |
|------|---------|-------|
| Power − responsibility | Domination, coercion, silence control | Abuse climate |
| Responsibility − power | Load without voice or decision rights | Resentment, collapse |

### 4) Dynamics inventory

For your top bond(s):

- Who decides money / calendar / social access?  
- Who can end conflict by leaving?  
- Who holds informational power?  
- Who performs unpaid emotional labor?  

Write without spin.

### 5) Action plan (power with responsibility)

Template:

> Where I hold ______ power, I will ______ (responsible acts).  
> Where I carry ______ responsibility, I need ______ (voice/decision right).  
> This week I will redistribute or clarify: ______.

### 6) Analogy: seesaw

If one side sits with all weight and no care for the other, someone hits dirt. Balance is not identical mass in every domain; it is fair matching of load and leverage under type (marriage ≠ colleague).

### 7) Better whole

Using a partner as power battery (“regulate me / decide everything for me”) is fusion. Adult bonds share power with clear roles — not parentalize a spouse.

### 8) Three-day power audit

Each day log:

| Moment | Power I used | Responsible? | Power I lacked | Resentment? |
|--------|--------------|--------------|----------------|-------------|

### 9) Continuity

Ungoverned power explodes in conflict patterns (Week 4). Owned power can pause and ask.
