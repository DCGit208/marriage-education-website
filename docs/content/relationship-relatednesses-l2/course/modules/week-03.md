## The Central Truth

Power without responsibility creates **abuse**.  
Responsibility without power creates **resentment**.  
Healthy relatedness balances the seesaw: authority matched to accountability, load matched to voice.

You are **teleios** by design. Power does not invent your worth; responsibility does not manufacture your being. Practice yokes leverage to duty because persons already are callable — not because domination or martyrdom creates a “real self.” Better wholes share load under type; they do not parentalize a spouse or battery-charge a friend.

How is power held with responsibility — when teleios forbids both soft tyranny and silent martyrdom?

Every bond has power dynamics — money, knowledge, charisma, decision rights, emotional leverage. Pretending “we’re equal in every way” often hides unowned power. Name it; yoke it to responsibility. Week 2 fences created the field; Week 3 governs leverage inside that field. Course 10 conflict mastery will fail if Week 3 leaves power unnamed.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Power defined (relational)

**Power** = capacity to influence outcomes, access, mood, or decisions in a bond.

Sources: role, resources, expertise, attractiveness/attention, narrative control, gatekeeping of kids/money/sex/time.

Power is not evil. Ungoverned power is. Teleios persons can hold power without making it ontology (“I am powerful, therefore I am more human”).

### 2) Responsibility defined

**Responsibility** = duty to use power for design-aligned good of the bond and persons — truth, protection, provision of what your role owes, repair when you harm (Course 08).

Responsibility without voice is not holiness; it is a seesaw with one side missing. Conative load without Mental voice and Behavioral decision rights breeds contempt.

### 3) Teleios grammar for the seesaw

| Illegal power grammar | Teleios power grammar |
|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Domination proves strength | Stewardship proves likeness |
| Martyrdom proves love | Matched load and voice prove honor |
| “They complete my will” | I am teleios; I do not outsource Conative life |
| Soft tyranny as “care” | Care with accountability |
| Silent resentment as virtue | Named need for decision rights |

Practice does not manufacture being. Owning power reveals character; it does not create personhood. Abdicating power to feel “humble” can be teleios denial dressed as piety.

### 4) Ratiocination

- Premise 1: Teleios persons already bear design responsibility before any audit.
- Premise 2: Power without duty treats persons as objects; duty without voice treats persons as disposable laborers.
- Premise 3: Design matches load to leverage under type (marriage ≠ colleague).
- Conclusion: Power must be held with responsibility from teleios + better whole, or “leadership” manufactures abuse or resentment.

### 5) Two failure modes

| Mode | Pattern | Fruit |
|------|---------|-------|
| Power − responsibility | Domination, coercion, silence control | Abuse climate |
| Responsibility − power | Load without voice or decision rights | Resentment, collapse |

### 6) Dynamics inventory

For your top bond(s):

- Who decides money / calendar / social access?  
- Who can end conflict by leaving?  
- Who holds informational power?  
- Who performs unpaid emotional labor?  
- Where is Emotional leverage used as Moral force?  

Write without spin. Then ask: am I using power to complete my ontology or to steward a bond between wholes?

### 7) Action plan (power with responsibility)

Template:

> I am teleios by design; this plan does not invent me.  
> Where I hold ______ power, I will ______ (responsible acts).  
> Where I carry ______ responsibility, I need ______ (voice/decision right).  
> Better whole: I will not parentalize or battery-charge ______.  
> This week I will redistribute or clarify: ______.

### 8) Analogy: seesaw

If one side sits with all weight and no care for the other, someone hits dirt. Balance is not identical mass in every domain; it is fair matching of load and leverage under type. Teleios dignity sits on both seats.

### 9) Three-day power audit

Each day log:

| Moment | Power I used | Responsible? | Power I lacked | Resentment? | Teleios check (owning or inventing?) |
|--------|--------------|--------------|----------------|-------------|--------------------------------------|

### 10) Faculties under power load

| Risk | Stewardship |
|------|-------------|
| Narrative control (Mental) | Tell truth; refuse spin |
| Mood leverage (Emotional) | Do not weaponize Affective weather |
| Decision rights (Conative) | Match duty to voice |
| Presence / exit (Behavioral) | No silent abandonment without return time (Week 4 preview) |

Course 07 self-command: you remain responsible for your will even when someone else holds more money or charisma.

### 11) Better whole

Using a partner as power battery (“regulate me / decide everything for me”) is fusion. Adult bonds share power with clear roles — not parentalize a spouse. Recruiting power to feel real is teleios denial.

### 12) Continuity

Ungoverned power explodes in conflict patterns (Week 4). Owned power can pause and ask. Course 08 repair requires the powerful to own impact; Course 10 will intensify this under covenant load.

### 13) What this week is not

Not identical roles in every domain.  
Not anti-authority.  
Not permission for soft tyranny baptized as “headship” or “care.”  
Not martyrdom baptized as “servant love.”

### 14) Language discipline

- “I decide because I care.” → “I decide with accountability and their voice where load sits.”  
- “I do everything; I need nothing.” → “I carry load; I need fitting decision rights.”  
- “Power makes me someone.” → “I am teleios; power is stewardship.”

## Case Contrasts

### Case A — Soft tyrant
Chris controls calendar and money, calls it “care,” skips accountability, and treats the other’s agency as inconvenience. Teleios of the other erased. Fruit: fear, secret rebellion, abuse climate.

### Case B — Overloaded “saint”
Avery does all labor, has no voice, baptizes resentment as holiness, and slowly breeds contempt. Teleios of self denied. Fruit: collapse, quiet hatred, bond as unpaid factory.

### Case C — Matched yoke from teleios
Sam names dynamics, shares decision rights where load sits, audits three days, clarifies one redistribution with Honor Code language, and refuses parentalizing. Fruit: lighter contempt, clearer duty, seesaw that can hold conflict next week.
