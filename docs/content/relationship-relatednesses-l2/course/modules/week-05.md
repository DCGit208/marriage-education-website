# Week 5: Rebuilding Trust
## Brick by Brick — Not Promise by Promise

> **Week 5 of 6** | 8–10 hours | Structural Rebuild  
> **Sources:** Course 08 W2 · Week 4 · Proverbs 25:19 · teleios · better whole

---

## The Central Truth

Trust can be rebuilt — **only through structural acts**, not promises.  
A brick wall rises brick by brick. Speeches without bricks are scaffolding with no wall.

You are **teleios** by design. Rebuilding trust does not manufacture your being, and demanding premature “I trust you” does not invent the other’s ontology. Practice lays bricks because likeness under load is stewardship — not because promises create a new self or buy worth. Better wholes require structure without permanent prosecution and without fake forgiveness deadlines.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How is trust rebuilt brick by brick — when teleios forbids both speech-only rebuild and endless prosecution as identity?

After rupture, the injured party needs pattern evidence under load. The repairer needs humility without fusion self-erasure. Both need a plan with metrics and time. Week 4 bruised trust through patterns; Week 5 rebuilds with architecture Course 08 already named (predictability + truth + repair). ## Core Teaching

### 1) Broken trust inventory

Name breaks without fog:

- What act?  
- What promise/type violated?  
- What impact (known)?  
- What has been tried already?  
- Teleios check: am I using this break to delete my identity or theirs?  

Unnamed breaks cannot be bricked over — only painted. Mental honesty precedes Conative brick-laying.

### 2) Teleios grammar for rebuild

| Illegal trust grammar | Teleios trust grammar |
|-----------------------|------------------------|
| “Trust me because I love you” | “Watch these bricks for 30/60/90 days” |
| Promise as wall | Structural act as brick |
| “I am trash until trusted” | I am teleios; I repair without identity deletion |
| Endless prosecution as identity | Structure + review + wisdom release |
| Fake forgiveness on a deadline | Observable milestones without coercion |

Practice does not manufacture being. Bricks reveal faithfulness; they do not invent personhood. Shame-as-fuel after rupture is chase ontology wearing repentance vocabulary.

### 3) Ratiocination

- Premise 1: Teleios persons remain image-bearing after rupture.
- Premise 2: Speeches without pattern evidence treat trust as mood theater.
- Premise 3: Design rebuilds by predictability, truth, and repair under review (Course 08 W2).
- Conclusion: Trust rebuild must start from teleios + better whole, or “repair” manufactures fusion apology or prosecutor identity.

### 4) Trust Repair Plan

1. Full ownership (Course 08 ritual) — without “I am trash” ontology  
2. Transparency window (agreed scope/time — not endless surveillance as lifestyle)  
3. Three structural behaviors (daily/weekly)  
4. Review cadence (e.g., Sunday 20 minutes)  
5. What “rebuilt enough” looks like (observable)  
6. Limits: no coercion; no fake forgiveness deadlines  
7. Teleios lines: repairer remains a person; injured remains a person — better wholes under structure  

### 5) Three behaviors (examples)

- On-time presence where you previously flaked  
- Proactive update before asked  
- Device/account agreements kept without sulking  
- Same-day repair when you miss (Week 4 continuity)  

Choose three fit to the break. Behavioral evidence is the brick; Emotional warmth may lag — do not demand feelings on your schedule.

### 6) Daily trust log (7 days)

| Day | Brick laid | Miss? | Repair | Other’s observable response | Teleios slip (shame / pressure / prosecution)? |
|-----|------------|-------|--------|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|

### 7) Analogy: brick wall

One brick is not a wall. One perfect week is not a lifetime. Keep laying. Do not demand the injured party “feel trust” on your schedule. Teleios patience is stewardship, not passivity.

### 8) Injured party stewardship

If you are the injured: better whole means you may require structure without becoming a permanent prosecutor. Name what would help; refuse moving goalposts as revenge (Week 2 honor). Your “no” to premature trust is not hostility; endless goalpost moving as identity is.

### 9) Proverbs warning

Confidence in an unfaithful person in time of trouble is like a broken tooth — pain. Rebuilding is mercy and wisdom, not naivety. Structure protects both. Teleios dignity includes refusing foolish exposure while still allowing brick evidence.

### 10) Faculties under rebuild load

| Party | Stewardship |
|-------|-------------|
| Repairer | Conative bricks; Mental ownership; Emotional humility without fusion |
| Injured | Mental clarity on milestones; Emotional honesty; Behavioral review without sadism |
| Both | Affective desire for quick relief does not authorize fake trust |

### 11) Better whole

“Trust me again because I love you” is preference pressure.  
“Watch these bricks for 30/60/90 days” is design.  
“I am nothing until you trust me” is teleios denial.  
“You are nothing until I feel safe forever” can become prosecutor ontology.

### 12) Continuity

Rebuilt trust still needs governance (Week 6) or the wall weathers without maintenance meetings. Course 10 structural forgiveness intensifies release-with-rails — preview: bricks first, speeches second. Week 4’s pattern journal is evidence for which bricks matter; do not invent a generic plan that ignores the actual rupture type.

### 13) Evidence bar — School 1 greatness

Kept means: breaks named without fog; three behaviors running; seven log days; one review held; observable “rebuilt enough” defined; teleios grammar survives both shame spiral and premature trust demand. A great School 1 does not graduate speech-rebuilders or permanent prosecutors. If you still pressure for feelings on your schedule, stay before Week 6 branding.

### 14) What this week is not

Not cheap grace speeches.  
Not surveillance-as-lifestyle forever.  
Not anti-mercy.  
Anti-**promise-only theater** and anti-**prosecution-as-identity**.

### 15) Language discipline

- “Just believe me.” → “Watch these three behaviors under review.”  
- “I’ll never trust again so I stay safe forever.” → “I require bricks; I refuse prosecutor identity.”  
- “I’m trash until they smile.” → “I am teleios; I lay bricks and repair misses.”  
- “Love means instant trust.” → “Love means structural evidence under teleios patience.”

Drew apologizes beautifully; repeats break; demands “I trust you” on day three; identity collapses between speeches. Teleios denied. Fruit: trust dies; Course 08 repair becomes theater.
