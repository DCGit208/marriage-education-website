# Week 5: Rebuilding Trust
## Brick by Brick — Not Promise by Promise

> **Week 5 of 6** | 8–10 hours | Structural Rebuild  
> **Sources:** Course 08 W2 · Week 4 · Proverbs 25:19

---

## The Central Truth

Trust can be rebuilt — **only through structural acts**, not promises.  
A brick wall rises brick by brick. Speeches without bricks are scaffolding with no wall.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How is trust rebuilt brick by brick?

### Key Insight
After rupture, the injured party needs pattern evidence under load. The repairer needs humility without fusion self-erasure. Both need a plan with metrics and time.

### Learning Outcomes
- List where trust has been broken (specific)  
- Write a Trust Repair Plan  
- Practice three trust-building behaviors this week  
- Keep a daily trust log for 7 days  
- Pass a Rebuilding Trust Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Broken trust inventory

Name breaks without fog:

- What act?  
- What promise/type violated?  
- What impact (known)?  
- What has been tried already?  

Unnamed breaks cannot be bricked over — only painted.

### 2) Trust Repair Plan

1. Full ownership (Course 08 ritual)  
2. Transparency window (agreed scope/time — not endless surveillance as lifestyle)  
3. Three structural behaviors (daily/weekly)  
4. Review cadence (e.g., Sunday 20 minutes)  
5. What “rebuilt enough” looks like (observable)  
6. Limits: no coercion; no fake forgiveness deadlines  

### 3) Three behaviors (examples)

- On-time presence where you previously flaked  
- Proactive update before asked  
- Device/account agreements kept without sulking  
- Same-day repair when you miss  

Choose three fit to the break.

### 4) Daily trust log (7 days)

| Day | Brick laid | Miss? | Repair | Other’s observable response |
|-----|------------|-------|--------|------------------------------|

### 5) Analogy: brick wall

One brick is not a wall. One perfect week is not a lifetime. Keep laying. Do not demand the injured party “feel trust” on your schedule.

### 6) Injured party stewardship

If you are the injured: better whole means you may require structure without becoming a permanent prosecutor. Name what would help; refuse moving goalposts as revenge (Week 2 honor).

### 7) Proverbs warning

Confidence in an unfaithful person in time of trouble is like a broken tooth — pain. Rebuilding is mercy and wisdom, not naivety. Structure protects both.

### 8) Better whole

“Trust me again because I love you” is preference pressure.  
“Watch these bricks for 30/60/90 days” is design.

### 9) Continuity

Rebuilt trust still needs governance (Week 6) or the wall weathers without maintenance meetings.
