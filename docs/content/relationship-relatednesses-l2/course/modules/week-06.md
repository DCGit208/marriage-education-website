# Week 6: Covenant Governance
## Health Sustained by Order — Not by Hope Alone

> **Week 6 of 6** | 9–11 hours | Capstone  
> **Sources:** Weeks 1–5 · Course 08 · Proverbs 24:3 · better whole

---

## The Central Truth

Relationship health is sustained by **governance**, not by hope.  
A boardroom is intentional order. Homes and friendships that refuse all structure often call chaos “organic” until contempt arrives.

Governance is not corporate coldness. It is love with a calendar, rules that match type, and review that prevents drift.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Covenant / bond governance defined

**Governance** = agreed rules, roles, rhythms, and review by which relatedness stays design-aligned under load.

Marriage covenants are formal; friendships and kinship still need lighter governance (cadence, confidentiality, money, conflict protocol).

### 2) Rules worth writing

Minimum set for a key bond:

1. Communication norms (Course 08 W3)  
2. Conflict pause/return (Week 4)  
3. Repair ritual (Course 08 W4)  
4. Boundaries/honor (Week 2)  
5. Money/time/access fitting type (Week 1)  
6. Trust review if rebuilding (Week 5)  

### 3) Governance meeting agenda (45–60 min)

1. Appreciation (one concrete)  
2. What worked this week  
3. What drifted  
4. One conflict pattern check  
5. Boundaries needing adjust  
6. Decisions / next acts  
7. Schedule next meeting  

Not a courtroom. Not a therapy dump without structure.

### 4) Two structure gaps

Name where hope has been substituting for rails (calendar, sex/loyalty talks, in-laws, devices, finances). Install one rail per gap this week.

### 5) Analogy: boardroom / house built

Proverbs 24:3 — house built by wisdom. Governance is wisdom’s meeting. Hope is a feeling; wisdom is architecture.

### 6) Better whole

Governance is mutual for adult bonds — not one spouse parenting the other forever. If one party refuses all structure, that is data for readiness (Course 06), not a prompt to nag into fusion.

### 7) Hold the meeting

This week: actual meeting. Notes required. If no partner available, hold with a wise witness reviewing your rules for friendship/family bonds you can govern unilaterally (your side of the edge).

### 8) Level 2 integration

| Week | System piece |
|------|----------------|
| 1 | Framework / map |
| 2 | Boundaries & honor |
| 3 | Power & responsibility |
| 4 | Conflict patterns |
| 5 | Trust rebuild bricks |
| 6 | Governance cadence |

### 9) Continuity into later School 1 courses

Temperament Intelligence and further tracks assume governed relatedness. Keep the meeting rhythm for 30 days minimum after this course.

### 10) Final ratiocination

- Premise 1: Relatedness under load drifts without review.  
- Premise 2: Hope does not correct drift by itself.  
- Premise 3: Agreed rules + meetings restore design order.  
- Conclusion: Covenant governance is maintenance of love’s architecture.
