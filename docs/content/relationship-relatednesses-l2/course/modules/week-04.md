# Week 4: Conflict Patterns
## Govern the Weather — Don’t Worship the Storm

> **Week 4 of 6** | 8–10 hours | Pattern Governance  
> **Sources:** Course 08 communication/repair · Week 3 power · James 1:19 · teleios · better whole

---

## The Central Truth

Conflict is a **pattern to be governed**, not a storm to be feared — and not a sport to be won.  
Weather maps predict fronts. Your conflict has fronts too: cue → move → counter-move → rupture or repair.

You are **teleios** by design. Conflict does not invent your personhood, and “winning” does not manufacture worth. Practice governs patterns because likeness under load is stewardship — not because storms prove you are unfinished junk awaiting reinvention. Better wholes pause, ask, and listen; they do not fuse or flee as ontology projects.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Conflict is not failure

Finite persons differ. Conflict can clarify design. **Ungoverned** conflict damages honor, trust, and body.

Teleios persons still conflict. Design-state is not emotional flatness. Likeness means governing the front — Mental naming, Emotional stewardship, Conative pause, Behavioral return — not deleting the weather.

### 2) Teleios grammar for conflict

| Illegal conflict grammar | Teleios conflict grammar |
|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Storm = we are wrong for each other | Storm = pattern to govern |
| Winning = worth | Repair = stewardship |
| “I am my pattern” | Pattern is practice, not ontology |
| Fuse or flee as identity | Pause with return time as adults |
| Conflict invents who I am | I am teleios; conflict reveals likeness gaps |

Practice does not manufacture being. A governed fight reveals character; it does not create a new self. Shame spiral after conflict (“I am trash”) is chase ontology wearing repair vocabulary.

### 3) Ratiocination

- Premise 1: Teleios identity precedes conflict practice.
- Premise 2: Ungoverned conflict treats persons as enemies or trophies.
- Premise 3: Design presents pause, truth, paraphrase, and repair as fitting under load (Course 08).
- Conclusion: Conflict must be governed from teleios + better whole, or “communication skills” manufacture winning cults or storm-fear.

### 4) Common pattern pairs

| Pattern | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Pursue–withdraw | One presses; one exits |
| Attack–defend | Contempt vs justification |
| Escalate–escalate | Volume and kitchen-sink |
| Peace-fake–explode | Soft lie then detonation |
| Scorekeep–scorekeep | Archive warfare |

Pick your top two honestly. For each, name the cue (Behavioral tell, Emotional spike, Mental story).

### 5) Design replacement

Example: pursue–withdraw →  

> At cue (raised voice), we pause 20 minutes; withdrawer sets return time; pursuer journals one ask; resume with paraphrase first.  
> We remain teleios persons; this protocol does not invent us — it aligns us.

Write one replacement card for your top pattern. Vague cards fail at peak heat.

### 6) Pause — ask — listen

1. **Pause** — Behavioral interrupt (Course 07); Conative choice against escalate  
2. **Ask** — one clarifying question, not a trap (Mental discipline)  
3. **Listen** — paraphrase before rebuttal (Course 08 W3); Emotional weather named without vetoing truth  

Teleios check mid-conflict: “Am I trying to win identity, or steward a bond between wholes?”

### 7) Journal fields (one week)

| Date | Cue | My pattern | Their pattern | Pause? | Outcome | Repair? | Teleios slip (worth/winning/fusion)? |
|------|-----|------------|---------------|--------|---------|---------|--------------------------------------|

### 8) Analogy: weather map

You cannot outlaw weather. You can refuse to picnic in lightning. Pattern literacy is relational meteorology. Teleios persons still need coats; they do not need to become the storm.

### 9) Power in conflict

Week 3: the one who can leave, shout louder, or withhold may “win” and still lose the bond. Govern power mid-conflict: no coercion, no silent abandonment without return time. Honor Code (Week 2) still binds.

### 10) Better whole

“Conflict means we’re wrong for each other” can be fear.  
“Conflict means I get to fuse or flee” can be immaturity.  
“Conflict means I am unfinished until they regulate me” is teleios denial.  
Governed conflict is adult relatedness.

### 11) Continuity

Broken patterns bruise trust. Week 5 rebuilds with bricks, not speeches. Course 10 will demand mastery under covenant load — start the journal now so intensity has data. Course 08 paraphrase and same-day repair are not optional add-ons; they are the landing gear after every pause–ask–listen cycle.

### 12) Evidence bar — School 1 greatness

Kept means: two patterns named; replacement card usable at peak heat; journal rows under real conflict; return-time honored; teleios grammar survives the first raised voice. A great School 1 does not graduate people who map storms and still worship winning. If your journal shows chase-worth mid-fight most days, stay before Week 5 branding.

### 13) What this week is not

Not conflict addiction.  
Not cold technician mode that maps patterns to avoid heart and skip repair.  
Not “never raise volume” as holiness.  
Anti-**ungoverned** conflict and anti-**winning-as-ontology**.

### 14) Language discipline

- “We always destroy each other.” → “We run pattern X; we install replacement Y.”  
- “If I win, I’m okay.” → “I am teleios; repair is the meter.”  
- “Conflict means leave.” → “Conflict means govern; wisdom exits are separate from storm-fear.”  
- “I am my anger.” → “Anger is Emotional weather; I remain teleios under design.”
