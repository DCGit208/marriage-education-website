## The Central Truth

Harmony is achieved by **honoring, not erasing**, differences.  
An orchestra needs every instrument. Marriage needs temperament honor without demanding sameness — and without resentment when difference costs. Erasure projects (“make them like me”) are not love. Resentment museums are not discernment. Daily honor is the practice.

This week closes Course 13 and seals School 1’s personal/relational Foundation spine (Courses 02–13) with a living charter.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Appreciation list (10 specifics)

Rules:

- Name the **flavor strength**, not generic “they’re great”  
- No backhanded compliments  
- Include at least three items that used to irritate you  

Example shapes: “Your slow processing protects us from rash debt.” “Your fast initiative gets the household moving when I stall.”

### 2) Daily honor act

Tied to their flavor:

- Give processing time without punishing silence  
- Invite their pace into a decision  
- Celebrate initiative without competing  
- Protect quiet / protect social gear briefly without contempt  
- Match intensity down or up wisely without mockery  

Honor is Behavioral, not only Mental admiration.

### 3) Complaint → gratitude → clean ask

When complaint rises:

1. Catch it  
2. Force one gratitude sentence about the same trait’s upside  
3. Then one clean ask about the downside (specific, time-bound)  
4. No erasure demand (“stop being who you are”)  

This is not toxic positivity. It is ordered speech.

### 5) Analogy: orchestra

Mute the violins because you prefer brass — and you lose the piece. Conduct difference; don’t fire instruments for being themselves. The conductor still cues entries — governance (Course 09–10) remains. Honor ≠ chaos.

### 6) Wisdom built the house (Prov 24:3)

Harmony is architectural. Wishful “acceptance” without honor acts and clean asks is not wisdom. Rulebooks (Course 10 W8) should include at least one temperament-honor rule after this week.

### 7) Better whole

You do not become them; they do not become you. Children (if any) need to see difference honored — or they will inherit contempt as family liturgy.

If a graduate of School 1 still:

### 14) Blessing without flattery

You may finish Course 13 with unfinished honor habits. Unfinished is not un-imaged. Keep the 7-day challenge logic for 30 more days. Espouse design. Live accordingly. Make School 1 great in the household, not only in the LMS.

### 15) Rulebook insert (copy into Course 10 rules)

> We will not call temperament differences character defects. We will honor pace/intensity differences with return-times and clean asks. Contempt words on our forbid list: ______. Weekly we name one strength in the other’s flavor.

### 16) Final School 1 charge

Make School 1 great where it counts: breakfast tone, conflict mid-week, desire loyalty, repair speed, honor of difference. Certificates are secondary. Teleios practice, emotional literacy, and temperament intelligence — lived — are the school’s reputation in a household.

### 17) Appreciation list quality control

Cross out any item that:

- is backhanded (“you’re slow but at least…”)
- is generic (“you’re nice”)
- secretly demands change

Replace with flavor-specific honor. Aim for sentences your spouse would recognize as true.

### 18) Clean ask bank (write five)

Examples to adapt:

1. “I need twenty minutes quiet after work before decisions.”
2. “Please don’t interpret my processing silence as rejection; I’ll return at ____.”
3. “I need us to decide by tonight; can we set a 15-minute consult?”
4. “When my intensity rises, tell me ‘volume’ as a cue — I’ll brake.”
5. “Join me for thirty minutes of my social gear this weekend; then we protect quiet.”

Honor without asks becomes resentment. Asks without honor become control.

### 19) Children / community witness

If children are present: they learn whether difference is mocked or conducted. One week of honor acts is a liturgy. If no children: your friends still learn from how you speak about your spouse’s flavor in public. Public honor is part of Week 3.

### 20) School 1 final greatness triad

For 90 days, keep visible:

1. **Teleios** — practice from perfection (Course 11)
2. **Literacy** — name, brake, mend (Course 12)
3. **Temperament honor** — no contempt, daily honor, clean asks (Course 13)

A school is great when kitchens change. Let the charter be a kitchen document.

### 21) Public speech audit (7 days)

Each evening: Did I mock their flavor to a third party? Did I honor them in front of someone? One public honor act weekly is required for the harmony challenge to count as School 1 grade.
