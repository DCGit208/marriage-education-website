## Course thesis

Anthropocentric development is the ordered education of the human person according to design.  
It asks: How does a person who is already teleios by design become mindful, educated, and responsibly aligned in lived form over time?

This course does not teach that growth manufactures personhood, holiness, or teleios maturity.  
It teaches responsible development as the disciplined expression of what is already true by design.

---

## Doctrine guardrails

- Teleios/maturity is design-state / full development; activity does not create being.
- The car drives because it is a car. Action expresses design; action does not invent design.
- Holiness is state (`be holy`), not a skill produced by repetition.
- Image is given; likeness is formed alignment.
- Better whole, not better half.
- Development is anthropocentric (human-centered in design truth), not trend-centered.
- Skills, attributes, capacities, and maturation pillars remain distinct.
- Audits score stewardship and expression, never ontological worth.

## Learning outcomes (course)

By the end of 3 weeks, the learner will:

1. Define anthropocentric development without collapsing ontology into behavior.
2. Distinguish growth stage logic from productivity culture and personality branding.
3. Apply maturation laws to real life failures and breakdown points.
4. Build a 30-day developmental gap repair protocol with measurable stewardship practices.
5. Articulate a personal Development Charter: mindful, educated, espouse design, live accordingly.
