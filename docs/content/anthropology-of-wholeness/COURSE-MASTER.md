## Course thesis

To understand marriage you must first understand the human being.  
SBD begins with **anthropology**: what a person is **by design** — not by trauma, trend, or feeling.

This course deepens what Teleios Blueprint introduced: you were created **teleios** (whole). Here you learn why that claim is anthropology; how **image**, **holiness**, and **likeness** differ; how the Four Attributes belong to personal development (UDHR 26:2); and how to become mindful, espouse design, and live accordingly. Practice expresses responsibility; it does not manufacture maturity.

## Canon anchors

- `SBD-UNIFIED-CANON.md` §V.2 Anthropology  
- Design precedes doctrine; reason from design (Isaiah 1:18)  
- 1 + 1 = 1 (not ½ + ½); formation before fellowship; better whole, not better half  
- Hebrews 5:14 — the *life* of those of full age (senses exercised in living). **Not** “use manufactures maturity.” Car drives because it is a car.  
- UDHR 16 (full age / family) · UDHR 26:2 (full development of personality)  
- **Holiness** = state of being (1 Pet 1:16 — *be* ye holy); **not** a trained skill.  
- **Maturity / teleios** = who you are by design (axiomatic completeness / full development). Education → mindful, educated, espouse design, **live accordingly**. Living/use = responsibility & accountability — **not** what creates maturity.  
- **Likeness** = formed character aligning practice to design (distinct from manufacturing holiness or maturity).  
- **Personal Development — Maturation Map** (authoritative structure for skills / attributes / capacities / maturity):  
  `docs/content/_canon-assets/personal-development-maturation-map.png`  
  - **Skills:** Critical Thinking, Expression, Writing, Hearing, Reading  
  - **Attributes (Personal Development):** Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental  
  - **Capacities (Faculties):** Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, Affective  
  - **Maturation pillars:** Etymology, Axiology, Epistemology, Ontology  
  - Ground: UDHR Art. 26(2) · A/RES/41/128 Art. 2(1) — full development of the human personality / anthropocentric development  

Use this map whenever course prose says “skills,” “capacities,” “attributes,” or “maturity.” Do not collapse them into a virtue checklist. Do not teach that activity manufactures teleios.
