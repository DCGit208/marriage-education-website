## The Central Truth

**Image** is what you are — given nature.  
**Likeness** is formed character — life aligned to that nature.  
**Holiness** is state — *be* ye holy (1 Pet 1:16) — not a skill you manufacture.  
**Maturity / teleios** is who you are by design — not something activity invents (Week 1: the car drives because it is a car).

Confusion here destroys identity: people try to *feel* their way into a nature they already have, or *declare* a likeness they have not lived, or treat holiness and maturity as workshop products. SBD builds identity on **design** and **covenant responsibility** — not trauma, labels, or applause.

You do not become human by self-invention. You **espouse** who you are by design — mindful, educated — and **live accordingly**. That is the anthropology of Week 2.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Genesis 1:26–28 — the anthropological charter

God creates humanity in His **image**, male and female, with mandate: fruitfulness, stewardship, dominion under God. This is not a vibe. It is **ontology**:

- You are a person, not a product  
- You are sexed by design (male or female) — not rewritten by cultural preference or trend  
- You are commissioned — life has assignment, not only preference  
- You are relational by design — solitude is not the final anthropology; covenant and community are part of the mandate  

When anthropology collapses, sexuality, marriage, and family collapse next. Course 02 exists so School 1 does not float on slogans.

Canon §V.2: humanity created **teleios** — whole, not broken halves. **Image** is the gift of that designed nature. **Likeness** is the life that matches it — formed as you espouse design and live accordingly (practice FROM perfection, §V.8).

### 2) Image = given nature

**Image** answers: *What kind of being am I?*

You do not invent image. You **receive** it. That ends two modern lies:

1. **Self-creation:** “I define myself from scratch.”  
2. **Self-erasure:** “I am only my wounds.”  

Image means you already have dignity, moral agency, and covenant capacity. You are not waiting for a partner, a platform, or a diagnosis to become human — and you are not a “half” waiting for a “better half” (Week 1).

Image also means **limits**. Creatures are not gods. You cannot redefine design by intensity of desire. Desire is real; desire is not ontology.

### 3) Likeness = formed character

**Likeness** answers: *Is my life matching the nature I received?*

Ephesians 4:22–24: put off the old self, be renewed in mind, put on the new self. Colossians 3:10: put on the new self, renewed in knowledge after the image of its creator. That is formation language — education into design, not self-invention.

Likeness is formed as you become mindful and educated across the **Personal Development Map**:

| Layer | What likeness stewards |
|-------|------------------------|
| **Attributes** | Behavioral, Temperamental, Emotional, Mental (Week 3 deepens) |
| **Skills** | Critical thinking, expression, writing, hearing, reading |
| **Capacities** | Conative, Cognitive, Psychomotor, Affective (Course 04 deepens) |

Fruits (truthful speech, repair, fidelity, ordered rhythm) may appear. They are **evidence** that you are living accordingly — not the *definition* of likeness, and not a virtue brand that manufactures teleios.

You can have image and still live unlike. That is the tragedy of design unespoused — teleios nature with a life that refuses it.

### 4) The put off / put on loop

Do not treat Ephesians 4 as poetry only. Use it as a daily operating system:

| Step | Question | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| Put off | What old pattern am I refusing today? | Name it specifically |
| Renew mind | What design truth replaces the lie? | One sentence |
| Put on | What likeness act will I live today? | One measurable act |

Example:

- Put off: silent punishment after conflict  
- Renew: “I am created teleios; repair is fruit of living accordingly — withdrawal-as-weapon refuses design.”  
- Put on: one clear ownership sentence within 24 hours  

Without the loop, “identity work” becomes journaling that never changes Tuesday.

### 5) False identity sources SBD rejects

| False source | What it claims | What it produces |
|--------------|----------------|------------------|
| Trauma | “I am what happened to me.” | Permanent victimhood; covenant becomes therapy |
| Sexuality labels | “I am my desire pattern.” | Desire rules ontology; design is demoted |
| Social validation | “I am what is applauded.” | Unstable self; cannot carry private fidelity |
| Better-half romance | “I am incomplete until you finish me.” | False equation (½ + ½); spouse as missing fragment |

**Design commitments (non-negotiable this week):**

1. I will not define myself by damage.  
2. I will not outsource identity to culture or trend.  
3. I will pursue likeness — mindful, educated, living accordingly.  
4. I refuse “better half”; I honor better whole.

Trauma can be acknowledged and healed without becoming your name. Labels can describe patterns without becoming your ontology. Validation can encourage without becoming your foundation. Romance can honor a spouse without rewriting your ontology as a half.

### 6) Design statements that carry weight

Weak identity: “I feel like…” / “I’m trying to be…” / “I’m just…”  
Design identity: “I am designed to… / I am responsible for… / I am becoming…” (likeness language — *becoming* here means formed living, not manufacturing teleios from nothing)

Examples:

- “I am designed teleios; I espouse that design when silence would protect my ego.”  
- “I am responsible for stewarding desire as image-given sexuality under covenant — not as my ontology.”  
- “I am becoming (in likeness) a person whose life matches design under pressure — practice FROM perfection.”  
- “I am designed male/female by creation order; I will steward that design, not rewrite it by cultural preference.”  
- “I am responsible for the atmosphere I bring into relatedness.”  
- “I refuse ‘better half’; my spouse (or future spouse) is a better whole joined to me.”  

These statements are **testable**. If they never show up in life, they are cosplay.

### 7) Image, sexuality, holiness, and covenant (bridge without derailing)

Canon: you are sexual **by design** — sexuality is in nature before any act. That belongs to **image** (given nature).

**Holiness is not trained into existence.** It is **state** — who you are.  
*”Be ye holy; for I am holy.”* (1 Peter 1:16). The copula **be** means *have the quality of being* / *be identical to* — not “acquire holiness as a skill.” Holiness is ontological identity under God, not a workshop product.

What *is* formed is **likeness**: education and responsible living so your life matches design — including covenant fidelity as formed character. Confusing the layers produces either:

- shame that denies given nature (image), or  
- license that refuses likeness formation, or  
- moralism that treats holiness as a self-made performance, or  
- the car-becomes-a-car error (activity manufacturing maturity)  

Week 2 does not teach full sexuality doctrine (later School lanes). It installs the rule: **image/nature is given; holiness is state (be); likeness is formed as you espouse design and live accordingly.**

## Case Contrast

### Trauma identity
“I am my wound.” The story is real; the ontology is false. Image is overwritten. Partners are recruited as healers. Likeness never starts because damage has been named as the self.

### Label identity
“I am anxious / avoidant / [trend].” The label becomes a permission slip. Formation stops. Partners are recruited as regulators. The person is not lying about a pattern — they are **ontologizing** a pattern.

### Validation identity
A young professional’s self rises and falls with likes, titles, and romantic attention. Covenant dating feels like auditioning. Private fidelity feels optional because the audience is the real god.

### Better-half identity
“When I find my other half, I’ll be complete.” Image is treated as incomplete. Likeness is postponed until romance arrives. Week 1’s false equation wears a wedding dress.

### Design identity
“I have patterns. I am created teleios. I am responsible to espouse design and live accordingly.” Same nervous system. Different anthropology. Spouse sought as **better whole**, not missing fragment.

## Common Errors This Week

1. **Using “image” as flattery** — dignity without duty.  
2. **Using “likeness” as perfectionism** — frantic performance that tries to manufacture holiness or maturity.  
3. **Trauma honesty without agency** — naming wounds while refusing to live design.  
4. **Design statements as affirmations only** — words without Tuesday evidence.  
5. **Skipping sexed design** — treating male/female creation as optional decoration.  
6. **Treating fashion/attire language as the enemy** — the enemy is preference that rewrites design, not disciplined presentation (A3 / MV House remain clean).  
7. **Keeping “better half” in your mouth** after Week 1 named it.

## Daily Drills (7 days)

### Morning (10 min) — Practice FROM perfection + put on likeness
Write four lines:

1. Image (given): “I am…” (design, not mood) — include teleios / whole if true to you  
2. Holiness (state): “I am holy under God — state, not self-made performance.”  
3. Put off: one old pattern you refuse today (trauma / label / validation / better-half)  
4. Put on: one likeness act you will live today (attribute, skill, or capacity)  

State: “I practice FROM perfection. I do not become a car by driving.”

### Midday (2 min) — Checkpoint
Ask: *Am I acting from design — or from trauma, label, validation, or better-half math?*  
If not design — reset one concrete act that espouses who you are.

### Evening (15 min) — Evidence
- One moment I chose design  
- One moment I chose trauma / label / validation / better-half  
- What fruit followed each?  
- Repair for tomorrow  

### Day 7 — Three design statements
Finalize:

- “I am designed to …”  
- “I am responsible for …”  
- “I am becoming …” (likeness — living accordingly, not manufacturing teleios)  

These travel with you into Weeks 3–4 and into Course 03.
