## The Central Truth

Growth is sustainable when rooted in design, not in self-hatred.  
A plant grows steady, healthy, rooted. Scorched-earth “improvement” kills the life it claims to cultivate. You remain teleios by design; growth is likeness over Law of Time and Repetition — not a project to finally become human.

Be ruthless. “High standards” that require self-hatred are still illegal.

### 2) Self-stewardship rule (non-negotiable)

After miss:

1. Own the act (specific)  
2. Repair within the time you promised (same day when possible)  
3. Resume the rail next cue  
4. No multi-day sentence  
5. No identity deletion  
6. Optional: one witness check if the miss is patterned  

This is holiness with Behavioral teeth — not soft denial.

### 3) 30-day goal — design-rooted

Rules for the goal:

- One domain only (speech, sleep, desire loyalty, presence, money order, etc.)  
- Metric visible to you (and ideally a witness)  
- Weekly 15-minute review  
- Ontology line fixed in writing: “I grow as teleios, not into personhood.”  
- Fatigue-ready minimum (MDS logic)

Illegal goals: “become a new me”; “earn the right to be loved”; “finally be enough.”

### 4) Growth log (30 days; launch now)

Days 1–7 are this week’s assignment; Days 8–30 continue after Course 11 closes.

### 5) Analogy: plant

Water, light, soil, time.  
Not ripping the plant up daily to check roots.  
Not bleaching the plant to force “purity.”  
Not refusing water because “I should already be a forest.”

Law of Time (Course 05): depth needs duration.  
Law of Repetition: reps stabilize.  
Neither invents ontology.

### 6) Hebrews 5:14 — again

Senses exercised in the life of the mature — responsibility profile, not maturity factory. Sustainable growth is exercised discernment and practice as a teleios person under load.

### 8) Continuity into Course 12

Emotional Literacy will train naming and governance of feeling. If chase returns (“I am my emotions” or “I must feel holy”), you will fail literacy. Carry the declaration into Week 1 of Course 12 explicitly.

### 9) Better whole

Do not make a spouse the scorekeeper of your 30-day goal in a parental way. Witness ≠ parent. Adult stewardship invites accountability without fusion.

### 10) Final ratiocination

- Premise 1: Teleios persons still require likeness over time.
- Premise 2: Self-hatred agriculture damages persons and households.
- Premise 3: Rails + repair + joy + duration produce sustainable fruit.
- Conclusion: Growth is stewardship of design — or it is not growth worthy of School 1.

### 11) Linking the 30-day goal to School 1 rails you already own

Do not invent a exotic goal. Prefer a goal that reactivates an existing rail:

- Course 07 MDS item that drifted
- Course 10 rulebook rule that became a wish again
- Course 11 identity practice cue
- Course 08 repair speed

Sustainable growth often means **returning** to design you already named — under Law of Time — not collecting new certificates.

### 12) Pride risk after “I practice from perfection”

Teleios grammar can be twisted into superiority over “less mature” people. Illegal. Ontology is gift. Likeness is responsibility. If your 30-day plan includes contempt for others’ pace, rewrite the plan.

### 13) Sample 30-day goal (template only)

Domain: evening presence.  
Metric: device-free 20 minutes with spouse/friend/family ≥5 nights/week.  
Miss protocol: same-night ownership + make-up block within 24h.  
Weekly review: Sunday 15 minutes with witness.  
Ontology line: “I am teleios; this is likeness, not worth purchase.”

Rewrite to your domain; keep the structure.
