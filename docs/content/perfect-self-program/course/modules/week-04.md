# Week 4: Sustainable Growth
## Rooted Stewardship — Not Self-Hatred Agriculture

> **Week 4 of 4** | 9–11 hours | Capstone  
> **Sources:** Weeks 1–3 · Course 05 · plant analogy

---

## The Central Truth

Growth is sustainable when rooted in design, not in self-hatred.  
A plant grows steady, healthy, rooted. Scorched-earth “improvement” kills the very life it claims to cultivate.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do we grow without punishing ourselves into change?

### Key Insight
Audit growth patterns: which are stewardship, which are self-punishment? Replace punishment with rails + 30-day goal that assumes teleios starting point.

### Learning Outcomes
- Audit growth patterns  
- Replace self-punishment with self-stewardship  
- Set a 30-day growth goal (design-rooted)  
- Launch a growth log  
- Write Perfect Self closing charter  

---

### 2) Self-stewardship rule

After miss: own → repair → resume rail. No multi-day sentence. No identity deletion.

### 3) 30-day goal

One domain. Metric visible. Weekly review. Ontology line fixed: “I grow as teleios, not into personhood.”

### 4) Growth log (launch 30 days)

| Day | Rail | Miss? | Repair | Note |
|-----|------|-------|--------|------|

### 5) Analogy: plant

Water, light, soil — not ripping the plant up daily to check roots. Sustainable growth trusts design while tending.

### 6) Integration

W1 starting point · W2 practice · W3 joy · W4 sustain.

### 7) Continuity

Carry into Emotional Literacy (Course 12): emotions stewarded from teleios, not chased for worth.
