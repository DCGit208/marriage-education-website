# Week 1: Teleios Starting Point
## Practice Because You Are — Not To Become

> **Week 1 of 4** | 8–10 hours | Ontology Reset  
> **Sources:** Matthew 5:48 · Course 01–03 · seed/tree analogy

---

## The Central Truth

You do not practice to become perfect; you practice because you are made **teleios in design**.  
A seed contains the tree — starting point, not unfinished junk awaiting invention. Chase-worth spirituality is a counterfeit of stewardship.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
What changes when perfection is starting point, not finish line?

### Key Insight
Chase mode: “I will be acceptable when…”  
Practice mode: “I am teleios by design; today I align expression.”

### Learning Outcomes
- Write a Perfect Self / Teleios declaration  
- List two ways you chase instead of practice  
- Replace one striving loop with gratitude  
- Run a daily teleios gratitude journal  
- Pass a Teleios Starting Point Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Two grammars

| Chase | Practice from teleios |
|-------|------------------------|
| Become worthy | Express design |
| Mood as meter | Rails as meter |
| Self-hatred fuel | Responsibility fuel |
| Never arrives | Daily likeness |

### 2) Declaration template

> I am teleios by design — image-bearing, sexed, responsible.  
> I do not practice to invent myself.  
> I practice to live as who I am.  
> Today I refuse chase-worth in ______.

### 3) Chase inventory

Name two: performance holiness; comparison; romantic completion; productivity-as-ontology; shame spirals after miss.

### 4) Gratitude as interrupt

Gratitude is not denial of gaps. It is Mental premise correction: “design is given” before “gap to steward.”

### 5) Daily journal (7 days)

Morning: one teleios truth. Evening: one chase moment + one practice moment.

### 6) Analogy: seed

The seed is not a defective tree. Growth reveals what was present. Education awakens; it does not manufacture personhood.

### 7) Better whole

Chasing a spouse to “complete” you is chase ontology. Practice from wholeness instead.
