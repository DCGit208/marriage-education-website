## Practice Because You Are — Not To Become

> **Week 1 of 4** | 8–10 hours | Ontology Reset

## The Central Truth

You do not practice to become perfect; you practice because you are made **teleios in design**.  
A seed contains the tree — starting point, not unfinished junk awaiting invention. Chase-worth spirituality is a counterfeit of stewardship. Education awakens; it does not manufacture personhood.

Matthew 5:48 — *be* perfect — is state language, not a motivational moonshot. The Perfect Self Program begins here or it becomes another self-improvement cult with Christian vocabulary.

## Core Teaching

### 1) Two grammars of formation

| Chase grammar | Practice-from-teleios grammar |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| Become worthy | Express design |
| Mood as meter of progress | Rails as meter of stewardship |
| Self-hatred as fuel | Responsibility as fuel |
| Never arrives | Daily likeness |
| Marriage/partner as completion | Better whole under covenant |
| Practice invents the self | Practice reveals and aligns the self |

If your private sentences still sound like the left column, you will weaponize Courses 02–10 against yourself.

### 2) What “perfect” means here

In this canon, teleios / perfect = **design-state / full development** — the human as designed, not a flawless performance record.

It does **not** mean:

- never missing a rail
- emotional flatness
- spiritual celebrity
- arrival at a life without temptation

It **does** mean:

- image given
- nature given (including sexed nature)
- capacity and responsibility given
- holiness as state to be lived (`be holy`), not a mood to be earned by misery

### 3) Ratiocination (non-negotiable)

- Premise 1: What a person *is* precedes what a person *practices*.
- Premise 2: Practice that assumes unfinished ontology turns growth into self-creation religion.
- Premise 3: Scripture and design anthropology present humans as image-bearing and callable to *be* perfect/holy as state language.
- Conclusion: Practice must start from teleios identity, or it will manufacture anxiety even when “successful.”

### 4) Seed and tree

The seed is not a defective tree. Growth reveals what was present.  
Scorching the seed with self-hatred does not speed the oak. Watering, light, and ordered soil do.

Analogy limit: you are not a vegetable. You are a responsible person. The point is ontology-before-activity — not passivity.

### 5) Chase inventory (name costs)

Common chase patterns in School 1 graduates:

1. Performance holiness — “I’m only okay when my streak is clean”
2. Comparison — “I’m behind their marriage / body / income”
3. Romantic completion — “I’ll feel whole when someone chooses me”
4. Productivity-as-ontology — “I am my output”
5. Shame spiral after miss — “I am trash; start Monday as a new species”

For two patterns, write four cost columns: Body · Household · Work/calling · Inner life.

If you cannot name costs, you are still romanticizing chase as “high standards.”

### 6) Passivity is not the alternative

False binary: chase OR coast.  
True alternative: **stewardship from design** — rails, repair, joy, and truth without worth-panic.

“I’m already perfect so I need no practice” is ontology talk used to baptize drift. Teleios persons still require likeness, education, and self-command (Courses 05–07).

### 7) Declaration template (write yours)

> I am teleios by design — image-bearing, sexed, responsible, covenantal.  
> I do not practice to invent myself or to purchase worth.  
> I practice to live as who I am under load.  
> Today I refuse chase-worth in ______.  
> When I miss, I will repair without identity deletion.  
> I will not recruit a better half to complete my ontology.

Witness this declaration. Private mythology loves unshared vows.

### 8) Gratitude as Mental interrupt

Gratitude here is not denial of gaps. It is premise correction:

1. Design is given (ontology)  
2. Gap is real (epistemic honesty)  
3. Next act is stewardship (Conative)  

Morning: one teleios truth.  
Evening: one chase moment noticed + one practice moment kept.

### 9) Better whole

Chasing a spouse, boyfriend/girlfriend, or “eventual marriage” to complete you is chase ontology wearing romance. Course 08–10 already forbade better-half fusion. Course 11 forbids it at the identity root.

### 10) Continuity into Week 2

Starting-point declarations without daily identity practice become posters. Week 2 installs the mirror rails.

### 11) What this week is not

Not a permission slip for sin.  
Not “manifest your perfect self.”  
Not contempt for imperfect feelings.  
Not anti-growth. It is anti-**wrong-rooted** growth.

## Case Contrasts

Dana finishes every School 1 course and still wakes as “not enough.” Rails become evidence of failure. Growth is self-punishment. Fruit: anxiety, brittle holiness, household walking on eggshells.

### Case B — Passive “already perfect”
