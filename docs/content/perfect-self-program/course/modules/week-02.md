## The Central Truth

Practice is how design becomes visible reality in time — **likeness**, not ontology manufacture.  
A mirror reflects; it does not invent the face. Identity practice keeps the mirror clean of chase fog and leak stories. Hearing without practice is self-deception (James 1:23–25) — but practice without Week 1 ontology is self-creation religion.

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do daily practices reinforce given identity without self-invention myths?

### Key Insight
Identity leaks are micro-stories: “I am my failure / job / desire / mood / marital status.” Install one practice that returns you to design truth on a cue — then prove it for 7 days with a witness.

### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this week you will:

- Map daily identity leaks with cue / story / act / cost
- Install one identity-reinforcing practice (cue → truth script → act → log)
- Share the practice with a partner or wise witness
- Complete a 7-day identity check-in under fatigue days, not only easy days
- Pass an Identity Practice Audit without branding fog

## Core Teaching

### 1) Likeness vs ontology (again, on purpose)

| Ontology (given) | Likeness (practiced) |
|------------------|----------------------|
| Image | Aligned expression |
| Teleios design-state | Rails, speech, desire loyalty |
| Sexed nature | Stewardship of desire |
| Worth given | Honor enacted |

Practice changes **expression**. It does not rewrite **being**. If your practice language says “I’m becoming someone,” rewrite it: “I’m aligning what I already am.”

### 2) Identity leaks — taxonomy

1. **Role collapse** — I am my job / parenting / ministry title  
2. **Shame after miss** — I am my failure  
3. **Applause addiction** — I am what is praised  
4. **Secret life** — private self contradicts public claim  
5. **Mood ontology** — I am my Emotional weather  
6. **Desire ontology** — I am my urge (Course 06 W11 continuity)  
7. **Relational fusion** — I am okay only if they…  

Pick your top three. Leaks ignored become household climate.

### 3) Practice install formula

1. **Cue** — when the leak fires (criticism, mirror, bank app, loneliness, scroll)  
2. **Truth script** (15–30 seconds) — Week 1 declaration line + specific denial of the leak  
3. **Aligned act** — one Behavioral/Conative move (repair, phone down, honest sentence, walk)  
4. **Log** — one line  

Example:

> Cue: harsh feedback at work.  
> Script: “I am teleios; this critique addresses an act, not my being.”  
> Act: ask one clarifying question; no revenge silence at home.  
> Log: Y/N.

### 4) Why sharing matters

Unshared practices become private mythology. A witness may not be a spouse — but someone must be allowed to ask, “Did you run the script?” Course 05 anti-delusion architecture applies.

### 5) Faculties and attributes in the practice

Identity practice is not only Cognitive slogans:

- **Mental attribute** — interrupt illegal premises  
- **Emotional attribute** — name feeling without becoming it (Course 12 preview)  
- **Behavioral** — the act after the script  
- **Conative** — keep the cue schedule  
- **Affective** — care for future self and household in the act  
- **Psychomotor** — show up in body (eye contact, leave the room, kneel, walk)

Slogan-only “identity work” fails under load.

### 6) Analogy: mirror

Dirty mirror: chase stories and leaks.  
Clean mirror: design.  
Practice is cleaning — not surgery inventing a new face.  
Breaking the mirror after a bad day is shame ontology.

### 7) Married and unmarried tracks

**Married:** leaks often recruit the spouse as mirror-replacement (“tell me who I am”). Better whole: spouse may affirm; they are not your ontology supplier.

**Unmarried:** leaks often recruit fantasy (“when someone loves me I’ll know who I am”). Practice now — readiness is fruit (Course 06 W12), not a future person’s job.

### 8) Load test

Your practice is fake if it only runs on calm mornings. Schedule at least two fatigue-day reps this week (late night, conflict day, disappointing news).

### 9) Continuity into Week 3

Identity practice without holiness/joy becomes grim duty or proud grit. Week 3 installs joyful alignment — holiness as state, not shame fuel.

### 10) Illegal practices

- Affirmation fog with no Behavioral act  
- “New me” challenges that assume you were non-teleios  
- Using practice to dominate a spouse (“I’ve done my identity work; submit”)  
- Secret practice that never touches the leak that actually costs the household
