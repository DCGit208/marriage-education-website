---

## The Central Truth

Practice is how design becomes visible reality in time — **likeness**, not ontology manufacture.  
A mirror reflects; it does not invent the face. Identity practice keeps the mirror clean of chase fog and leak stories.

---

## Week Overview

### Core Question
How do daily practices reinforce given identity without self-creation myths?

### Key Insight
Identity leaks are micro-stories: “I am my failure / job / desire / mood.” Install one practice that returns you to design truth on a schedule.

### Learning Outcomes
- Identify daily identity leaks  
- Install one identity-reinforcing practice  
- Share practice with a partner/witness  
- Complete a 7-day identity check-in  
- Pass an Identity Practice Audit  

---

## Core Teaching

### 1) Leaks

Common: role collapse; shame after miss; applause addiction; secret life; “I’m just being real” as excuse for drift.

### 2) Practice install

Cue → 60–90 second truth script → one aligned act → log.  
Example: after criticism → “I am teleios; I will own the act without ontology collapse” → repair ask.

### 3) 7-day check-in

| Day | Leak noticed | Practice used? | Act |
|-----|--------------|----------------|-----|

### 4) Share

Witness kills private mythology. Share the practice; invite one correction.

### 5) Analogy: mirror

Dirty mirror: chase stories. Clean mirror: design. Practice is cleaning — not surgery inventing a new face.

### 6) Continuity

Identity practice without holiness/joy (Week 3) stays grim duty. Joy is next.
