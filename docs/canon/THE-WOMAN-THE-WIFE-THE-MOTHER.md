### Integration law

```
WOMANHOOD is the foundation.
The WIFE lives in the WOMAN.
The MOTHER lives in the WIFE.

Lose womanhood to "wife"     → lose woman AND wife.
```

The man is tied to **wifehood through womanhood** — not through functional roles, not through motherhood as substitute. Biology, one-flesh fidelity, conjugal order. This is ontology (design), not sentiment.

| Level | Unit | Basis |

What fails is **fragmentation**: wifehood or motherhood **disconnected** from womanhood foundation.

## Integration Doctrine Guardrails (MANDATORY — all courseware, book, editorial)

**Womanhood is the foundation that makes wifehood and motherhood operational.** This is the whole point of the program. Covenant and family are not reductions of womanhood—they are where teleios womanhood is **deployed** in one-flesh generativity.

### NEVER in any teaching, exercise, quiz, or liturgy

- Language that pits **womanhood against covenant**, wifehood, motherhood, home, or family  
- **"Before any covenant role/title/status"** without immediate integration: wife/mother **express** womanhood **in** covenant  
- **"Domestic reduction"**, **"cosmic vs domestic"**, or any framing that treats marriage, home, or generative labor as **lesser** than an "authentic" identity elsewhere  
- **"Identity before roles"** woke grammar — use **foundation → expression** (integration law)  
- **Role-strip** exercises (remove husband/children to "find yourself") — diagnose **fragmentation**, never negate covenant expressions  
- **"Alone before God"** / **"when roles are stripped away"** as if covenant life were optional or contaminating  
- **"Woman first"** as rebellion against wife/mother duties — use **foundation governs expression**  
- **Function vs flesh** binaries — target **fragmentation** (role without womanhood root), not legitimate covenant work  
- Petition God to **make** home, identity, or teleios what He already declared — **steward and deploy** as co-heir (Rom 8:17; Gal 4:6–7; 1 John 4:17)  
- Vague **"spiritual atmosphere"** or undefined **spiritual** language — use faculties, design, Scripture, body; reason from facts (Isa 1:18)  
- Labeling structural infidelity as mere "SBD opinion" — when womanhood leaves covenant flesh, infidelity **has occurred** by design fact  
- **Endless becoming**, **authentic self**, **deferred identity**, vague **renounce** language  

### ALWAYS

- **Integration law** stated whole: Woman → Wife → Mother; lose foundation → lose all expressions  
- **Chronology in design** (teleios female before wedding day) paired with **covenant deployment** (wife/mother express wholeness in one flesh)  
- **Home = first university**; eudaemonia scales Person → Marriage → Family → Civilization (UDHR 16:3)  
- **Practice FROM perfection** — alignment and discipline, not begging to become  
- **Fragmentation** named as deformation — never wife/mother/home as enemies  
- **Meritorious attraction** — certificate does not command desire; fitness, health, mental strength, A³, dress-by-design are covenant duty  
- **Guard the foundation** — Psalm 11:3; muliebrity guarded diligently  
- Scripture anchors: Gen 1:26–28, Gen 2:21–24, Ps 11:3, Matt 5:48, Col 2:10, 1 John 4:17, Rom 8:17, Gal 4:6–7, Isa 1:18

## Liturgy & Prayer Guardrails (MANDATORY — courseware)

SBD liturgy is **remembrance and alignment**, not therapeutic begging to become what God already declared.

### NEVER in prayer/liturgy

- Petition God to **make you teleios**, **form you**, or **complete you** — you are already complete in Christ (Col 2:10) and teleios by design (Gen 1:26–28)
- **"Before any covenant title"** framed as anti-covenant or anti-role — teleios precedes vows **chronologically in design**, not **existentially outside** one-flesh life
- Vague **"renounce deferred identity"** or woke **self-authorship** language
- **Function vs flesh** binaries — function is not the enemy; **fragmentation** (role without womanhood foundation) is
- **"Make my home eudaemonia"** — God does not bypass your heirship; you **manifest** order, affection, responsibility, fidelity as co-heir (Rom 8:17; Gal 4:6–7)
- **Endless becoming**, **authentic self**, entitlement framing without Practice From Perfection

### ALWAYS in prayer/liturgy

- **Practice FROM perfection** (Rom 3:24; Matt 5:48) — align, steward, deploy what is already true
- **1 John 4:17** posture — *as He is, so are we in this world*
- Covenant **complements** teleios — two wholes deploy wholeness in one flesh; wife/mother **express** womanhood **in** covenant
- Confession of **forgetting** or **misalignment**, not of lacking being
- Petition for **discipline, revelation, remembrance, courage to embody** — not for God to invent your identity
- Scripture-anchored doxology through Christ
