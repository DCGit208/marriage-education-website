## Core principle

**Executive Network Director is NOT:**

- another commission layer  
- another override  
- another payout burden  
- another redundancy  

**Executive Network Director IS:**

> A designation for someone who has **successfully reproduced another complete RD deployment civilization**.

They earn by **building again** — not by sitting above others collecting extra percentages.

---

## What the Executive does **not** receive

| Mechanism | Executive payout |
|-----------|------------------|
| SBD subscription stack (500+250+250) | **0** — no executive slice |
| Ambassador onboarding upline pool (1,500 FCFA) | **0** — upline walk **stops at RD** (policy v2026-03-v3) |
| Product sale “executive override” (legacy 6.67%) | **0** — removed (Phase 4, March 2026) |

---

## How the Executive **does** earn

The Executive owns or develops **multiple full RD networks**. Each mature RD generates recurring economics through normal **RD override (5% of 5,000 FCFA)** on retained Foundation subscribers — the same rate as any RD, not a special Executive rate.

### Planning model (mature month ~10, illustrative)

**One RD network:**

- 500 ambassadors (50 seniors × 10 each)  
- Mature retained subscribers ≈ **1,000,000** (planning target with deployment + retention assumptions)  
- RD override: 1,000,000 × **250 FCFA** = **~250,000,000 FCFA/month**

**Executive with two replicated RD infrastructures:**

| Network | Subscribers (planning) | RD recurring override |
|---------|------------------------|------------------------|
| RD #1 | ~1,000,000 | ~250M FCFA/month |
| RD #2 | ~1,000,000 | ~250M FCFA/month |
| **Total** | ~2,000,000 | **~500M FCFA/month** |

This is **multi-regional infrastructure ownership**, not passive top-layer extraction.

## Hierarchy of infrastructure

| Role | Builds |
|------|--------|
| **Ambassador** | Subscriber infrastructure (direct referrals) |
| **Senior Ambassador** | Team infrastructure (~10 ambassadors) |
| **Regional Director** | One regional deployment civilization (~50 seniors · 500 ambassadors) |
| **Executive Network Director** | **Multiple** regional civilizations through replication |

---

## Promotion criteria (operational — Phase 8)

Executive designation requires demonstrating **reproduction of another full RD infrastructure**, including:

- Operational stability of the first RD network  
- Second RD network launched and sustained  
- Subscriber retention and deployment consistency  
- Leadership continuity (seniors active, not hollow headcount)

Exact scorecard to be operationalized in Phase 8 (`executive_networks` metadata — ownership linking, not payout rules).

---

## Why this structure works

- **Mathematically cleaner** — three payout roles on transactions; Executive = N × RD economics  
- **Easier to explain** — “I built two regions” vs “I collect an extra %”  
- **Merit-based** — must replicate, not inherit passive override  
- **Non-redundant** — no inflationary commission leakage  
- **Scalable globally** — Executive income scales with **N mature RDs**, not new percentages
